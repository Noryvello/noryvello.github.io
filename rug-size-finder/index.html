Rug Size Finder

A rug that is too small makes a room look unfinished, and one that is too big crowds it. Enter your room size and how your furniture sits, and this finder suggests a rug size with the working shown.

This is general guidance — personal taste matters, and odd-shaped rooms need measuring by hand. The sizes below are a starting point, not a rule.

How the size is worked out

The rug is always smaller than the room, so a strip of bare floor shows around it. How much smaller depends on the layout:

  • Front legs only: the rug is 30 cm shorter than the room in each direction — the front legs of the sofa and chairs sit on the rug.
  • All legs: the rug is 20 cm shorter than the room in each direction — all the furniture legs sit on the rug.
  • Floating: the rug is 60 cm shorter than the room in each direction — the rug sits in the middle with no furniture on it.

Each side of the rug is rounded to the nearest 10 cm.

Worked examples

  • Example 1: a 5 × 4 m room, front legs only — 5 m − 0.30 m = 4.70 m and 4 m − 0.30 m = 3.70 m, so the suggested rug is 4.70 m × 3.70 m.
  • Example 2: a 6 × 5 m room, all legs — 6 m − 0.20 m = 5.80 m and 5 m − 0.20 m = 4.80 m, so the suggested rug is 5.80 m × 4.80 m.
  • Example 3: a 4 × 3 m room, floating — 4 m − 0.60 m = 3.40 m and 3 m − 0.60 m = 2.40 m, so the suggested rug is 3.40 m × 2.40 m.

Before you buy

  • Measure your furniture, not just the room. The rug has to fit under the legs you want on it, so check the distance between the sofa and the chairs as well as the room size.
  • Tape the outline on the floor. Mark the suggested size with masking tape and live with it for a day. It is much easier to move tape than to return a rug.
  • Go slightly bigger between sizes. If the suggested size falls between two standard sizes, the bigger one usually looks better.
  • Leave room for doors. Check that doors can open over the rug, and that the rug does not block a walkway.

Questions people ask

How much bare floor should show around a rug?

It depends on the layout. This finder leaves the rug 20 to 60 cm smaller than the room in each direction, so a strip of bare floor shows around it. The floating layout leaves the most bare floor, and the all-legs layout leaves the least.

What does front legs only mean?

It means the front legs of the sofa and chairs sit on the rug, and the back legs sit on the bare floor. It is the most common living-room layout, and it makes the seating area feel connected without needing a rug as big as the room.

What if my room is not a rectangle?

Measure the longest length and the widest width of the area where the rug will sit, and use those numbers. For L-shaped rooms or rooms with alcoves, measure the main seating area only, not the whole room.

What if shops do not sell the exact size?

Pick the nearest standard size, and go slightly bigger rather than smaller when you are between sizes. A rug that is a little too small looks lost in the room, while one that is slightly bigger still looks right.
