How the generator works

Choose school or work, then hot or cold. Hot mains are soups, pasta, rice dishes and leftovers that travel well in a flask; cold mains are sandwiches, wraps and salads that need no heating. Tick nut-free and the generator leaves out anything with nuts or peanut butter, including peanut butter sandwiches and mixed nuts. Every press of New ideas gives a fresh set of 5 combos, each with a different main.

Packing tips

  • Keep cold food cold with an insulated bag and an ice pack.
  • Keep hot food hot in a flask, and fill the flask with boiling water for a few minutes before adding the food.
  • Pack fruit whole where you can — it travels better than cut fruit.
  • Put sauces and dressings in a small separate pot so sandwiches and salads do not go soggy.
  • Pack the lunchbox in the morning when you can, rather than the night before.

Questions people ask

What should I pack in a lunchbox?

A packed lunch usually has four parts: a main such as a sandwich, wrap or pasta; a side such as carrot sticks or a cheese stick; a piece of fruit; and a drink. This generator builds combos from those four parts, so each lunchbox has a bit of variety. Pack what you know the person likes, and keep portions realistic for their appetite.

How do I keep a packed lunch cold?

Use an insulated lunch bag with an ice pack, and pack the lunchbox in the morning rather than the night before if you can. Keep the bag out of direct sun and away from radiators. Food that needs to stay cold should not sit at room temperature for hours, so an ice pack is the simplest way to keep it cool until lunchtime.

What is a good nut-free lunchbox idea?

Tick the nut-free box on this page and the generator will leave out anything with nuts or peanut butter, such as a peanut butter sandwich or a bag of mixed nuts. Good nut-free mains include cheese and tomato sandwiches, hummus wraps and pasta salad. Always check your school's own rules about nuts and allergens, as some schools are nut-free.

How can I make packed lunches less boring?

Change one part of the lunchbox at a time: a different main, a new fruit, or a side you have not tried before. This generator gives you 5 fresh combos each time you press New ideas, so you can pick the ones you like. Planning the week's lunches in one go also helps, because you can shop for exactly what you need.
