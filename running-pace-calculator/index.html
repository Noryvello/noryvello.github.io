Questions people ask

How is running pace calculated?

Divide the total time by the distance. A 5 km run in 25 minutes is 25 ÷ 5 = 5 minutes per kilometre. Multiply by 1.60934 for the pace per mile — in this case 8 minutes 3 seconds per mile.

How do I work out my finish time from a pace?

Multiply the pace by the distance. At 5 minutes per kilometre, a marathon (42.195 km) takes 42.195 × 5 = 210.975 minutes, which is 3 hours 30 minutes 58 seconds.

How accurate is the race time predictor?

It uses the Riegel formula, a widely used endurance-sports estimate: predicted time = known time × (new distance ÷ known distance) raised to 1.06. It assumes similar fitness, course, and conditions. It is an estimate, not a promise — weather, hills, and training change real results.
