Pick an occasion and this generator returns funny award ideas with wording you can read out as it stands. Every award is kind and clean, so it suits family parties, classrooms, offices and friend groups.

How the funny awards generator works

Choose the occasion and press the button. The generator shows three award ideas from a bank of thirty original awards, each with wording you can read out as it stands. Press the button again for three more ideas for the same occasion. You can use the wording exactly as written, or change a detail to fit the person you have in mind.

Superlative awards work best when they are specific. An award that mentions a real habit, such as always being first to arrive or always knowing the coffee order, gets a bigger laugh than a general one. Read through the ideas, pick the ones that fit people in your group, and keep the rest for next time.

How to present the awards

Keep the presentation short and light. Read the award title, then the wording, then the name of the winner, and let everyone applaud before moving on. A simple printed certificate, a handwritten card or even a paper crown makes the moment feel special without much effort. If you have many awards, spread them through the evening rather than reading them all at once.

Give every winner a moment in the spotlight, and make sure the applause is warm for each one. If you are presenting to children, let them clap for each other and keep the pace brisk. If you are presenting to colleagues, keep the tone friendly and professional, and skip anything that could touch on work performance.

Keeping funny awards kind

  • Laugh with people, never at them. The winner should enjoy hearing the award.
  • Pick habits and talents, not looks, mistakes or things people cannot change.
  • If an award could embarrass someone, choose a different one.
  • Make sure everyone who wants an award gets one, so nobody feels left out.
  • When in doubt, ask yourself whether you would be happy to receive the award yourself.
