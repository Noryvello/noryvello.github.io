How Much BBQ Meat Do You Need for Every Guest?

Short answer: Enter the number of adults and children, choose a meat portion per person, and split the total across your selected BBQ meats. The result is a planning estimate, not a fixed serving rule.

How to use the estimate

The calculator adds the adult and child portions to estimate total raw meat, then divides that amount using the percentages you enter. The three shares must total 100%. Use the labels for whichever meats you plan to serve.

  • Choose portions based on whether BBQ is the main meal or one part of a larger spread.
  • Count children separately if you expect smaller servings.
  • Include only the meat guests will actually eat; sides, bread and other mains can reduce demand.
  • Round practical shopping quantities to the package sizes available to you.
Important limit: Appetites and side dishes change quantities; round up for big eaters.

Questions people ask

How much raw meat should I plan per adult?

There is no universal portion. Enter a serving estimate that fits your guests, menu and whether BBQ is the main meal.

Should children count as full portions?

Not necessarily. The calculator has a separate child portion so you can choose a smaller estimate.

Do the meat percentages need to total 100%?

Yes. Beef, chicken and other meat shares must add up to exactly 100% before the split is calculated.

Does the calculator include side dishes?

No. Side dishes are not calculated; use them when deciding what portion per person to enter.
