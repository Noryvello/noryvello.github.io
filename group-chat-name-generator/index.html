Group Chat Name Generator

Pick the kind of group you have and this generator gives you five chat name ideas to choose from. All forty names in the bank are original, friendly and safe to use with any group.

How to use this generator

Choose the kind of group you have, then press the button. You will get five names that suit that kind of group, drawn from a bank of forty. Press the button again for a different set, and let the group vote on the favourite.

The group types are only a guide. A name listed under friends might suit your work group perfectly, and a travel name might fit your family chat. Browse every type before you decide.

Picking a name everyone likes

The best group chat names make the whole group smile. A name that references a shared joke, a shared plan or a shared habit will feel personal in a way a generic name never does. Ask the group for ideas before you pick.

Keep it short enough to read at a glance. Long names get cut off on small screens, and a name with too many words is hard to remember. Two to four words is usually the sweet spot.

Keep it kind. Only use a name the whole group is happy with, and skip anything that teases one person or shares a private joke outsiders might misunderstand.

When to change the name

Groups change, and names can change with them. A chat that started for one trip can get a fresh name for the next one, and a work group can get a new name when a project ends. Renaming costs nothing and keeps the chat feeling current.

Mark special moments with a temporary name. A birthday week, a holiday countdown or a big match can all get a name of their own, then the group can go back to the usual name afterwards.

Questions people ask

How many names are in the bank?

Forty original names, five for each of eight group types: friends, family, work, classmates, sports teams, book clubs, travel and gaming.

Can I use a name from a different group type?

Yes. The types are only a starting point. A name listed for friends might suit your family group just as well.

What if nobody in the group likes the name?

Press the button again for a new set of five, or let each person pick a favourite and vote. The best group names are the ones everyone agrees on.

Are the names safe for a work chat?

Yes. Every name in the bank is clean and friendly, with nothing rude or unkind, so they work for work groups as well as friends.

Can I change the name later?

Yes. Most chat apps let you rename a group at any time, so you can refresh the name for a trip, a season or a new joke.
