The Day of Arafah and Hajj

The Day of Arafah falls the day before Eid al-Adha, on Saturday 15 May 2027. It is the central day of the Hajj pilgrimage, when pilgrims gather on the plain of Arafat. Eid al-Adha itself marks the end of Hajj.

What is Eid al-Adha?

Eid al-Adha, the Festival of Sacrifice, is one of the two main festivals in Islam. It remembers Ibrahim's willingness to sacrifice his son in obedience to God. Muslims mark the day with prayer, family gatherings, and sharing food with relatives and those in need.

How is Eid al-Adha different from Eid al-Fitr?

Eid al-Fitr marks the end of Ramadan, the month of fasting. Eid al-Adha comes at the end of the Hajj pilgrimage and is known as the Festival of Sacrifice, remembering Ibrahim's willingness to sacrifice his son. Both are days of prayer, family and giving.
