How to use a story prompt

  1. Pick a genre and read the prompt it gives you.
  2. Ask who the character is, what they want, and what stands in their way.
  3. Write the first scene without worrying about getting it perfect.
  4. If the prompt does not spark anything, press New prompt and try another.

What makes a good story prompt

A good prompt gives you three things: a character you can picture, a situation with a problem in it, and a twist that makes you ask what happens next. Every prompt on this page is built that way, and every one is original — none of them is taken from a published story.

Questions people ask

What is a story prompt?

A story prompt is a short starting idea for a story. It usually gives you a character, a situation and a twist, and leaves the rest for you to invent.

How do I use a story prompt?

Pick a genre, read the prompt, and start writing. You can follow the prompt exactly or change any part of it — the prompt is there to get you started, not to tell you what to write.

Can I use these prompts for school?

Yes. These prompts are free to use for school writing, homework, story clubs and classroom activities. The stories you write from them are your own work.

How do I turn a prompt into a story?

Ask three questions about the prompt: who is the character, what do they want, and what stands in their way. Then write the first scene, and let the answers to those questions guide what happens next.
