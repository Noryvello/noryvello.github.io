Own a Washing Machine or Use a Laundromat: Which Costs Less?

Short answer: enter machine cost, lifespan, repairs, running costs and laundromat charges to compare estimated cost per load and per year.

Compare the same number of loads

The calculator estimates the home machine's cost per load by spreading its purchase and installation cost over the expected lifetime loads, then adding repairs and running costs. Laundromat cost includes the per-load charge plus travel or other visit costs divided across loads per visit.

What may change the result?

  • Machine lifespan, repair frequency and energy efficiency.
  • Water and electricity rates, detergent use and load size.
  • Whether you need paid drying, special cycles or transport.
  • Whether a home machine can be installed where you live.
Important limit: Machine lifespan and repair costs change the answer.

Questions people ask

Is owning a washing machine cheaper than a laundromat?

It can be, but the answer depends on machine cost, lifespan, repairs, utility costs, loads per week and laundromat fees. Compare your own inputs.

What costs should I include for a home washer?

Include purchase and installation, repairs, maintenance, water, electricity, detergent and any relevant drying costs.

How does machine lifespan affect cost per load?

A longer useful life spreads the purchase cost over more loads, lowering the purchase-cost share per load. Unexpected repairs can offset that difference.

Does the calculator include drying?

Only if you include drying in your entered running cost or laundromat cost. Add it consistently to make the comparison fair.
