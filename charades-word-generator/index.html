Charades Word Generator

Pick a category and a difficulty, and the generator shows one word or phrase to act out without speaking. Press Skip for a different word, or Reveal hint for a one-line clue. There are 150 words to choose from, so every round feels fresh.

This page has no timer. If you want a time limit, use any phone timer and agree on the time before each round. Words are picked to be acted out without speaking, so no talking, spelling, or pointing at objects in the room.

How to play

  1. Split into two teams and take turns acting.
  2. The actor picks a category and difficulty, then acts out the word without speaking, spelling, or pointing at things in the room.
  3. The actor's team guesses out loud. A correct guess scores a point.
  4. If a word is too hard, press Skip for a new one — but agree beforehand whether a skip costs a point.
  5. Play to a set number of rounds, or set a target score, such as 10 points.

Acting tips

  • Start with the category: show the number of words with your fingers, then act each word in turn.
  • Use big, clear movements — small gestures are hard to read from across the room.
  • Act the idea, not the letters: for an elephant, show the trunk; for swimming, show the strokes.
  • If your team is stuck, act a word that rhymes or means something similar, then point back to the real word.
  • Keep it fair: no sounds, no mouthing words, and no pointing at objects in the room.

How the generator works

There are five categories: movies, animals, actions, food, and everyday objects. Each one has three difficulties: easy for kids and first-time players, medium for most groups, and hard for a real challenge.

Every word comes with a one-line hint, shown only when you press Reveal hint. The hint is a clue about what the word means, not a way to act it — the acting is still up to you.

The Skip button always shows a different word from the same category and difficulty. It never repeats the word on screen, so a skip always moves the game forward.

Questions people ask

How do you play charades?

Split into two teams. One player acts out a word without speaking while their team guesses. If the team guesses the word before time runs out, they score a point. Take turns acting, and the team with the most points at the end wins.

What if the word is too hard?

Press Skip to get a different word from the same category and difficulty. You can also switch to an easier difficulty, or press Reveal hint to see a one-line clue for the current word.

Is there a timer on this page?

No. This page has no built-in timer. If you want a time limit, use any phone timer or a kitchen timer and agree on the time before each round, for example one minute per word.

Are the words suitable for kids?

Yes. Every word is family-friendly, with no rude, scary, or grown-up content. For younger children, choose the easy difficulty and the animals or food categories.
