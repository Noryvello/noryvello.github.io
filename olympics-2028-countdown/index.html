LA Olympics 2028 Countdown

The LA Olympics 2028 open with the opening ceremony on Friday 14 July 2028, and the Games run until the closing ceremony on Sunday 30 July 2028. The Paralympic Games follow from Tuesday 15 August to Sunday 27 August 2028. This page counts down live to the opening ceremony, with the other key dates below.

The Olympic Games return to Los Angeles in 2028. The Games of the XXXIV Olympiad open on Friday 14 July 2028 and run for just over two weeks, closing on Sunday 30 July. The Paralympic Games follow in August.

When do the LA Olympics 2028 start?

The LA Olympics 2028 open with the opening ceremony on Friday 14 July 2028. The ceremony is shared by two stadiums: the LA Memorial Coliseum and SoFi Stadium. The Games then run until the closing ceremony on Sunday 30 July 2028, which takes place at the LA Memorial Coliseum.

The Paralympic Games follow a little over two weeks later, running from Tuesday 15 August to Sunday 27 August 2028.

About the Games

The LA Olympics 2028 are the Games of the XXXIV Olympiad, hosted by Los Angeles. The announced programme lists 36 sports and 353 events.

The opening ceremony is shared by the LA Memorial Coliseum and SoFi Stadium, and the closing ceremony takes place at the Coliseum.

The Paralympic Games, which follow the Olympics, run from 15 to 27 August 2028. They bring together para athletes from around the world for two weeks of competition.

How to follow the Games

Broadcast arrangements for the Games vary by country, and the official LA28 channels will carry the schedule as it is confirmed. Because this page is a dates and countdown page, it does not list broadcasters, fixtures, or results.

If you are planning to watch, the dates to put in the diary are the opening ceremony on Friday 14 July 2028 and the closing ceremony on Sunday 30 July 2028, with the Paralympics running from Tuesday 15 August to Sunday 27 August.
