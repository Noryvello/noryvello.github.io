Bar Soap or Body Wash: Which Costs Less Per Wash?

Short answer: enter the package size, price and amount you use per wash to calculate each product's estimated cost per wash.

Formula

Estimated washes per package equal package size divided by amount used per wash. Cost per wash equals package price divided by estimated washes. The result is a practical estimate: actual use changes with dispensing habits, product loss and how long a bar lasts.

Compare your own routine

  • Use the price and size printed on the package you buy.
  • Measure your typical use over several washes if you want a better estimate.
  • Compare products used for the same purpose and routine.
  • Do not assume a bar or liquid product is automatically more economical.
Important limit: Skin sensitivity beats cost; what irritates your skin is the wrong choice at any price.

Questions people ask

How do I calculate cost per wash?

Divide the product cost by the number of washes you estimate it provides. The calculator uses the package size and amount used per wash to estimate that count.

Can I compare different package sizes?

Yes. Enter each package's total amount and price, then enter how much you use per wash in the same unit, such as grams or milliliters.

Does body wash always cost more than bar soap?

No. Cost per wash depends on package price, product amount and how much you use each time. Your own inputs determine the comparison.

Should I use the same amount for both products?

Not necessarily. Enter the amount you actually use for a normal wash with each product. The amounts can differ.
