Band Name Generator

Pick a genre and this generator gives you five original band names to try. All forty names in the bank were invented for this page, so none of them are copied from real bands.

How to use this generator

Choose the genre that sounds closest to your band, then press the button. You will get five names drawn from that genre first, so the names should feel right for your sound. If you like a name, write it down before you press the button again, because the next press shows a different set.

The bank holds forty names, five per genre, and every name was invented for this page. Press the button as many times as you like and mix words from different names to build something of your own. The generator is a starting point, not a final answer.

Picking a name that sticks

Short names are easier to remember and easier to shout across a room. Two or three words usually work best, and a name that is easy to spell will help people find you online. Say each candidate out loud and imagine it announced before a show.

A good name also fits the music. A gentle name suits a quiet folk sound, while a harder name suits a loud rock band. If the name feels wrong for your songs, it probably is, so keep looking until one feels like it belongs to your band.

These names are invented suggestions, not a legal check. Before you use a name for a real band, search for it online and check that no other band, business or trademark already uses it.

Before you settle on a name

Once you have a shortlist, search each name online. Check music streaming sites, social media and video sites to see whether another band already uses it. A name that is already taken will cause confusion and could cause legal trouble later.

Check the name in writing too. Does it look good on a poster? Can people spell it after hearing it once? Ask a few friends what they think, and listen if several of them stumble over the same name.

Questions people ask

How many band names are in the bank?

Forty original names, five for each of eight genres: rock, pop, indie, metal, jazz, folk, electronic and country.

Can I mix names from different genres?

Yes. Pick a genre to start, then press the button again or try another genre and combine the words you like best into a new name of your own.

Are these real band names?

No. Every name in the bank was invented for this page. If a name happens to match a real band you know, pick another one.

What makes a good band name?

A good name is short, easy to say and easy to remember, and it fits the sound of the band. Say it out loud, write it down, and check how it looks on a poster.

Do I need to check a name before using it?

Yes. Search the name online and check music sites and trademark listings for your country before you print posters or release music under it.
