Questions people ask

Is Noryvello free to use?

Yes. Every tool and guide on Noryvello is free, with no sign-up and no download. Everything runs in your browser, on a phone or a computer.

Do the calculators give financial advice?

No. The calculators show standard arithmetic with the working steps, so you can see exactly how each answer is reached. They do not advise what to borrow, buy, or charge — real decisions also involve fees, taxes, and terms that vary.

Do the tools store my numbers?

No. Calculator inputs stay on your own device and are never sent anywhere. The wedding budget planner can save to your own browser's local storage if you choose, and only on that device.
