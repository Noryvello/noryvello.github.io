What is Chinese New Year?

Chinese New Year, also called the Spring Festival, marks the start of the lunar new year. It is the most important traditional festival in China, and families come together to celebrate it.

The Year of the Goat

The Chinese zodiac runs on a 12-year cycle, with each year named after an animal. 2027 is the Year of the Goat.
