How Do You Read Every Line on Your Payslip?

Short answer: Read a payslip from top to bottom: confirm the pay period and employee details, check gross earnings, review each deduction and contribution, then verify net pay and year-to-date totals. Compare the figures with your agreed pay and recorded hours.

Common payslip lines explained

Pay period

The dates covered by this payslip. Check that the period matches the work or salary cycle you expect.

Gross pay

Your earnings before deductions. It may include base salary or wages, overtime, bonuses, commissions, allowances or other pay items.

Hours, rate or units

For hourly or variable work, the payslip may show hours, units, pay rates or categories. Compare them with your approved timesheet or records.

Pre-tax deductions

Some payroll systems show deductions taken before particular taxes or calculations. The treatment depends on local rules and the type of deduction.

Tax and other withholdings

These may include income tax, social insurance or other statutory withholdings. The labels and calculation rules vary by jurisdiction.

Employer contributions

Some statements show amounts the employer contributes separately from money deducted from your pay. Check whether the line is informational or affects your take-home pay.

Net pay

The amount payable to you after the deductions included in the calculation. Compare it with the amount deposited or paid.

Year-to-date totals

Running totals for the current tax or reporting year. These help you track cumulative earnings and deductions.

What to check if something looks wrong

  1. Confirm the pay period and employee details.
  2. Compare hours, overtime and leave entries with your records.
  3. Check whether one-off payments or deductions are explained.
  4. Compare net pay with the payment received.
  5. Ask payroll or HR for an explanation and keep the response.
A note before you act: Payslip formats and deduction names differ by country and employer. Use your employer's payroll notes and local official guidance for exact definitions and tax treatment.

Frequently asked questions

What is the difference between gross pay and net pay?

Gross pay is earnings before the deductions shown in the payroll calculation. Net pay is the amount remaining after those deductions.

Why is my take-home pay lower than my gross pay?

A payslip may show tax withholding, social insurance, benefit contributions or other deductions. The exact items depend on your employer and local rules.

What does year-to-date mean on a payslip?

Year-to-date totals show accumulated amounts from the start of the relevant reporting or tax year through the current payslip.

Who should I contact if my payslip is incorrect?

Contact your employer's payroll or HR team, explain the line that concerns you and provide relevant records such as approved hours or prior statements.
