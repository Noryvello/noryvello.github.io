Questions people ask

How do I calculate stove running cost?

Multiply the energy used in a cooking session by the price per matching energy unit, then multiply by the number of sessions.

Can I compare gas and electricity directly?

Yes, if both usage values and rates are expressed on a comparable energy basis, such as cost per kilowatt-hour equivalent.

Does the calculator include appliance efficiency?

Only indirectly through the energy-use values you enter. It does not measure burner or cookware efficiency.

Why do local rates matter?

Gas and electricity tariffs differ by location, provider, tariff type, and billing structure, so your own rates are needed for a useful estimate.
