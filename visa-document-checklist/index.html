How to use the checklist

  1. Choose your trip type from the list — tourist visit, family visit, study, work, medical, or pilgrimage.

  3. Tick each document as you gather it. The counter shows how many you have checked off.
  4. Switch trip types any time. Your ticks are kept for each type, so nothing is lost when you switch back.

Use the checklist as a planning aid: gather the documents first, then go through the embassy's own official list and add anything this page does not mention.

What most applications ask for

Almost every visa application asks for the same core documents, whatever the trip type:

  • A valid passport. Check that it has blank pages and enough validity left for your trip.
  • A completed application form. Fill it in carefully and sign it where required.
  • Passport-size photographs. Taken recently, against a plain background.
  • Proof of funds. Something that shows you can pay for the trip, such as bank statements.
  • Travel plans. Flight bookings and proof of where you will stay.
  • Travel insurance. Cover for the trip, where the destination asks for it.

On top of these, each trip type has its own extras: an invitation letter for a family visit, an admission letter for study, a job offer for work, medical papers for treatment, and a booking for a pilgrimage.

Before you apply

  • Check the embassy's list early. The official list is the only one that counts — start there, and use this page to organise yourself.
  • Allow plenty of time. Some documents, such as certificates and medical checks, take time to arrange.
  • Keep copies. Photocopy or photograph every document you submit, and keep the copies separate from the originals.
  • Check names and dates. Names, dates and passport numbers should match across every document.

Questions people ask

Do I need a visa for every country?

No. It depends on your passport and where you are going. Some countries let you enter without a visa, some give one on arrival, and some need an application in advance. Check the embassy or official government site of the country you plan to visit.

What documents do most visa applications ask for?

Most ask for a valid passport, a completed application form, passport-size photographs, proof of funds, and details of your travel plans. Depending on the trip, they may also ask for an invitation letter, an admission letter, a job offer, or medical papers.

Why do requirements differ by country?

Each country sets its own entry rules, and embassies apply them for the country they represent. The documents asked for can also depend on the purpose of your trip and how long you plan to stay.

Is this checklist official?

No. This checklist is a general starting point based on the document types most applications ask for. The only official list is the one published by the embassy or consulate you are applying to, so always check it before you apply.
