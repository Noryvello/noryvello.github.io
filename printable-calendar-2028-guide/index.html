The 2028 year at a glance

2028 is a leap year, which means February has 29 days instead of 28, and the year has 366 days in total. The extra day, Tuesday 29 February 2028, is the only date in the year that appears once every four years. The year opens on Saturday 1 January and closes on Sunday 31 December, so the first and last days of the year both fall on weekends.

Five months of 2028 begin on a Saturday or a Sunday: January, April, July, October and December. That matters if you plan by weeks, because a month that starts on a weekend gives you a short first week. May is the only month that starts on a Monday, so it is the tidiest month to plan in whole weeks.

When you print, check the preview first. Most browsers add the page address and the date in the header and footer of a printout; turning those off in the print settings gives you a cleaner calendar. Landscape orientation suits a wall calendar, while portrait suits a page you keep in a folder.

How to print your calendar

Start by deciding how you will use the calendar, because that decides the print settings. A wall calendar wants large boxes you can write in, so print one month per page in landscape on the largest paper your printer takes. A desk calendar can be smaller: two months per page in portrait still leaves room for short notes.

Use the print button at the top of this page, or your browser's print command, and choose which pages to print so you only get the months you need. If your printer offers a draft or ink-saving mode, try it on one month first: calendar tables are mostly empty space, so they print well even on a light setting, but the grid lines should stay clear enough to read.

Plain paper works fine, but slightly heavier paper, the kind sold for printing documents you keep, holds up better on a wall and takes pen without showing through. If you plan to write on the calendar with a marker, test one square first: some markers bleed through thin paper and mark the page underneath.

Planning the year with a paper calendar

A printed calendar earns its place when you actually write on it. Mark the dates that matter as soon as you know them: birthdays, trips, deadlines, appointments and school dates. A simple colour code helps — one colour for family, one for work, one for money dates — but keep it to three colours at most, or the code itself becomes a chore.

At the start of each month, take five minutes with the new page. Copy over anything that carries forward, look ahead to the busy weeks, and note the one or two things that matter most that month. A monthly glance like this catches clashes early, when there is still time to move things around.

Keep last year's pages when the year is done. A filled-in calendar is a simple record of the year: where you went, what you finished, and when the good weeks happened. It takes up no space in a folder, and it is surprising how often you will want to check when something happened.

This guide lists no holidays, because holiday dates vary by country and region. Check your local official calendar before marking holidays, and double-check any date that matters against a second source.

Yes, 2028 is a leap year with 366 days, and February has 29 days. The extra day is Tuesday 29 February 2028.

What day of the week is 1 January 2028?

1 January 2028 is a Saturday. The year ends on Sunday 31 December 2028, so both the first and last days of the year fall on weekends.
