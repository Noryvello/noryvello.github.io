7 Flight Booking Mistakes That Can Cost You More

Short answer: compare the full journey cost, read the exact fare rules, check baggage and connection details, and verify every passenger and itinerary detail before paying.

7 flight booking mistakes that can cost you

  1. Comparing only the headline fare: check baggage, seat selection, payment and airport transfer costs before comparing total trip cost.
  2. Ignoring airport locations: a cheaper airport may add a long or expensive transfer. Check the exact airport and terminal.
  3. Overlooking baggage rules: compare cabin and checked baggage dimensions, weight limits and fees for your fare type.
  4. Choosing an unrealistic connection: allow enough time for immigration, security, terminal changes and separate-ticket transfers.
  5. Skipping name and date checks: match passenger names to travel documents and confirm dates, airports and times before payment.
  6. Assuming every ticket is refundable: read change, cancellation, no-show and refund terms for the exact fare.
  7. Not checking the final itinerary: verify the booking confirmation, operating airline, baggage transfer and any transit visa requirements.

Flight booking checklist

Compare the whole journey

Before booking, compare the complete door-to-door journey rather than the fare alone. Consider travel to the airport, arrival time, overnight waits, baggage handling and the consequences of a delayed first flight.

Important limit: Fares and rules change constantly; always check the airline's own terms.

Questions people ask

When is the best time to book a flight?

There is no single booking window that guarantees the lowest fare. Prices depend on route, dates, demand, airline inventory and other changing factors. Compare options and set a budget.

Are cheap flight tickets refundable?

Not necessarily. Refund and change rights depend on the airline, route, fare conditions and applicable law. Read the exact fare rules before paying.

How much connection time should I allow?

It depends on the airport, terminals, immigration, baggage transfer and whether flights are on one ticket. Check the airline’s minimum connection guidance and allow a sensible buffer.

Should I book directly with the airline?

Booking directly can make it simpler to manage a booking with the airline, but compare the full terms and support arrangements for any booking channel you consider.
