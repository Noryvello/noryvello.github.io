Free AI Image Generators: What Each Free Tier Really Gives

Short answer: The strongest free image generation in November 2026 runs inside Google's own apps, where the Nano Banana models are free under published caps in Flow and compute-based limits in the Gemini app. Leonardo gives 150 tokens a day, Ideogram gives 30 credits a week, and Pollinations needs no sign-up at all. ChatGPT and Adobe Firefly both offer free images but publish no fixed count — this page says so plainly instead of guessing.

Read this first: free image quotas are among the most volatile figures in this category — several tools on this page cut or reshaped their free tier during 2026. Every figure below was checked against the provider's own pages, or dated checks of those pages, on 14 November 2026. 1. Google Gemini app and Google Flow — the strongest free image tier

Google's image models, known as Nano Banana, are free in two of Google's own apps — but the limits work differently in each, and Google's own pages are the only reliable source:

  • Gemini app: free accounts create images with Nano Banana 2 at up to 1K resolution. Google publishes no fixed daily image count: limits are compute-based, depend on prompt complexity, features used and chat length, refresh every 5 hours until a weekly limit, and can be checked in the app under Settings, then Usage Limits. Older articles quoting "20 images a day" describe a figure Google no longer publishes.
  • Google Flow: Flow publishes fixed caps for accounts without a Google AI plan: up to 20 Nano Banana Pro images a day, and up to 400 images a day combined from Nano Banana 2.1 and Nano Banana 2 Lite. Images in Flow no longer spend Flow credits.
  • Google embeds an invisible SynthID watermark in Gemini images. There is no visible logo on free Gemini images.
  • The Gemini API lists no free tier for the Nano Banana models — the free routes are the apps, not the API.

2. ChatGPT — free images, no published number

ChatGPT includes image creation on its free plan, running the same ChatGPT Images model as paid plans. OpenAI's pricing page describes free image creation only as "limited" and publishes no count for any plan. Independent tests disagree wildly — one free account made 3 images before a 24-hour wait, another made 22 in a day without a limit message — so no article can honestly promise you a number. What is published: free accounts get 3 file uploads a day, and image generation has its own limit separate from text chat. When you reach it, ChatGPT shows when the limit resets.

3. Leonardo — 150 fast tokens a day

Leonardo's free plan refills 150 fast tokens every day, with no rollover: unused tokens expire at the end of the day. The free plan generates with the Auto model only — the 80+ other models, including third-party models, are paid — and everything you create is public, since private mode is a paid feature. Token cost varies by model and size and is shown before you generate; in one November 2026 test, a 1344 × 768 image cost 8 tokens. Leonardo's terms tie full ownership of outputs to an active paid subscription, so read them before commercial use.

4. Ideogram — 30 credits a week, best for text in images

Ideogram is the leader for readable text inside images — posters, logos, signage. Its free plan gives 30 credits a week (read from Ideogram's pricing page, November 2026), enough for about 5 images at medium quality or 10 at low quality. The allowance is weekly, not daily, and Ideogram says the weekly amount may vary. Free images are public by default and downloads are JPEG. Note that free credits require signing in with Google, Apple or Microsoft — email-and-password accounts get no free credits. Older articles quoting 10 prompts a day describe a previous version of the free plan.

5. Adobe Firefly — free daily generations, number not published

Adobe's Firefly plans page says you do not need a plan to get free daily generations across image, video and audio, with limits that refresh every day — but Adobe does not publish the daily number, and its help centre adds that the allowance can change and that free users get a curated set of models. Treat Firefly Free as a test run: enough to try the tools, not enough to plan a project around. Adobe describes Firefly output as usable commercially, since its models are trained on licensed content.

6. Pollinations — no sign-up, no published limit

Pollinations is the only mainstream option that needs no account, no API key and no card: open the site, type a prompt, get an image. It publishes no daily limit, though very heavy use from one address may be throttled, and one September 2026 test of the anonymous tier saw about one image every 40 seconds. The trade-off is quality: its hosted models trail the frontier tools visibly on faces, hands and text-in-image. No formal commercial licence is clearly stated, so confirm the terms before brand work.

Also worth knowing

Meta AI's Muse Image, inside WhatsApp, Instagram and the Meta AI app, is reported at roughly 25 free images a day, with a visible "Imagined with Meta AI" mark on every free output. That figure is reported, not officially published, so treat it as unconfirmed. Midjourney has no free tier — it removed its free trial in late 2024, so any "free Midjourney" claim is out of date.

How to choose

  • Best quality free: Google Flow for the published Pro allowance, or the Gemini app for everyday images.
  • Text in images: Ideogram, even on 30 credits a week.
  • Most images per day without paying: Leonardo's 150 daily tokens, or Pollinations if you want no account at all.
  • Commercial work: read the terms first — free tiers often make images public or restrict ownership.

Questions people ask

Which free AI image generator needs no sign-up?

Pollinations is the one mainstream option that needs no account, no API key and no card: you open the site, type a prompt and get an image. It publishes no daily limit, though very heavy use from one address may be throttled, and its quality sits visibly below the frontier tools. Check its terms before commercial use, because no formal commercial licence is clearly stated.

How many free images does Gemini give per day?

Google publishes no fixed daily image count for the Gemini app: limits are compute-based, refresh every 5 hours up to a weekly cap, and depend on prompt complexity and chat length. Google Flow is different and does publish caps: without a plan, up to 20 Nano Banana Pro images a day, and up to 400 a day combined from Nano Banana 2.1 and 2 Lite. Older articles quoting 20 images a day for the Gemini app describe a limit Google no longer publishes.

Can I use free AI images commercially?

It depends on the tool, and free tiers often add a catch: Leonardo's free images are public and its terms tie full ownership of outputs to a paid subscription, Ideogram's free images are public too, and Pollinations states no formal commercial licence clearly. Adobe describes Firefly output as usable commercially. Always read the terms of the tool you used before putting a free image into paid work.

Why do free AI image limits change so often?

Providers adjust free quotas as demand, costs and models change — Google, Ideogram and others have all cut or reshaped free allowances during 2026, sometimes without announcement. That is why this page dates every figure and says plainly where a provider publishes no number. Check the tool's own pricing or help page before planning work around a free tier.
