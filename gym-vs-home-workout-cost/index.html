Gym or Home Workouts: Which Costs Less Over a Year?

Short answer: enter gym membership and travel costs alongside home equipment and extras to compare estimated yearly totals.

What the yearly totals include

The gym estimate includes 12 months of membership, annual fees and 12 months of travel cost. The home estimate spreads the equipment purchase cost over the period you enter, then adds annual extras such as replacement items or paid workout resources. Enter zero for costs that do not apply.

Questions people ask

Is a home workout always cheaper than a gym?

Not always. Equipment, space, replacement items and paid resources can add up. Compare your own home costs with membership, fees and travel.

What gym costs should I include?

Include membership, joining or annual fees, travel, parking, classes or other recurring charges you actually pay.

How should I account for home workout equipment?

Enter the purchase cost and the number of years you expect to use it, then include recurring extras such as replacement items or paid services.

Does the calculator say which option is better for fitness?

No. It compares estimated costs only and makes no health or fitness claims. Choose an option that fits your preferences, schedule and needs.
