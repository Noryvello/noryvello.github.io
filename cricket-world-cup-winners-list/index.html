Cricket World Cup Winners List

Thirteen men's Cricket World Cup finals have been played between 1975 and 2023, and six teams have lifted the trophy. Australia lead the way with six titles, ahead of India and West Indies with two each. This page lists every final, every champion and every runner-up.

The Cricket World Cup is the sport's biggest one-day tournament, played every four years. The first final was played in 1975, and twelve more finals have followed it up to 2023. In that time the trophy has changed hands between six different teams, and the table below sets out the whole story: the year of every final, the team that won it, the team that finished runner-up, and the result detail recorded for each match.

This page records the winner, runner-up and result detail of every Cricket World Cup final from 1975 to 2023. Only the 2019 and 2023 finals carry extra detail here — the tied match and Super Over in 2019, and the six-wicket margin and venue in 2023. No other scores or margins are listed, because this guide's scope is the list of champions.

Titles by team

Six teams have won the Cricket World Cup in thirteen editions. Australia stand alone at the top with six titles — nearly half of all the finals ever played. India and West Indies have two titles each, and Pakistan, Sri Lanka and England have one each.

  • Australia — 6 titles (1987, 1999, 2003, 2007, 2015, 2023)
  • India — 2 titles (1983, 2011)
  • West Indies — 2 titles (1975, 1979)
  • Pakistan — 1 title (1992)
  • Sri Lanka — 1 title (1996)
  • England — 1 title (2019)

The list also shows how hard the trophy is to win: only six teams have managed it in thirteen editions, and three of those six have won it just once. Every other finalist on the list reached the final and fell at the last step.

Notes on each champion

Australia. Australia have won six of the thirteen finals, more than any other team. Their first title came in 1987, when they beat England. They then won three finals in a row — 1999 against Pakistan, 2003 against India, and 2007 against Sri Lanka — the only team on this list to win three straight titles. They beat New Zealand in 2015, and their most recent title came in 2023, when they beat India by six wickets at Narendra Modi Stadium, Ahmedabad, on 19 November 2023. Australia have also lost two finals: the very first one, in 1975 against West Indies, and the 1996 final against Sri Lanka. That makes eight final appearances in thirteen editions, with six wins from those eight.

West Indies. West Indies won the first two editions of the tournament, beating Australia in 1975 and England in 1979. They reached the final again in 1983, but lost to India, and they have not won the title since 1979. Their two titles both came in the tournament's opening years, which makes them the only team whose championships all date from the 1970s.

India. India's first title came in 1983, when they beat West Indies in the final. Their second came in 2011, when they beat Sri Lanka. India have also finished runner-up twice, in 2003 and again in 2023 — both times to Australia. That gives India four final appearances in total: two wins and two defeats, with both defeats coming against the same team.

Pakistan. Pakistan's single title came in 1992, when they beat England in the final. They reached the final once more, in 1999, but lost to Australia. One title from two final appearances is Pakistan's record on this list.

Sri Lanka. Sri Lanka won their only title in 1996, beating Australia in the final. They have been runners-up twice since then: in 2007, when they lost to Australia, and in 2011, when they lost to India. That is three final appearances in total, with one win and two defeats.

England. England's road to the trophy was the longest on this list. They lost three finals before winning one: in 1979 against West Indies, in 1987 against Australia, and in 1992 against Pakistan. Their breakthrough came in 2019, in the most unusual final the tournament has produced — the match was tied, the Super Over was tied, and England won on the boundary-count rule against New Zealand. One title from four final appearances is England's record.

The team that has never won. New Zealand are the only team on this list to have reached the final without ever winning it. They were runners-up in 2015, when they lost to Australia, and again in 2019, when they lost to England in the tied final decided by the boundary-count rule. Every other team that has reached a final has won at least one.

Looking ahead to 2027

The next men's Cricket World Cup takes place from 2 October to 21 November 2027, hosted by South Africa, Zimbabwe and Namibia, with 14 teams taking part. Australia go into the tournament as defending champions, having beaten India in the 2023 final, and India go in as the most recent runners-up. Questions people ask

Who has won the most Cricket World Cups?

Australia, with six titles: 1987, 1999, 2003, 2007, 2015 and 2023. No other team has won more than two.

Who won the 2023 Cricket World Cup?

Australia beat India by six wickets in the final at Narendra Modi Stadium, Ahmedabad, on 19 November 2023. It was Australia's sixth title.

Has New Zealand ever won the Cricket World Cup?

No. New Zealand reached the final twice, in 2015 and 2019, and finished runner-up both times, to Australia in 2015 and to England in 2019.

How many teams have won the Cricket World Cup?

Six teams have won the trophy in thirteen editions: Australia (six titles), India (two), West Indies (two), Pakistan (one), Sri Lanka (one) and England (one).
