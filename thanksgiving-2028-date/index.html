How the date is set

Thanksgiving in the United States is always the fourth Thursday of November. The rule is fixed, so the date can be worked out for any year without looking it up: find the Thursdays of November, count to the fourth, and that is Thanksgiving. In 2028 the Thursdays of November fall on the 2nd, 9th, 16th and 23rd, so the fourth Thursday is 23 November.

Because the rule is tied to a weekday rather than a calendar date, Thanksgiving always falls between 22 and 28 November, and it always falls on a Thursday. The date moves a little from year to year, but the weekday never does. That is why the holiday feels familiar even when the date is different: it is always a Thursday, and always near the end of November.

What happens on Thanksgiving

Thanksgiving is a day for family and for giving thanks. The centre of the day is a shared meal, and the traditional centrepiece of that meal is a roast turkey, served with side dishes that vary from family to family. Many people travel to be with family, and the days around the holiday are among the busiest travel days of the year. The turkey calculator in the links below helps with roasting times for the bird itself.

The holiday also opens the festive season. The day after Thanksgiving is Black Friday, which falls on Friday 24 November 2028, and the weekend that follows is the traditional start of Christmas shopping and planning. Thanksgiving itself stays a quieter day: a meal, family, and thanks.
