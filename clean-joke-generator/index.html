What makes a joke clean

A clean joke is funny without being unkind. Every joke on this page follows three simple rules:

  • No rude words. The humour comes from the situation, not from language anyone would need to apologise for.
  • No teasing. Nobody is the butt of the joke — no group of people, no job, and no way of life is made fun of.
  • Easy to understand anywhere. The jokes use everyday words and situations, with no local slang and no references that only make sense in one place.

That is why these jokes work at home, in a classroom, at a party, or anywhere people of different ages are together. A good clean joke is one you can tell your grandmother and your youngest cousin in the same breath.
