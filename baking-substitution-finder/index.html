Questions people ask

Are these baking substitutions guaranteed?

No. They are starting points; results depend on the recipe, ingredient brands, proportions, and baking method.

Can I replace butter with oil in every recipe?

No. Oil can work in many moist cakes, but it changes structure and spread in recipes that rely on solid butter.

Can baking powder replace yeast?

No. Yeast and baking powder work differently and are not interchangeable one-to-one.

Why can a substitute change the texture?

Ingredients contribute different amounts of fat, water, protein, acidity, and leavening, so a swap can change moisture, structure, flavour, and rise.
