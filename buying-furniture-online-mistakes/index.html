7 Mistakes to Avoid When Buying Furniture Online

Short answer: The most costly online furniture mistakes often start with measurements: buyers check the product dimensions but forget doorways, stairs, room clearance and delivery access. Use this checklist before placing an order.

The seven mistakes

  1. Measuring only the room. A sofa can fit the floor plan but fail to pass through a doorway.
  2. Ignoring depth and height. Check all three dimensions, not just width.
  3. Forgetting clearance. Drawers, recliners and cabinet doors need room to open.
  4. Trusting photos for scale. Use the listed dimensions and compare them with a measured object.
  5. Skipping material details. Read the construction, upholstery and care information.
  6. Overlooking assembly. Check whether the item arrives assembled and whether the parts fit through access routes.
  7. Not reading delivery and return terms. Confirm access requirements and the seller’s stated return process before ordering.

Measure twice

Mark the furniture footprint on the floor with tape. Also measure the full delivery path, including tight turns, lifts and stair landings.

Important limit: Always measure your space and doorways before ordering.

Questions people ask

Which furniture measurements should I check?

Check product width, depth and height, then compare them with the available room space and delivery route.

Why do doorways matter if the furniture fits the room?

The item still has to pass through doors, halls, stairs, lifts and turns before it reaches the room.

Should I trust furniture photos for size?

No. Use the listed dimensions and compare them with measurements taken in your own space.

What should I check before ordering online?

Review dimensions, materials, assembly needs, delivery access and the seller’s stated return conditions.
