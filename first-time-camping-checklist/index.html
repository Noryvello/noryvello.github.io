Safety basics

  • Tell someone your plan — where you are going and when you expect to return.
  • Check the weather and pack layers plus a rain jacket, even if the forecast looks clear.
  • Plan your water — carry at least 2 litres and a filter or purification tablets for refills.
  • Know the fire rules for your site before lighting a stove or campfire.
  • Store food properly away from sleeping areas where wildlife is active.

Leave No Trace

Pack out everything you pack in, including food scraps. Trash bags are on the checklist for a reason — leave the site cleaner than you found it.

Questions people ask

How many items should I bring on a first camping trip?

A basic list of 15 to 20 essential items usually covers most needs. This checklist has 16 — start there and add trip-specific gear.

Do I need a separate sleeping pad?

Yes. A sleeping pad adds comfort and insulation from the cold ground, which matters as much as the sleeping bag itself.

Can I use a regular backpack for camping?

A backpack sized 30 to 50 litres is ideal for carrying gear and food on an overnight trip. Day packs are usually too small for tent and sleep gear.

What is the best way to stay hydrated?

Carry at least 2 litres of water and bring a filter or purification tablets for refills. Do not rely on finding drinkable water at the site.
