Questions people ask

What is sleep hygiene?

Sleep hygiene is the set of everyday habits that surround sleep, such as keeping regular hours, winding down before bed and keeping the bedroom dark and quiet. It is a general description of habits, not a treatment for any sleep problem.

How many hours of sleep do adults need?

Most adults need around 7–9 hours of sleep a night, but needs vary from person to person and can change with age and lifestyle. The right amount is the amount that leaves you feeling rested during the day.

What should I avoid before bed?

It is best to avoid bright screens, caffeine, heavy meals and stimulating activity in the hour or two before bed, since these can make it harder to fall asleep. A calm wind-down routine in dim light is a common alternative.

When should I talk to a doctor about sleep?

If sleep problems last for weeks, affect your daytime life, or come with snoring, pauses in breathing, or feeling very sleepy during the day, discuss them with a doctor. This page is general information only and does not diagnose or treat any sleep problem.
