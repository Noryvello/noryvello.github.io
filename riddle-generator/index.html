Tips for solving riddles

Most riddles play on words that can mean two things, so read each clue slowly and ask what else it could describe. Try the simplest answer first, and say the riddle out loud — hearing it sometimes helps. If you are stuck, walk away and come back: the answer often feels obvious once you stop staring at the question.

Questions people ask

What is a riddle?

A riddle is a question or a short puzzle that describes something in a roundabout way, so you have to think sideways to work out the answer. Most riddles play with words, double meanings, or the way you expect an answer to sound.

How do I solve a riddle?

Read the riddle slowly and look for words that could mean more than one thing. Ask yourself what the clues have in common, try the simplest answer first, and give yourself time — stepping away and coming back often helps.

Are these riddles suitable for children?

Yes. Every riddle on this page is clean and family-friendly, and the easy set is written for younger solvers. Some of the tricky ones will still give adults something to think about.

What makes a riddle tricky?

A tricky riddle usually hides its answer behind a word with two meanings, or leads you to picture the wrong thing. The answer feels obvious once you hear it, which is what makes the puzzle satisfying.
