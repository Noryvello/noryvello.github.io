Rules for travelling with animals vary by transport company, route and destination, and they change. Check the rules for your transport and your destination before you book, and confirm them again before you travel. This checklist is general guidance only, not legal or veterinary advice.

Before you book

Check the rules before you book. Transport companies and destinations have their own rules for animals, and they change. Confirm what is allowed, what documents you need, and whether your pet travels with you or separately.

Getting your pet used to the carrier

Leave the carrier out at home for a few days before the journey, with a familiar blanket inside. Let your pet explore it in their own time. A carrier that smells like home is far less frightening than a new one on the day.

On the journey

Keep the journey calm and steady. Plan breaks for water and fresh air, keep the carrier out of direct sun, and talk to your pet in a quiet voice. Never leave your pet alone in a parked vehicle.

Arriving with your pet

Set up food, water and a sleeping spot as soon as you arrive. Let your pet explore the new place slowly, one room at a time, and keep the first evening calm.

Questions people ask

How do I choose the right carrier?

The carrier should be big enough for your pet to stand, turn around and lie down comfortably, but not so big that they slide around. A familiar blanket inside helps them settle.

Should I feed my pet before travelling?

Feed a light meal a few hours before you leave, rather than just before the journey. Pack water and a bowl, and offer small drinks along the way.

What documents do I need for my pet?

It depends on where you are going and how you travel. Check the rules for your transport and destination, and carry your vet's contact details and your pet's records.

How often should I stop on a long journey?

Plan regular breaks for water, fresh air and a stretch. Short, calm stops are better than long ones, and never leave your pet alone in a parked vehicle.

What should I do when we arrive?

Set up food, water and a sleeping spot first, then let your pet explore slowly. Keep the first evening calm so your pet can settle into the new place.
