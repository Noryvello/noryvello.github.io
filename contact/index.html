Contact Noryvello

The way to reach Noryvello is through our Telegram channel — it is the only contact channel we run. Send a message about a broken tool, a wrong result, a suggestion, or a correction, and include the page address and what happened.

Noryvello is a small, independent site, and every message is read by the person who builds and checks the pages. There is no contact form on this site — it is a static site, so a form would have nowhere to send anything — and there is no phone number and no other channel. The one place to write to is our Telegram channel, which works from any phone or computer with Telegram installed. You can reach us here: Message us on Telegram.

Messages are read by one person, so replies take time — please send your message once and wait for a reply rather than sending it again. Telegram is the only contact channel for Noryvello. There is no email address, no phone number, and no contact form on this site. If someone offers you a different way to reach us, it is not from Noryvello.

What to write about

Most messages fall into four kinds, and each one is welcome. A clear message is the fastest way to a fix or an answer.

  • A broken tool — a button that does nothing, a page that will not load, or a tool that stops halfway.
  • A wrong result — a calculator answer that does not match your own working, or a guide step that does not make sense.
  • A suggestion — a tool or guide you would like to see added to the site.
  • A content correction — a typo, a wrong date, or a fact on a page that needs fixing.

What to include in your message

A message with the right details can usually be answered without a round of follow-up questions. Include these four things when you can.

  • The address of the page — copy it from your browser's address bar so the right page is checked.
  • What happened — what you did, what you expected, and what you saw instead.
  • For a calculator, the numbers you entered and the answer it gave.
  • For a suggestion, what the tool should do and who it would help.

How long until you hear back

The expected reply time is as soon as possible — messages are answered in the order they arrive. Every message is read, but replies are written by one person alongside building and checking new pages, so a short wait is normal.

If your message reports a bug, the fix often goes up before a reply is sent, so it is worth checking the page again after a few days. Sending the same message more than once does not make a reply arrive sooner — one clear message is enough.

What happens after you write

Once your message arrives, it is read and sorted by what it needs. A bug report goes to the page itself: the tool is tested again, the fault is found, and the page is fixed. A suggestion is noted for the list of future tools, and a correction is checked against the page before it is changed. You will hear back on Telegram — the same channel you wrote in on — so there is nothing else to watch.

Check first, then write

Before you write, a quick check can save you the wait. Reload the page once — a tool that looks broken sometimes recovers with a fresh load. Check that you are on the real site by looking at the address bar: Noryvello pages live at noryvello.github.io. And if a calculator gives a strange answer, try the worked example on the page first — it shows the exact steps the tool follows, and it often shows where a number was mistyped.

Questions people ask

How do I report a broken tool?

Send a message through our Telegram channel and include the address of the page, what you were doing, and what went wrong. If it is a calculator, include the numbers you entered and the answer it gave, so the fault can be found quickly.

How long until I hear back?

The expected reply time is as soon as possible — messages are answered in the order they arrive. Every message is read by one person, so please send your message once and wait — sending it again does not make a reply arrive sooner.

Can I suggest a new tool?

Yes. Send your idea through our Telegram channel and say what the tool should do and who it would help. Every suggestion is read, though not every idea can be built.

Do you accept guest posts?

No. Noryvello does not accept guest posts, and all pages are written by the site owner.
