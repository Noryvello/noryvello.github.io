How Can You Shift Your Sleep After Crossing Time Zones?

Short answer: Choose eastward or westward travel and enter the number of time zones crossed. The planner creates a general gradual schedule for shifting sleep and seeking light; it is an educational planning aid, not medical advice.

How to read the plan

The tool divides the entered time-zone difference into a simple daily shift target and displays suggested bedtime changes relative to your usual bedtime. Light timing is described broadly because the right timing depends on the destination, schedule and individual circumstances.

  • Eastward travel generally means moving the body clock earlier; westward travel generally means moving it later.
  • Use the schedule as a discussion and planning prompt, not a precise biological prediction.
  • Consider destination daylight and planned activities when deciding when to spend time outdoors.
  • Allow flexibility rather than forcing a schedule that leaves you excessively tired.
Important limit: This is general information, not medical advice; sleep disorders need a doctor.

Questions people ask

Does eastward travel usually require an earlier sleep schedule?

Generally, yes. Eastward travel shifts the destination clock earlier relative to the origin, so the planner illustrates gradual earlier bedtimes.

Does westward travel usually require a later sleep schedule?

Generally, yes. Westward travel shifts the destination clock later relative to the origin, so the planner illustrates gradual later bedtimes.

Can this planner predict exactly when I will adjust?

No. Adjustment varies between people and depends on factors the simple planner does not measure.

Is this a treatment for a sleep disorder?

No. It provides general travel information only and is not medical advice or a treatment plan.
