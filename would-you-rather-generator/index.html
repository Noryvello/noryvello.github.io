How to play

Would you rather is one of the simplest games there is, and it works with two people or twenty. Here is how to play it with this generator:

  1. Pick a category. Start with Funny for a light mood, or Tricky when you want a real debate.
  2. Read the question out loud. Everyone picks one of the two options — no skipping, no "both", no "neither".
  3. Tap your choice. A fun one-line reaction appears for the option you picked.
  4. Explain your pick. The best part of the game is the reason. Ask the group why they chose what they chose.
  5. Press Next question for a new pair from the same category, or switch categories any time.

For a party, take turns reading the question and let the reader go last, so everyone else has to commit first. For a quiet evening, just play a few rounds with one other person and see where the conversation goes.

Questions people ask

What is "Would you rather"?

It is a simple party game. Each question gives you two fun choices, and you pick the one you would rather do. There is no right or wrong answer — the fun is in explaining your pick and hearing what others choose.

Are the questions original?

Yes. Every question pair on this page was written for Noryvello. They are clean, family-friendly and written to be understood anywhere in the world.

How do the choice buttons work?

Each question shows two buttons, one for each option. Tap the one you prefer and a fun one-line reaction appears. Press Next question to get a new pair and clear the reaction.

Is it suitable for kids and parties?

Yes. All questions are clean and family-friendly, with no rude, scary or grown-up content. It works well for family nights, classrooms, parties and long journeys.
