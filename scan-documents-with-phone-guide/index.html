How Can You Scan Documents Clearly With a Phone?

Short answer: use even light, place the phone parallel to the page, capture every edge, correct the crop and inspect the saved file at zoom before sharing it.

Step 1: Prepare the page

  1. Place the document on a flat, contrasting surface.
  2. Flatten curled corners and move objects that cast shadows.
  3. Use bright, even light. Avoid glare from direct flash or glossy paper.

Step 2: Position the camera

  1. Open your phone’s camera or a document-scanning app.
  2. Hold the phone parallel to the page, not at a steep angle.
  3. Fit all four page corners inside the frame and keep the phone steady.
  4. Tap to focus on the text if needed.

Step 3: Capture and crop

  1. Take the photo or use the app’s document capture mode.
  2. Adjust the crop to include the full page without cutting off content.
  3. Correct perspective if the app offers that control.
  4. Use colour or grayscale when it preserves important marks; avoid filters that remove faint text, stamps or annotations.

Step 4: Check and save

Zoom in to inspect small text, signatures, dates and page edges. Save as a PDF when multiple pages belong together, use clear page order, and choose a descriptive file name. Keep the original image or paper document when the content is important.

Scan quality checklist

Important limit: Scans are not certified copies; official uses may need originals. Check the receiving organisation’s requirements before relying on a phone scan.

Questions people ask

Is a phone scan good enough for official documents?

It depends on the organisation and purpose. Some accept digital scans, while others require certified copies, notarisation or original documents.

Should I use flash when scanning a document?

Usually start with even ambient light. Flash can create glare or bright spots on glossy paper, so check the result before using it.

What is the best file format for scanned pages?

PDF is convenient for multi-page documents. JPEG or another image format may be suitable for a single page when the recipient accepts it.

How can I make phone scans easier to read?

Keep the phone parallel to the page, use even light, focus carefully, crop accurately and inspect the saved scan at full size.
