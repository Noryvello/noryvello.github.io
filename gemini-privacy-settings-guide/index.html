Gemini Privacy Settings: What to Check

By the Noryvello team · Published 18 November 2026 · Updated 18 November 2026

Gemini's privacy settings control what is saved from your chats, how long it is kept, and whether your activity is used to improve the service. The most important controls are your activity history, data retention, and review settings, and checking them takes only a few minutes. This guide explains what each control does so you can choose the settings that fit you.

Every chat you have with Gemini leaves a trail: the questions you asked, the answers you got, and sometimes the files you shared. That trail is useful when you want to pick up an old conversation, but it is also personal data, and it is worth knowing where it lives and who can see it. Gemini gives you controls for this, and they are worth a few minutes of your time.

This guide walks through the controls in plain words: what is saved, how long it is kept, how to review or delete it, and what to avoid sharing in the first place. The exact names and locations of settings change between updates, so follow the ideas here and read the short description next to each setting on your own screen.

What Gemini saves from your chats

When you chat with Gemini, the service keeps a record of the conversation so you can return to it later. This usually includes your messages, the responses, and any files or images you attached. It may also include basic information about how you used the service, such as when you chatted and from which device.

Some of this is saved for your convenience, and some of it is used to run and improve the service. The distinction matters: convenience features like chat history help you, while improvement uses mean your activity may be reviewed in anonymized form. The settings below let you control both sides, so it helps to know which control affects which.

Your activity controls: pause, review, and delete

The main control is your activity history setting. When it is on, your chats are saved to your account and you can revisit them. When it is off, new chats are not saved to your history in the same way, though the service may still keep them briefly for safety and abuse prevention. Turning history off does not erase what is already saved.

Take a moment to open your activity page and look at what is there. You will usually find a list of past conversations with options to view, rename, or delete them. Deleting a conversation removes it from your history, and it is worth doing regularly for chats you no longer need, especially any that contain personal details.

Data retention: how long things are kept

Retention settings control how long your saved activity is kept before it is deleted automatically. Some services offer a choice, such as keeping activity for a few months or until you delete it yourself. Shorter retention means less of your history sits on a server, but it also means old conversations disappear sooner.

Think about how you actually use Gemini. If you return to old chats for work or study, longer retention is convenient. If you mostly ask one-off questions, a shorter retention period keeps your footprint small. Either way, the setting only affects future retention, so pair it with a manual clear-out of anything you no longer need.

Reviewing and deleting past activity

Deleting is usually straightforward: open your activity or history page, find the conversation, and choose the delete option. Many services also offer a way to delete everything at once, or everything older than a certain point. If you have used Gemini for a long time, a full review can take a while, so start with the conversations you remember containing sensitive details.

Remember that deleting from your history is not the same as deleting from every system. Copies kept for safety or legal reasons may be held for a short period before they are removed. That is normal for online services, and it is one more reason not to share information you would not want stored anywhere, even briefly.

What Gemini never needs from you

The strongest privacy control is what you choose not to type. Gemini does not need your full name, address, phone number, account numbers, passwords, or identification numbers to answer a question. When you need help with a document, remove personal details before you paste or upload it.

The same applies to other people's information. Do not paste a colleague's email, a child's school details, or a friend's private messages into a chat. If you need help drafting a message, replace real names with placeholders and fill them in yourself afterwards. This habit protects both you and the people around you.

A quick privacy check you can do in five minutes

Open your Gemini settings and work through four things: first, check whether activity history is on and decide if that suits you. Second, look at the retention setting and pick a period you are comfortable with. Third, scan your saved conversations and delete any you no longer need. Fourth, check whether there is a setting about using your data to improve the service, and set it the way you prefer.

Write yourself a short note of what you chose and why, so the next time a settings page moves after an update, you know what to look for. A five-minute check every few months is enough for most people, and it keeps your settings intentional rather than accidental.

Privacy on shared devices and family accounts

If you share a device or an account, your Gemini history may be visible to anyone who opens the app. Sign out when you are done on a shared device, and keep your account's screen lock on. If children use the same device, consider whether their chats are being saved under your account, and review the history together.

For work accounts, remember that your employer may have its own rules about what can be pasted into AI tools. Company documents, customer details, and internal messages usually belong nowhere near a personal chatbot. When in doubt, use the tool your workplace provides, and follow its guidance on what is allowed.

Questions people ask

Does turning off activity history delete my past chats?

No. Turning history off stops new chats from being saved to your history, but anything already saved stays until you delete it. Open your activity page and delete old conversations separately if you want them gone.

Can I use Gemini without saving any activity?

You can pause or turn off activity history, which stops new chats from being saved to your account history. The service may still keep conversations briefly for safety and abuse prevention, so it is not the same as leaving no trace at all.

Does Gemini use my chats to improve its models?

How your activity is used depends on your settings and the service's current policy, which can change. Check the setting about data use for improvement in your account, and read the service's privacy page for the current position.

How do I delete one conversation?

Open your activity or history page, find the conversation, and choose the delete option next to it. Deleting removes it from your history, though copies held for safety reasons may be kept for a short time before removal.

What should I never type into Gemini?

Never type passwords, account numbers, identification numbers, or your full address. Avoid pasting other people's private details too, and strip personal information from any document before you upload it.
