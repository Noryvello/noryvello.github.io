7 Mistakes That Make Handmade Craft Listings Harder to Sell

Short answer: Clear photos, accurate descriptions, realistic production times and complete shipping details help buyers understand handmade items. Avoid the seven listing mistakes below and check every detail before publishing.

The seven mistakes

  1. Using unclear photos. Show the item in good light, from useful angles and at a scale buyers can understand.
  2. Leaving out dimensions. Include measurements and relevant weight so customers can judge size.
  3. Being vague about materials. State what the item is made from and how it should be cared for.
  4. Hiding handmade variation. Explain natural differences in colour, texture or finish where they occur.
  5. Promising unrealistic dispatch times. Account for making, drying, finishing and packing time.
  6. Ignoring packaging and shipping. Describe how fragile items are packed and what delivery options or restrictions apply.
  7. Pricing materials but not labour. Include your time, packaging, platform or payment fees where applicable, and business overheads.

Build buyer confidence

Proofread the listing, make policies easy to find and answer common questions before publishing. Keep claims factual and avoid implying that handmade items are identical when variation is expected.

Important limit: Price for your time too, not just materials.

Questions people ask

What photos should a handmade craft listing include?

Use clear, well-lit photos from multiple useful angles and include a scale reference when size may be hard to judge.

Which details should I include in a handmade listing?

Describe dimensions, materials, finish, care, handmade variations, production time, packaging, shipping and return conditions.

How should I explain handmade variations?

State clearly which differences in colour, texture, pattern or finish may occur between individual pieces.

What costs should I consider when pricing handmade crafts?

Consider materials, your labour time, packaging, applicable platform or payment fees and business overheads.
