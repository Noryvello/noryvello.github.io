Dinner Idea Generator

Pick how much time you have and a cuisine you fancy, and this generator shows three dinner ideas with ingredients and three simple steps each. Every idea comes from a bank of 30 original dinners, so you can press the button again whenever you want a fresh set.

Cooking times are guides, not guarantees. Always check meat is cooked through before serving, and adjust quantities to the number of people at your table. If you have an allergy, read every ingredient list carefully.

How the generator picks your dinners

The bank holds 30 dinner ideas across six cuisines: Italian, Mexican, Indian, East Asian, Mediterranean and Middle Eastern. Each idea is tagged with a time band, so the generator can match what you pick. When you press the button, it looks for ideas that match both your time and your cuisine, shuffles them, and shows three.

If fewer than three ideas match both picks exactly, the generator fills the list from the same cuisine first, then from the same time band. Each card shows its cuisine and time band, so you can see at a glance which ideas match your picks and which are close neighbours. Press the button again for a different three, or change a pick to explore another corner of the bank.

Making the most of your three ideas

Every idea lists its ingredients first, so you can check your cupboard before you commit. If one ingredient is missing, do not let that stop you: most of these dinners are forgiving. A different vegetable, a different bean or a different herb usually works fine, and the method stays the same.

The three steps on each card are deliberately short. They tell you the order of work and the point to watch, and they leave the seasoning to your taste. If you are cooking for children or for someone who dislikes heat, leave the chilli out and let people add their own at the table.

Dinner ideas without the stress

The hardest part of dinner is often not the cooking but the deciding. A short list of three ideas is easier to choose from than a whole cookbook, and a time band keeps the choice honest about the evening you actually have. On a busy night, pick the quick band and let the generator do the thinking. On a slow evening, pick the long band and enjoy a pot that looks after itself.

Keep a few staples on hand and the generator becomes much more useful: tinned tomatoes, beans and chickpeas, rice and pasta, onions and garlic, a few dried spices and some soy sauce. With those in the cupboard, most of the 30 ideas are only a fresh vegetable or a protein away.

Questions people ask

How does the dinner idea generator work?

Pick how much time you have and a cuisine, then press the button. The generator shows three dinner ideas from its bank of 30, each with ingredients and three simple steps. If fewer than three ideas match both of your picks, it fills the list with ideas from the same cuisine first.

Can I use it for a quick weeknight dinner?

Yes. Choose Under 20 minutes and you will only see ideas that fit a short evening. Every quick idea uses everyday ingredients and three short steps.

Do the ideas need special equipment?

No. Every idea uses an ordinary hob, oven or pan. Nothing needs a specialist gadget.

Are the ideas suitable for vegetarians?

Several ideas are meat-free, including the chickpea curry, the vegetable chilli and the lentil soup. Check the ingredients list on each card before you cook.

Can I change the ingredients?

Yes. Treat each list as a starting point. Swap a vegetable, a protein or a spice for something you already have, and keep the same method.
