Your full 30-day plan

The plan uses six training days followed by one rest day. On each training day, the target increases by one small step after every three training days. Your chosen level sets the starting target and step size.

How the targets are calculated

  • Beginner: starts at 3 push-ups and a 10-second plank; each step adds 1 push-up and 5 seconds.
  • Intermediate: starts at 8 push-ups and a 25-second plank; each step adds 2 push-ups and 10 seconds.
  • Advanced: starts at 15 push-ups and a 45-second plank; each step adds 3 push-ups and 15 seconds.

For a training day, count the number of training days completed so far, excluding every seventh-day rest day. The step number is the whole number of completed groups of three training days, starting from zero. The target equals the level's starting amount plus the step number multiplied by that level's step size.

Worked examples

  1. Beginner, Day 1: This is training day 1, so the step number is 0. Push-ups: 3 + (0 × 1) = 3 reps. Plank: 10 + (0 × 5) = 10 seconds.
  2. Intermediate, Day 10: Day 7 is a rest day, so Day 10 is the 9th training day. Step number: floor((9 − 1) ÷ 3) = 2. Push-ups: 8 + (2 × 2) = 12 reps. Plank: 25 + (2 × 10) = 45 seconds.
  3. Advanced, Day 30: Rest days are 7, 14, 21 and 28, so Day 30 is the 26th training day. Step number: floor((26 − 1) ÷ 3) = 8. Push-ups: 15 + (8 × 3) = 39 reps. Plank: 45 + (8 × 15) = 165 seconds.

Questions people ask

How does the 30-day progression work?

Choose a starting level and challenge day. Training targets rise by a small step after each three training days, while every seventh day is a rest day.

Can I start from a day other than Day 1?

Yes. Choose any day from 1 to 30 to view that day's target. The full plan remains visible, and progress is tracked by challenge day.

Where is my progress saved?

Progress is saved in this browser on this device using local storage. It is not synced across browsers or devices, and clearing site data may erase it.

What if a target feels too difficult?

Do not force a target. Pause, reduce the effort, or skip the session if needed. This tracker is a simple planning tool, not an assessment of your ability or a personalised training programme.
