Nickname Idea Generator

Pick a style and this generator gives you five nickname ideas to try. All forty nicknames in the bank are original and kind, and the golden rule is simple: only use a nickname the person likes.

How to use this generator

Choose the style you want, then press the button. You will get five nickname ideas in that style, drawn from a bank of forty. Press again for a different set, or try another style to see what else is there.

Nicknames work best when they fit the person. A sporty nickname suits someone who loves to move, a nature nickname suits someone who loves the outdoors, and a sweet nickname suits someone close to you.

The keep-it-kind rule

The most important rule of nicknames is simple: only use a nickname the person likes. A nickname should feel like a gift, not a label. If someone asks you to stop using one, stop straight away, no questions asked.

Never build a nickname around something a person is sensitive about, such as how they look, how they speak or how they learn. The kindest nicknames come from things people enjoy or do well.

A nickname is only fun if the person likes it. Ask first, and drop it straight away if they do not. Never use a nickname that teases someone about how they look, speak or learn.

Making a nickname your own

The generator gives you starting points. You can shorten a nickname, combine two of them, or add a word that means something to you both. A nickname that only makes sense to two people is often the best kind.

Test a nickname by using it a few times. If it feels natural and the person smiles when they hear it, it has done its job. If it feels forced, move on to the next idea.

Questions people ask

How many nicknames are in the bank?

Forty original nicknames, five for each of eight styles: sweet, funny, cool, classic, nature, food, sporty and short.

What if the person does not like the nickname?

Then do not use it. A nickname only works when the person enjoys hearing it. Ask, listen to the answer, and pick something else if needed.

Can I use these nicknames for a pet?

Yes. Many of the sweet, nature and food nicknames work well for pets too, and pets never complain about their names.

Can I combine two nicknames?

Yes. Mix words from different styles to make something new, such as a nature word with a food word, and you have a nickname nobody else has.

Are the nicknames suitable for children?

Yes. Every nickname in the bank is clean and friendly, with nothing rude or mean, so they are safe for children and for the classroom.
