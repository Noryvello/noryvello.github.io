How the maths works

Each activity's daily data use is its hours multiplied by its per-hour rate, and the month is counted as 30 days:

daily GB = hours × GB per hour (for each activity)
monthly GB = daily GB of all activities added together × 30

The per-hour rates below are typical values to start from. Rates vary by service, quality setting and device, so edit them on the calculator to match your reality:

  • SD streaming: 0.7 GB per hour
  • HD streaming: 3 GB per hour
  • 4K streaming: 7 GB per hour
  • Gaming (online play): 0.1 GB per hour
  • Browsing and social: 0.15 GB per hour
  • Video calls: 1 GB per hour

Worked examples

Example 1 — a typical day. SD 1 hour, HD 2 hours, 4K 0 hours, gaming 1 hour, browsing and social 2 hours, video calls 0.5 hours. SD: 1 × 0.7 = 0.70 GB. HD: 2 × 3 = 6 GB. 4K: 0 × 7 = 0 GB. Gaming: 1 × 0.1 = 0.10 GB. Browsing and social: 2 × 0.15 = 0.30 GB. Video calls: 0.5 × 1 = 0.50 GB. Daily total: 7.60 GB. Monthly: 7.60 × 30 = 228 GB per month.

Example 2 — one hour of HD streaming a day, nothing else. HD: 1 × 3 = 3 GB per day. Monthly: 3 × 30 = 90 GB per month.

Example 3 — 2 hours of 4K streaming and 1 hour of video calls a day, nothing else. 4K: 2 × 7 = 14 GB. Video calls: 1 × 1 = 1 GB. Daily total: 15 GB. Monthly: 15 × 30 = 450 GB per month.

What uses the most data

Streaming quality makes the biggest difference by far. One hour of 4K streaming uses about ten times as much data as one hour of SD streaming, and more than twice as much as HD. If your data use looks high, the streaming quality setting is the first place to look.

Gaming and browsing are light by comparison. An hour of online play typically uses only about 0.1 GB, and browsing and social media about 0.15 GB per hour — though video that auto-plays in your feed pushes the browsing number up. Video calls sit in the middle at around 1 GB per hour with video on.

Remember that the gaming rate covers online play only. Downloading a game, or a large update, is a one-off download that can be many GB on its own — those are not counted here, so add them separately if they apply to you.

Questions people ask

How much data does streaming use?

It depends on the quality. A typical hour of streaming uses about 0.7 GB in SD, 3 GB in HD and 7 GB in 4K. Those are typical rates, not fixed figures: the same service can use more or less depending on the device, the title and the quality settings, so edit the rates on this page to match your own habits.

Why can I edit the rates?

Because the same activity uses different amounts of data for different people. Streaming services compress video differently, quality settings change the rate, and a video call with the camera off uses less than one with video on. The defaults are typical rates to get you started; editing them to match your reality makes the estimate much more useful.

Does this include uploads?

No. The calculator estimates the data your activities use as a whole, using the per-hour rates shown, and it does not split uploads from downloads. It also leaves out one-off downloads such as game downloads, system updates and cloud backups. If those matter to you, add them separately on top of the monthly total.

How do I check my real usage?

Most phones show mobile data usage in their settings, and many routers or broadband accounts show how much data your home connection has used. Your provider's app or account page usually shows the same. Compare the real number with this estimate, then adjust the rates here until the two roughly agree.
