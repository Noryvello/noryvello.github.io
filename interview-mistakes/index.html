7 Interview Mistakes That Can Undercut a Strong Candidate

Short answer: prepare specific examples, understand the role, listen carefully, ask thoughtful questions and check the practical details before the interview.

Seven interview mistakes to avoid

  1. Arriving unprepared: learn what the organisation does and reread the role description.
  2. Giving vague answers: use a specific situation, your action and the outcome.
  3. Not listening: pause, answer the question asked and clarify if needed.
  4. Speaking negatively about past employers: stay factual and professional.
  5. Ignoring the role's requirements: connect your experience to the skills listed.
  6. Having no questions: prepare thoughtful questions about the work, team and expectations.
  7. Forgetting practical details: check the time, location or video link, travel and contact instructions.

Preparation checklist

A simple answer structure

For experience questions, describe the situation, the task, the action you took and the result. Keep the focus on your own contribution and be ready to explain the details.

Important limit: No guide guarantees a job; preparation improves chances only.

Questions people ask

What is the biggest interview mistake?

A common damaging mistake is arriving without understanding the role or preparing relevant examples. The most important preparation depends on the interview.

How many questions should I prepare for an interviewer?

Prepare a small set of relevant questions about the role, team, expectations and next steps. Choose the ones that have not already been answered.

How should I answer experience questions?

Use a specific example and explain the situation, task, actions you took and result. Be accurate about your own contribution.

What should I do the day before an interview?

Review the role and your application, prepare examples and questions, confirm the time and location or link, and organise anything you need to bring.
