7 Common Moving Day Mistakes + Day-Plan Checklist

Short answer: the biggest moving day mistake is failing to pack an essentials box for the first night. Avoid the seven mistakes below and tick off the day-plan checklist as you go.

Note: this guide offers general advice; specific circumstances — valuable items, pets, long-distance moves — may need professional help.

7 mistakes to dodge

  1. No essentials box. Pack toiletries, chargers, medicines and a change of clothes separately — you will need them before any box is unpacked.
  2. Too few supplies. Boxes, tape and padding always run out faster than expected; buy more than you think you need.
  3. Heavy items last. Load heavy boxes and furniture first so lighter items sit safely on top.
  4. No final walk-through. Check every room, cupboard, balcony and storage space before leaving the old place.
  5. Unlabelled boxes. Label every box by room and contents — unloading takes half the time.
  6. Over-packed truck. Cramming causes damage; leave space and secure loads so nothing shifts.
  7. Unconfirmed arrival time. Confirm the movers' ETA the day before and keep their number with you.

Questions people ask

What is the biggest moving day mistake to avoid?

Failing to pack a moving-day essentials box. Keep toiletries, chargers, medicines, a change of clothes and important documents with you — not in the truck.

How can I keep track of tasks on moving day?

Use the checklist on this page and tick tasks off as you go. Label every box by room so unloading stays organized.

Should I clean the old place before leaving?

Yes. A quick clean helps you get your security deposit back and leaves the place ready for the next occupant. Do a final walk-through of every room, cupboard and balcony.

What if my new home is not ready when I arrive?

Plan a backup spot for your belongings, keep valuables and documents with you, and communicate with the landlord ahead of time about the handover.
