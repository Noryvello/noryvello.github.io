How Many Pizzas Should You Order for Your Group?

Short answer: enter the guest count, choose appetite and slices per pizza, and get an estimated whole-pizza order with no guesswork about the arithmetic.

How the estimate works

The calculator multiplies guests by the selected slices per person, then divides by slices per pizza and rounds up to a whole pizza. For example, 8 average-appetite guests at 3 slices each need 24 slices, or 3 eight-slice pizzas.

Ordering tips

  • Count children and adults according to likely appetite, not just headcount.
  • For a long event, late-night meal or group with few side dishes, consider the hungry setting.
  • For a buffet with substantial sides, the light setting may be a better starting estimate.
  • Check whether the restaurant cuts its pizzas into the number of slices you selected.
Important limit: Appetites vary; round up for hungry groups.

Questions people ask

How many pizzas do I need for 10 people?

At 3 slices per person and 8 slices per pizza, 10 people need 30 slices, so order 4 pizzas. This is an estimate.

How many slices should I allow per person?

A practical starting estimate is 2 slices for a light appetite, 3 for average appetite and 4 for a hungry group. Actual appetites vary.

Should I order extra pizza for a party?

Consider rounding up when guests are hungry, the event is long or there are few side dishes. The calculator already rounds partial pizzas up.

Does pizza size change the calculation?

Yes. Pizza size can affect slice size even when slice count is the same. Use the slice count and portion size that match the pizzas you plan to order.
