Storage Unit Size Finder

Not sure what size storage unit to book? Tick the furniture you plan to store, say how many boxes you have, and this finder adds up the typical volumes and suggests a unit size that fits — in cubic metres, so you can compare it with any facility's sizes.

Tick the items you plan to store. Each volume is a typical estimate for that item.

The volumes here are typical estimates, not measurements of your own furniture, and unit sizes vary by facility — check their sizes before booking. This finder is a planning guide only, not a guarantee that everything will fit.

How the suggestion is worked out

The finder adds up the typical volume of every item you tick, plus 0.2 m³ for each moving box, and matches the total to a size band:

  • Up to 3 m³ — small unit (around 25 sq ft)
  • Over 3 m³ up to 7 m³ — medium unit (around 50 sq ft)
  • Over 7 m³ up to 15 m³ — large unit (around 100 sq ft)
  • Over 15 m³ — extra-large unit (150 sq ft or larger)

Worked examples:

  • Example 1. Sofa (2.0 m³) + double bed (2.5 m³) + 4 boxes (0.8 m³) = 5.3 m³ — medium unit (around 50 sq ft).
  • Example 2. Sofa (2.0 m³) + double bed (2.5 m³) + wardrobe (1.5 m³) + dining table (1.0 m³) + 6 boxes (1.2 m³) = 8.2 m³ — large unit (around 100 sq ft).
  • Example 3. 10 boxes only: 10 × 0.2 m³ = 2.0 m³ — small unit (around 25 sq ft).

Before you book

  • Measure your biggest items. If your sofa or bed is much larger than average, your total will be higher than the estimate — measure length × width × height in metres to get its volume.
  • Leave space to walk in. A unit packed to the ceiling is hard to use. Leave a path to the back so you can reach things without unpacking everything.
  • Stack boxes, but not too high. Heavy boxes go at the bottom, light ones on top, and nothing you need soon should end up buried at the back.
  • Check the facility's own sizes. The same label can mean a different size at different sites, so compare your total volume in cubic metres with the unit you are looking at.

Questions people ask

How much space do I need for a one-bedroom flat?

It depends on what you own. Tick everything you plan to store and the finder adds up the volumes for you. As a rough guide, a sofa, a double bed and four boxes come to 5.3 cubic metres, which points to a medium unit of around 50 sq ft.

What if my items are bigger than average?

Measure them. The volume of any item is its length times its width times its height, all in metres. If your total is much higher than the typical volumes used here, move up to the next size band.

Do I need extra space inside the unit?

Yes, leave room to walk in and reach the things at the back. If you plan to visit the unit often, or your total sits close to the top of a size band, consider going one size up.

Are storage unit sizes the same everywhere?

No. Unit sizes vary by facility, and the same label can mean a different size at different sites. Always check the facility's own sizes and, if you can, look at the unit before you book.
