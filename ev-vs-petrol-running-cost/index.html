How the Calculation Works

  • Metric Formula: EV Cost = (Distance 100) (kWh per 100 km) Electricity Rate. Petrol Cost = (Distance 100) (Liters per 100 km) Petrol Price.
  • Imperial Formula: EV Cost = (Distance Miles per kWh) Electricity Rate. Petrol Cost = (Distance MPG) Petrol Price per Gallon.

Questions people ask

Why does the calculator ask me to enter my own electricity and petrol rates?

Electricity tariffs and fuel taxes vary dramatically by country, region, time-of-use plan, and whether you charge at home or at a public DC fast charger. Entering your actual local numbers gives an accurate comparison.

Does home charging cost differently than public fast charging?

Yes. Off-peak home electricity rates are often substantially lower per kWh than commercial public fast-charging stations, which can narrow or reverse the running-cost gap.

What is charging efficiency loss in an EV?

When charging an EV from a wall outlet or charger, a small percentage of electricity is lost as heat during AC-to-DC conversion and battery thermal management, meaning meter consumption is slightly higher than energy stored in the battery.

What other costs matter besides energy per distance?

Upfront vehicle price, financing interest, insurance rates, tire wear, registration fees, and scheduled maintenance all affect total cost of ownership over several years.
