Cable TV or Streaming: Which Bundle Costs More?

Short answer: enter the cable bill and every streaming subscription or add-on to compare the monthly cost of each bundle.

Compare equivalent bundles

List every service you actually pay for, including sports, premium channels, extra screens, ad-free upgrades, rentals and bundled add-ons. Compare what each option provides, not just the headline monthly subscription. If a bundle includes internet or phone service, separate those costs where possible.

Simple review checklist

  • Check the latest bill, not an introductory offer.
  • Count annual plans as their monthly equivalent for a fair comparison.
  • Include taxes, equipment rental and recurring fees.
  • Check cancellation terms and whether you would lose channels or content you use.
Important limit: Streaming costs creep up with add-ons; count every subscription.

Questions people ask

How do I compare cable TV and streaming fairly?

Add the full monthly cable bill and all streaming subscriptions, add-ons, rentals and recurring fees. Compare the services and channels you actually use.

Should I count annual streaming plans?

Yes. Divide an annual plan cost by 12 to estimate its monthly equivalent, and include it alongside monthly subscriptions.

Can streaming be more expensive than cable?

Yes. Several subscriptions and add-ons can add up. The result depends on your actual plan costs and bundle choices.

Does this calculator include internet service?

No. Enter only the costs you want to compare. If cable and streaming both require internet, compare internet costs separately or add the relevant amount consistently to both sides.
