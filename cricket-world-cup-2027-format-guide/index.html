Cricket World Cup 2027 Format Guide

The Cricket World Cup 2027 has 14 teams and 54 matches. The format runs in four stages: two groups, then a super stage, then the semi-finals, then the final in Johannesburg on Sunday 21 November 2027.

The format at a glance

  1. Group stage — the 14 teams play in two groups.
  2. Super stage — the teams that advance from the groups play on.
  3. Semi-finals — the teams that come through the super stage play for a place in the final.
  4. Final — Sunday 21 November 2027, in Johannesburg.

The format in four stages

The tournament is built in stages, and each stage narrows the field down to the final.

1. Two groups. The 14 teams are split into two groups for the opening stage of the tournament.

2. Super stage. After the groups, the teams that advance move into a super stage, where they play on before the knockout rounds.

3. Semi-finals. The teams that come through the super stage play the semi-finals, and the two winners go through to the final.

4. The final. The final is played on Sunday 21 November 2027 in Johannesburg, and the winner of that match lifts the trophy.
