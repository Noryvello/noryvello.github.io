Gutter Length Calculator

Enter the lengths of your roof edges and the spacing between downspouts, and this calculator works out the total gutter length, how many downspouts you need and how many joiners to buy. The working is shown so you can check every step.

This calculator counts lengths and fittings from your measurements only. It does not check falls, outlets or local building practice, and every roof needs a slight fall towards the downspouts to drain. Follow the maker's fitting guidance for your gutter system.

How to use this calculator

Measure each straight roof edge that needs a gutter, in metres, and enter up to four sides. Leave any side you do not need blank. Enter the spacing between downspouts. Your gutter maker's guidance will suggest a figure. Enter the length of one gutter piece as sold, then press Calculate to see the total gutter length, the downspout count and the joiner estimate.

How the calculation works

The gutter length is the sum of the sides you entered. The downspout count is the total length divided by the spacing, rounded up, so no stretch of gutter is longer than the spacing you chose. Joiners are counted side by side: each side is split into whole gutter pieces, and each join between two pieces needs one joiner. The steps show the split for each side so you can check it.

How to use the result

Buy the gutter length shown, plus the joiners, and check that your downspout count matches where water can actually drain away. Corners, stop ends and outlets are not counted here, so list those separately from your roof shape before ordering. Check the fall of each run when fitting, so water flows towards a downspout instead of pooling.

Questions people ask

What is a downspout?

A downspout is the vertical pipe that carries water from the gutter down to the ground or a drain. The calculator spaces them evenly along the total gutter length.

How far apart should downspouts be?

It depends on the gutter system and the roof area it drains. Check your gutter maker's guidance for a spacing figure and enter it here.

What is a joiner?

A joiner connects two lengths of gutter end to end. Each side of the roof needs one joiner for every join between gutter pieces, and the calculator counts them side by side.

What if my roof has more than four sides?

Add the extra sides together and enter the combined length in one of the four boxes. The total length and downspout count stay correct, though the joiner count is then approximate.

Do I need anything else besides gutters and joiners?

Yes, corners, stop ends, outlets and brackets depend on your roof shape and are not counted here. List them from your roof plan before ordering.
