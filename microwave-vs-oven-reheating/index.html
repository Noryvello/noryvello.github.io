Questions people ask

Does the oven estimate include preheating?

Yes. The oven time and energy estimate add the preheating minutes to the reheating minutes.

Why can microwave reheating take less time?

Microwaves heat food differently from conventional ovens, but the actual time depends on food quantity, shape, starting temperature, and appliance power.

Does wattage equal the energy used every minute?

The tool estimates energy using the entered rated wattage for the full entered time; real appliances may cycle or vary power.

Is this a measured comparison for every food?

No. It is an estimate from your inputs and does not account for food type, uneven heating, or required safe internal temperature.
