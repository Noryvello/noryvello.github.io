Cricket World Cup 2027 Venues

Twelve stadiums across South Africa, Zimbabwe and Namibia will host the 2027 Cricket World Cup, from 2 October to 21 November 2027. South Africa has eight venues, Zimbabwe has three, and Namibia has one, and the final is scheduled for Sunday 21 November 2027 at Wanderers Stadium in Johannesburg. This page lists every venue by country, with its city.

The 12 venues were announced on 30 July 2026. They are spread across the three host countries: eight in South Africa, three in Zimbabwe and one in Namibia. The tournament runs from 2 October to 21 November 2027, with 14 teams playing 57 matches, and the venues cover the whole of that window, from the Super Series in Windhoek at the start to the final in Johannesburg at the end.

Three countries sharing one tournament means the venues are spread across southern Africa rather than concentrated in a single country. South Africa takes two-thirds of the venues, Zimbabwe takes a quarter of them, and Namibia takes one. Every venue on this page comes from the announcement of 30 July 2026, and the list below gives each stadium with the city it stands in.

This page lists the venues exactly as announced on 30 July 2026. It does not give stadium capacities or say how many matches each venue hosts, because those details were not part of the announcement this page is based on.

How the venues were announced

The full list of 12 venues was announced on 30 July 2026, more than a year before the tournament begins. The announcement named every stadium and its city, grouped by host country, and it also confirmed where the final will be played: the final is scheduled for Sunday 21 November 2027 at Wanderers Stadium in Johannesburg.

The split between the three host countries is eight venues in South Africa, three in Zimbabwe and one in Namibia. That adds up to 12, which is the full set of stadiums for a tournament of 57 matches. The announcement also confirmed the role of Namibia's single venue: the Namibia Cricket Ground in Windhoek hosts the Super Series, the round robin played from 2 to 6 October 2027 that decides the last group-stage place.

One of the 12 venues is a new stadium: the Fale Mosi-oa-Tunya International Cricket Stadium in Victoria Falls, Zimbabwe. The announcement gave the stadium names and cities as listed below, and this page does not add anything beyond what was announced.

South Africa — eight venues

South Africa has eight of the 12 venues, more than the other two host countries combined. Its venues are spread across eight cities, from Johannesburg and Cape Town to Paarl and KuGompo City. The final is scheduled at Wanderers Stadium in Johannesburg, so South Africa hosts both the largest share of venues and the last match of the tournament.

The eight South African venues are Wanderers Stadium in Johannesburg, SuperSport Park in Centurion (Tshwane), Newlands in Cape Town, Kingsmead in Durban, St George's Park in Gqeberha, Mangaung Oval in Bloemfontein, Boland Park in Paarl, and Buffalo Park in KuGompo City, which was formerly known as East London. Each venue is in a different city, so the tournament reaches eight South African cities in total.

Johannesburg's Wanderers Stadium is the venue for the final, which is scheduled for Sunday 21 November 2027. That makes Wanderers the stadium where the tournament ends, on the last day of the window that runs from 2 October to 21 November 2027. The other seven South African venues host matches across the tournament, and the announcement did not say how many matches each one hosts.

Zimbabwe — three venues

Zimbabwe has three of the 12 venues. Two of them are in Harare and Bulawayo, and the third is in Victoria Falls, where a new stadium has been built for the tournament.

The three Zimbabwean venues are Harare Sports Club in Harare, Queens Sports Club in Bulawayo, and the Fale Mosi-oa-Tunya International Cricket Stadium in Victoria Falls. The Victoria Falls stadium is the new stadium in the list: it is the only one of the 12 venues described as new in the announcement. Zimbabwe is also one of the two host nations that qualified directly for the tournament, alongside South Africa.

Namibia — one venue

Namibia has one of the 12 venues: the Namibia Cricket Ground in Windhoek. It is the smallest share of the three host countries, but the venue has a distinct role in the tournament: it hosts the Super Series, the round robin played from 2 to 6 October 2027 between the three lowest qualifier teams, the teams that finished 2nd, 3rd and 4th in the World Cup Qualifier.

The Super Series is played at the very start of the tournament window, which opens on 2 October 2027. Its winner advances to the group stage as Qualifier B, so the Namibia Cricket Ground is where the last group-stage place is decided, days before the group stage begins on 7 October. Namibia is the third host country of the tournament, alongside South Africa and Zimbabwe.

The final

The final is scheduled for Sunday 21 November 2027 at Wanderers Stadium in Johannesburg. That is the last day of the tournament window, which runs from 2 October to 21 November 2027. The final is the last of the tournament's 57 matches, and it is played in South Africa, at the Johannesburg stadium that is one of the eight South African venues.

The final venue was confirmed in the same announcement as the other 11 venues, on 30 July 2026. The date and venue are stated here as scheduled: the final is scheduled for Sunday 21 November 2027 at Wanderers Stadium, Johannesburg.
