Hotel vs Apartment Stay Cost

A hotel and an apartment charge in different ways: hotels often bundle meals and cleaning into the nightly rate, while apartments add a cleaning fee and leave you to feed yourself. Enter your nights, rates, fees and meals included, and this page shows the total stay cost for each.

This assumes three meals a day and counts only the costs you enter. It leaves out transport, activities, deposits, service charges and anything the stay charges separately — check what your booking actually includes.

How it works

Each side is added up the same way: the nightly rate times the number of nights, plus the cleaning fee for the stay, plus the meals you still need to buy. The calculator assumes three meals a day, so the meals you buy each day are three minus the meals included with the stay. Those meals are charged at the average meal cost you enter.

The cleaning fee is counted once per stay, not per night. If a stay has no cleaning fee, enter 0. If a stay includes no meals, enter 0 for meals included and all three daily meals will be counted at your meal cost.

How to use the result

Longer stays change the balance. The cleaning fee is charged once, so it matters less the more nights you stay, while the meals you buy matter more. A hotel that looks expensive per night can work out cheaper over a long stay if it includes meals, and an apartment with a kitchen can win if you cook instead of eating out.

If you plan to cook in the apartment, enter the cost of a home-cooked meal as your average meal cost. The calculator only needs your own average, so use the figure that matches how you actually eat.

What this comparison leaves out

A stay costs more than the room and the food. The calculator does not count transport, activities, deposits, service charges, or anything the stay charges separately, such as parking or laundry. Check the booking details for what is included before you decide.

It also leaves out comfort and space. A hotel room and an apartment of the same price can be very different places to stay, so use the cost result alongside what each place actually offers.

Questions people ask

Why three meals a day?

It is a simple assumption so the two stays can be compared on the same basis. If you eat fewer meals out, enter a lower meal cost to match your own pattern.

What if the apartment has a kitchen and I cook?

Enter the cost of a home-cooked meal instead of a meal out. The calculator only needs your own average cost per meal.

What if the hotel includes breakfast only?

Enter 1 for meals included per day at the hotel. The other two meals a day will be counted at the meal cost you enter.

Does the cleaning fee apply per night or per stay?

Per stay, as a single amount. That is how this calculator counts it, so enter the full fee once.

What else should I check before booking?

Check what the rate includes, the cancellation terms and any extra charges. The calculator only counts the figures you enter.
