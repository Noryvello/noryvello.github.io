Drywall Sheets Calculator — How Many Sheets Will Cover Your Walls?

Estimate drywall sheets by dividing the total wall and ceiling area by the area of one sheet, then rounding up. Enter the combined area you plan to cover and choose a standard sheet size.

Honest limit: Count doors and windows out only if measuring precisely; add one spare sheet.

How the Drywall Sheet Formula Works

  1. Add the wall and ceiling areas you intend to cover.
  2. Divide the total by the selected sheet's face area.
  3. Round up because sheets are purchased as whole panels.

This is an area-only estimate. It does not optimize sheet orientation, staggered joints, framing layout, offcuts, damaged panels, multiple layers, or special fire-, moisture-, or sound-rated assemblies.

Worked Example

If the measured coverage area is 960 sq ft and you choose 4 × 8 ft sheets, each sheet covers 32 sq ft. The area calculation is 960 ÷ 32 = 30 sheets. Add a spare sheet and any additional allowance needed for the room layout.

Questions people ask

How do I calculate how many drywall sheets I need?

Add the wall and ceiling areas to be covered, divide by the face area of one selected sheet, and round up to the next whole sheet.

What is the area of a standard 4 by 8 drywall sheet?

A 4 × 8 foot sheet has a face area of 32 square feet, before accounting for cuts, waste, or installation details.

Should I subtract doors and windows from wall area?

You may subtract openings when you measure them precisely. For a rough estimate, leaving them included can help offset cuts and waste; do not subtract them and then forget to allow for offcuts.

Does this calculator account for waste or multiple drywall layers?

No. It calculates one layer from total area only. Add a spare sheet and separately account for layout waste, damaged sheets, and any additional layers.
