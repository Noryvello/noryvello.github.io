7 Rental Desk Mistakes to Avoid Before You Drive Away

Short answer: Before leaving the rental lot, understand the agreement, inspect and document the car, confirm fuel and mileage rules, and know what to do if there is damage or a breakdown. Use the checklist before signing and driving away.

The seven mistakes at the rental desk

  1. Signing without reading. Review charges, driver rules, permitted use and dispute procedures.
  2. Assuming insurance is included. Ask what cover applies, the excess, exclusions and any optional products.
  3. Missing the fuel rule. Confirm whether the car must be returned full, at the same level or under another policy.
  4. Ignoring mileage limits. Check included distance and any extra-distance terms.
  5. Not documenting existing damage. Photograph the exterior, wheels, glass and interior, and have existing damage recorded.
  6. Skipping a basic vehicle check. Familiarize yourself with lights, controls, tires and warning indicators.
  7. Forgetting return logistics. Confirm location, opening hours, late-return process and after-hours key drop rules.

Keep a record

Save the agreement, condition report, photographs, fuel record and return confirmation until the final account is settled.

Important limit: Read the rental agreement before signing; insurance and fuel terms vary.

Questions people ask

What should I inspect before driving a rental car?

Check and photograph existing damage, inspect tires and lights, and familiarize yourself with controls and warning indicators.

Is rental car insurance always included?

Coverage varies by rental agreement and location. Confirm the cover, excess, exclusions and optional products in writing.

What fuel policy should I choose?

Read the specific agreement and confirm the required return fuel level and how any shortfall is charged.

What should I do when returning the rental car?

Follow the stated return location and timing, document the vehicle condition and fuel level, and keep the return confirmation.
