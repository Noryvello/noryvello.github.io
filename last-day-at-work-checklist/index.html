Last Day at Work Checklist

This checklist covers handover, returning equipment, saving contacts, sorting documents and saying goodbye, so your last day runs smoothly. Tick each item as you go and leave with everything in order.

A good last day is planned, not improvised. The handover notes, the returned laptop, the saved contacts and the goodbye message all take a little time, and they are much easier to finish when you start early.

This checklist covers the five parts of leaving well: handover, returns, contacts, documents and farewell. Start it a week or two before your last day, and tick items off as you go.

Start the handover early

Write your handover notes while you still remember the details. List your open tasks, where the files live, and who to contact about what. If a replacement is starting, introduce them to your main contacts before you go.

Finish what you can, and be honest about what you cannot. A short note saying a task is half done and where it stands is worth more than a silent inbox.

Leave on good terms

Thank the colleagues who helped you, and keep it warm and professional. A short goodbye message to your team costs little and is remembered well.

Save the contacts you want to keep, with their permission, and update your professional profiles. The people you worked with are the most useful thing you take with you — treat the farewell as the start of a longer connection, not the end of one.

Questions people ask

When should I start the handover?

Start a week or two before your last day if you can. Write the notes while the details are fresh, and leave time for questions from your replacement.

What should I return on my last day?

Return your laptop and charger, keys, badges, passes and any company phone. Check with your manager or the office team for anything you may have missed.

Can I keep my work contacts?

You can keep contacts who agree to stay in touch. Do not take client lists or confidential information — that belongs to your employer.

What documents do I need when I leave?

Check your final payslip and tax documents, and ask for a reference letter if you need one. Keep copies of anything you are entitled to.

Should I write a farewell message?

A short goodbye message to your team is a kind way to leave. Thank your colleagues, share your contact details if you like, and wish them well.
