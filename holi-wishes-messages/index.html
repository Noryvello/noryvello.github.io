How to write a Holi greeting

A good Holi greeting mentions colour, joy and the season ahead. Wish the person happiness and good health, and add a line about the festival itself: the colours, the music or the time spent with family and friends.

Keep it warm and respectful. Holi is a religious festival for many people, so avoid jokes about the festival and focus on colour, joy and good wishes.

When to send Holi wishes

Send your wishes on the day of Holi or in the days around it. A message on the morning of the festival is a lovely way to start the celebrations.

If you are sending a card, post it a few days early so it arrives in time. A message sent after the festival is still welcome, especially if it looks forward to the year ahead.

Questions people ask

What is a good Holi wish for family?

A good family wish mentions colour, joy and togetherness. Wish them a bright year ahead, and add a line about the festival you share.

Can I send Holi wishes to someone who does not celebrate?

Yes, if you know them well. A simple 'Happy Holi' with warm wishes is kind. If you are unsure, a general greeting about the season is a safe choice.

Should Holi wishes mention colour?

Colour is at the heart of Holi, so it is natural to mention it. You can also wish for joy, health and a bright year ahead.

Is it okay to send Holi wishes by text?

Yes. A text or message is fine for friends and colleagues, and a card suits family. Send it on the day or in the days around the festival.

What should I avoid in a Holi message?

Avoid jokes about the festival or about getting covered in colour. Keep the message warm, respectful and focused on joy and good wishes.
