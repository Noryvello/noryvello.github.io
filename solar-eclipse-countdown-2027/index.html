Solar Eclipse 2027 Countdown

A total solar eclipse happens on Monday 2 August 2027. The countdown below shows the days, hours, minutes and seconds left until it begins.

Never look at the sun without proper eclipse glasses.

Never look at the sun without proper eclipse glasses. Ordinary sunglasses do not protect your eyes. This page counts down to the date only — it does not give viewing times for any place, so check a local eclipse guide for the timing where you are.

Where the eclipse will be visible

The path of totality — the narrow band where the sun is fully covered — passes through these places:

  • Southern Spain
  • Morocco
  • Libya
  • Egypt
  • Saudi Arabia

Outside the path of totality, the eclipse appears as a partial eclipse, where only part of the sun is covered.

How the countdown works

The countdown counts to midnight at the start of 2 August 2027 in your own local time, and updates every second. Once the date has passed, it shows how many days and hours have gone by since the eclipse day began.

Questions people ask

When is the solar eclipse in 2027?

The total solar eclipse happens on Monday 2 August 2027.

Where will the 2027 solar eclipse be visible?

The path of totality passes through southern Spain, Morocco, Libya, Egypt and Saudi Arabia. A partial eclipse will be visible from a much wider area.

Is it safe to look at a solar eclipse?

No — never look at the sun without proper eclipse glasses. Ordinary sunglasses do not protect your eyes, and looking at the sun can cause serious eye damage.

How long will totality last in 2027?

It varies by place along the path of totality, so there is no single figure — the length depends on where you watch from.
