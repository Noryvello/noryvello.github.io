What you need before you start

Before you print, make sure the printer is switched on, has paper loaded, and has ink or toner. A printer in sleep mode may not appear on your phone, so wake it up first by pressing a button on the printer itself. If you are printing over Wi-Fi, the phone and the printer must be on the same network, which is the most common thing people get wrong. If your home has two networks, such as a main network and a guest network, both devices need to be on the same one.

You do not usually need to install anything. Android phones have a built-in print service, and iPhones have built-in printing that works with printers that support it. If your printer is older, it may need the maker's app, which you can find by searching for the printer maker's name in your app store. The steps below assume the built-in option, and the troubleshooting checklist covers what to do if the printer does not appear.

Printing over Wi-Fi

Wi-Fi printing is the simplest option once it is set up. The phone finds the printer on the network, sends the document, and the printer prints it. You can print from almost any app: a document, a photo, a web page, or an email. Open what you want to print, tap the share button or the menu, choose Print, and pick your printer from the list. Choose the number of copies and the paper size, then press Print.

The first time you print, the phone may take a few seconds to find the printer. If it does not appear, check that the printer is on the same Wi-Fi network and that it is not in sleep mode. Once the phone has found the printer once, it usually remembers it, and later prints are quicker. Keep the phone within range of the router while printing, because a weak signal can interrupt a large print job.

Printing without Wi-Fi

If there is no Wi-Fi network, many printers offer a direct wireless mode. In this mode the printer sends out its own wireless signal, and your phone connects to it directly, the way it would connect to a network. Turn the direct mode on from the printer's screen or buttons, find the printer's network name in your phone's Wi-Fi settings, and connect to it. Then print as usual. Remember that while your phone is connected to the printer this way, it may not have internet access.

A USB cable is another option on Android phones that support it. Connect the phone to the printer with a suitable cable and adapter, and the phone should offer the printer when you choose Print. This is a useful fallback when wireless printing will not work, and it is also handy for a printer that has no wireless features at all. iPhones do not print over a USB cable, so a direct wireless mode or a Wi-Fi network is needed instead.

Printing photos and documents

Photos and documents print differently, and a few choices make a big difference. For photos, use photo paper if you have it, and choose the photo paper size in the print options so the image is not stretched. For documents, plain paper is fine, and printing in black and white saves colour ink when colour is not needed. Check the preview before you print, because a document that looks right on a phone screen can have awkward page breaks on paper.

If a document prints too small or cuts off at the edges, check the paper size setting. A document made for one paper size can print badly on another. Most print dialogs let you fit the page to the paper, which fixes the problem. For a long document, print one page first to check it looks right before printing the whole thing.

These steps cover the built-in printing on Android phones and iPhones. Older printers may need the maker's app, and some very old printers cannot print from a phone at all. If your printer manual does not mention phone printing, a computer may still be needed.

Questions people ask

Can I print from my phone without Wi-Fi?

Yes, if your printer supports a direct connection. Many printers offer a direct wireless mode or a USB cable connection, and some support Bluetooth. Check your printer manual for the options your model offers.

Why does my phone not find my printer?

The most common cause is that the phone and the printer are on different networks. Make sure both are on the same Wi-Fi network, that the printer is switched on, and that the printer is not in sleep mode.

Do I need an app to print from my phone?

Often not. Android and iPhone both have built-in printing that works with most modern printers. Some older printers need the maker's app, so check your printer manual if the built-in option does not find your printer.

Can I print a photo from my phone?

Yes. Open the photo, tap the share button, and choose Print. Pick your printer, choose the paper size and number of copies, and press Print. Photo paper gives a better result than plain paper for photos.

Why did my print come out blank?

A blank page usually means the printer is out of ink or toner, or a nozzle is blocked. Check the ink levels, run the printer's cleaning cycle from its settings, and try a test page before printing again.
