How to use this generator

Choose the time you have and the style you want, then press the button. You will get three ideas that match, each with a short ingredient list and three steps. If you want a different set, press the button again and the generator will show the next three ideas for your choices. Every idea in the bank is original and written for this page.

The ingredient lists are starting points. Swap fruits, spreads, breads and toppings for whatever you have at home, and change the amounts to suit your appetite. The steps are kept short on purpose, so each idea is easy to follow on a busy morning without reading a long recipe.

Making mornings easier

The best breakfasts on busy mornings are the ones that are mostly ready before the morning starts. Overnight oats can be mixed the night before and grabbed from the fridge. Smoothie ingredients can be portioned into bags and frozen, ready to blend. Egg muffins can be baked at the weekend and reheated during the week. A little preparation the night before turns a rushed morning into a calm one.

Keep a small set of breakfast staples in the cupboard and fridge: oats, bread, eggs, yoghurt, fruit, nuts and a spread you like. With those on hand, most of the ideas in this generator are possible without a special shopping trip. When you find an idea you like, write it down and make it part of your regular rotation, so you never have to think hard about breakfast again.

Mixing up the week

Eating the same breakfast every day is fine, but a little variety makes mornings more enjoyable. Try a sweet idea one day and a savoury one the next, or a light idea on a warm day and a hearty one when you have a long morning ahead. The generator is designed for this: pick a different style each day and let it suggest something new.

Weekends are the time for the longer ideas, such as baked oatmeal, a frittata or a proper breakfast plate. Those take 20 minutes or more, but they are worth it when there is no rush. Save the quick ideas for weekdays, when ten minutes is all you have, and let the slow ideas be a weekend treat.

This page is general cooking guidance. Take care with hot pans, boiling water and sharp knives, and keep them away from children. If anyone has a food allergy, check every ingredient before cooking, and cut grapes and similar round foods for young children.

Ideas for different appetites

A light breakfast suits a morning when you want something small and fresh, such as a yoghurt bowl or fruit with cheese. A hearty breakfast suits a long morning or a day with hard work ahead, such as a full plate with eggs and beans. A sweet breakfast suits a slow morning or a treat, such as pancakes or baked oatmeal. A savoury breakfast suits anyone who prefers salt to sugar in the morning, such as eggs with vegetables or a breakfast burrito.

On-the-go ideas are for mornings when there is no time to sit down at all. A smoothie in a bottle, a breakfast burrito wrapped in foil, or a yoghurt pot with granola can all be eaten on the way. Make them the night before if you can, so the morning is just grab and go.

Questions people ask

How does the generator pick the three ideas?

It starts with ideas that match both your time and your style, then fills any gaps with ideas in the same style. Press the button again to see a different set of three.

Can I change the ingredients in an idea?

Yes. The ingredients are a starting point, not a rule. Swap fruits, spreads, breads and toppings for what you have at home, and adjust the amounts to your appetite.

Which ideas work for a busy morning?

Pick the under 10 minutes option, or the on the go style, for ideas you can make and eat in a hurry. Overnight oats and smoothie ideas can be prepared the night before.

Are the ideas suitable for children?

Most of them are, and children often enjoy helping with simple ones such as yoghurt bowls, toast toppings and smoothies. Keep hot pans and sharp knives to adults, and cut grapes and similar foods for young children.

How many ideas are in the bank?

Thirty original ideas, covering three time bands and five styles, so there is something for a quick weekday morning and for a slow weekend breakfast.
