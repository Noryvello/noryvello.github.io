Every fair is different. Check the organiser's own rules for your stall — table size, power, setup times — before the day. This checklist covers the usual things a first stall needs, not the rules of any one fair.

Before the fair

Give yourself a week, not a night. Count and price your stock, print your price list, and pack your bags, boxes and wrapping in advance. Pack the car the day before if you can, and keep a loading list so nothing is left behind.

On the day

Arrive early so you have time to set up without rushing. Once your stall is ready, walk the hall to see the other stalls, then spend the day talking to visitors — most people buy from stalls where they have had a friendly chat.

After the fair

Count what is left and compare it with the list you brought, so you know exactly what sold. Note which products went first and which ones sat still, and follow up with anyone who left an email address while the fair is still fresh in their mind.

Questions people ask

How much stock should I bring to my first craft fair?

Bring more than you think you will sell, but not so much that you are left with a car full of stock. A good starting point is enough to fill your table nicely, plus a few spares of your bestsellers underneath the table. Keep a list of what you brought so you can count what sold at the end of the day.

Do I need to take card payments?

Most visitors expect to pay by card, so a charged card reader is worth bringing. Keep a payment app on your phone as a backup, and bring some cash for change as well, because not everyone pays the same way.

What should I do if my stall is in a dim hall?

Bring a small lamp or battery lights so your products are easy to see. Good lighting makes colours look their best and draws people towards your stall, especially in halls with little natural light.

How early should I arrive on the day?

Arrive as early as the organiser allows, so you can set up without rushing and fix any problems before the doors open. A calm setup means a tidy stall, and a tidy stall sells better.
