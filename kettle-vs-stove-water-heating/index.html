Questions people ask

How do I compare kettle and stove boiling costs?

Estimate each method’s energy in kilowatt-hours from watts and minutes, then multiply by your electricity rate.

Why might a kettle heat water faster?

A kettle is designed to heat water directly, but the actual time depends on its power, water amount, starting temperature, and appliance condition.

Does the stove input need to be electrical watts?

For this calculator, enter an equivalent watt value for the heating input. A gas burner needs a suitable energy conversion before its result can be compared.

Is the result an exact bill amount?

No. It is an estimate based on the values you enter and does not measure real appliance efficiency or heat lost to the surroundings.
