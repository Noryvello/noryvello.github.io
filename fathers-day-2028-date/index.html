Why the date is different in different countries

Father's Day did not start as one worldwide holiday. Different countries built their own traditions at different times, and the dates stayed different. The most common pattern is the third Sunday of June, used by the United States, the United Kingdom, Canada and most other countries. Because the rule is tied to a Sunday, the date moves from year to year: in 2028 it falls on 18 June.

Australia and New Zealand keep the first Sunday of September instead, which gives 3 September in 2028, while Brazil uses the second Sunday of August, which gives 13 August. Germany ties the day to Ascension Day, a Thursday, so in 2028 it falls on 25 May. Spain, Italy and Portugal are the exceptions to the moving-date pattern: they keep the fixed date of 19 March every year.

If you are sending a card or planning a call across borders, check the date for the country your father lives in, not your own. The picker above shows the right date and starts a countdown to it, so you can see at a glance how long is left.

Questions people ask

When is Father's Day 2028 in the United States?

Sunday 18 June 2028, the third Sunday of June. The United Kingdom, Canada and most other countries share the same date.

When is Father's Day 2028 in Australia?

Sunday 3 September 2028, the first Sunday of September. New Zealand follows the same rule.

When is Father's Day 2028 in Brazil?

Sunday 13 August 2028, the second Sunday of August.

When is Father's Day 2028 in Germany?

Thursday 25 May 2028. Germany ties Father's Day to Ascension Day, so it always falls on a Thursday.

Does Father's Day fall on the same date every year?

No. Most countries tie it to a Sunday or to Ascension Day, so the date moves from year to year. Only Spain, Italy and Portugal keep the fixed date of 19 March.
