How to use this guide

Pick the anniversary year from the list above and you will see both themes for that year, with a few gift idea examples for each theme. You can follow the traditional theme, the modern theme, or mix the two — the lists are starting points, not rules.

For the 14th year, the traditional theme is ivory. New ivory is restricted in many countries, so ivory-coloured gifts — a framed print in ivory and cream tones, or ivory-coloured candles — are the usual way to follow that theme.

The big milestone years are easy to remember: silver at 25 years, pearl at 30, ruby at 40, gold at 50 and diamond at 60.

Questions people ask

What is the traditional gift for a 1st anniversary?

Paper is the traditional gift for a 1st anniversary. Common ideas include a handwritten letter, a framed map of a meaningful place, or a book with a note written inside.

What is the difference between traditional and modern anniversary gifts?

The traditional list is the older set of themes, starting with paper for year 1, while the modern list is a newer set, starting with clocks for year 1. Both lists cover the same years, and many couples follow one list, mix the two, or simply use the theme as a starting point.

What is the traditional gift for a 25th anniversary?

Silver is the traditional gift for a 25th anniversary. Silver jewellery, a silver photo frame, or a silver serving dish are all common choices.

What gift should I give for a 50th anniversary?

Gold is the theme for a 50th anniversary on both the traditional and modern lists. A gold bracelet, a gold ring, or a gold-framed photo of the wedding day are all fitting choices.
