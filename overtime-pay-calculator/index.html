Overtime Pay Calculator

Short answer: type your hourly rate, your regular hours and your overtime hours — the calculator shows your regular pay, your overtime pay and your total gross pay, with every step of the working.

Gross pay only — not take-home pay. The result is before tax and deductions, and overtime rules (when overtime starts, which multiplier applies) depend on your contract and local law. This page only does the arithmetic.

The formula

  • Regular pay = hourly rate × regular hours
  • Overtime pay = hourly rate × multiplier × overtime hours
  • Total gross pay = regular pay + overtime pay

Worked examples

  1. Rate 20, 40 regular hours, 10 overtime hours at 1.5×: regular pay 800, overtime pay 300 → total 1,100.
  2. Rate 15, 40 regular hours, 5 overtime hours at 2×: regular pay 600, overtime pay 150 → total 750.
  3. Rate 25, 35 regular hours, 8 overtime hours at 1.5×: regular pay 875, overtime pay 300 → total 1,175.

The math works with any currency — type your rate in your own currency and the answers come back in the same currency.

Questions people ask

How is overtime pay calculated?

Multiply your regular hours by your hourly rate for regular pay, then multiply your overtime hours by your rate times the overtime multiplier (commonly 1.5) for overtime pay. Add the two together for total gross pay.

What does time-and-a-half mean?

Time-and-a-half means overtime hours are paid at 1.5 times the normal hourly rate. At a rate of 20 per hour, each overtime hour pays 30.

Does this calculator include tax?

No. The result is gross pay before tax and deductions, which vary by country, employer, and contract. Your take-home pay will be lower.
