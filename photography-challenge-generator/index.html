Photography Challenge Generator

Pick a theme and a length and this generator builds you a photo challenge, seven days or thirty, from a bank of thirty original prompts. One prompt a day keeps your camera busy and your eye sharp.

How to use this generator

Choose a theme and a length, then press the button. A seven-day challenge gives you seven prompts, one for each day. A thirty-day challenge gives you all thirty prompts in order, starting with your chosen theme.

The bank holds thirty prompts across six themes. Press the button again for a fresh order, or pick a new theme and build a different challenge.

Making the most of each prompt

Read the prompt, then take your time looking before you shoot. Walk around the subject, try a low angle and a high one, and take more than one photo. The first shot is rarely the best one.

Look at your photos at the end of each day and pick a favourite. Noticing what you like about your own pictures is how your eye improves, one day at a time.

Photograph with respect. Ask before you photograph people, keep off private land, and never photograph children you do not know. A good photo is never worth an awkward moment.

Sharing your challenge

A challenge is more fun with company. Invite a friend to do the same prompts and compare photos at the end of each week. The same prompt can produce very different pictures.

If you share photos online, keep locations and other people out of the frame unless you have permission. Your own photos are yours to share, but other people's privacy is not. A printed photo on a wall lasts longer than a post in a feed, so print the favourites from your challenge.

Questions people ask

How many prompts are in the bank?

Thirty original prompts, five for each of six themes: nature, people, home, street, colour and light.

How does the 7-day challenge work?

You get seven prompts, one for each day, starting with your chosen theme. Take one photo a day and see how your eye changes by day seven.

How does the 30-day challenge work?

You get all thirty prompts in order, starting with your chosen theme. One prompt a day for a month gives you a full set of photos to look back on.

Do I need a fancy camera?

No. A phone camera is enough for every prompt in the bank. The challenge is about looking, not about equipment.

Can I repeat a challenge?

Yes. Press the button again for a fresh order, or pick a new theme. The same prompt looks different in a new season or a new place.
