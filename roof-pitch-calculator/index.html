Roof Pitch Calculator — What Angle and Slope Does Your Roof Have?

Roof pitch compares vertical rise with horizontal run. Enter both measurements in the same unit to calculate pitch as rise per 12 units of run, the roof angle in degrees, and slope percentage.

Honest limit: Pitch affects material choice and drainage; steep roofs need professional work.

How Roof Pitch Is Calculated

  1. Pitch per 12: (rise ÷ run) × 12.
  2. Roof angle: arctangent(rise ÷ run), converted to degrees.
  3. Slope percentage: (rise ÷ run) × 100.

Rise and run must use the same unit. The run is the horizontal distance, not the sloped rafter length. The result describes geometry; it does not determine whether a roof is suitable for a particular covering, climate, drainage system, or structure.

Worked Example

A roof that rises 6 inches over a 12-inch horizontal run has a 6:12 pitch. Its angle is about 26.57 degrees and its slope is 50 percent. A different rise-to-run ratio produces a different pitch and angle.

Questions people ask

How do I calculate roof pitch?

Divide vertical rise by horizontal run. Multiply that ratio by 12 to express pitch as rise per 12 units of run.

What does a 6:12 roof pitch mean?

A 6:12 pitch means the roof rises 6 units vertically for every 12 matching units of horizontal run.

Is roof pitch the same as roof angle?

No. Pitch is commonly expressed as rise per 12 units of run, while roof angle is the angle in degrees. Both describe the same rise-to-run geometry in different ways.

Can roof pitch tell me which roofing material to use?

Not by itself. Material suitability depends on manufacturer requirements, underlayment, climate, drainage, roof design, and local rules. Check product specifications and use a qualified professional where needed.
