    How to use the generator

    Pick who the gift is for from the first list, then pick a budget band from the second. The generator shows 5 ideas straight away, each with one line on why it fits that person and band.

    If none of the 5 feels right, press Shuffle for 5 more ideas from the same pool — you can shuffle as many times as you like. When an idea catches your eye, choose the exact item yourself, in a style and price that suit you.

    What the budget bands mean

    Small means a modest everyday gift — the kind of thing you would give as a small thank-you or a little extra. Medium means a main gift, the sort you would give for a birthday or a holiday. Generous means a bigger treat, for a milestone or someone very close.

    The bands are relative, not prices, so they work in any currency: pick the band that matches what you want to spend, not a fixed amount.

    Tips for choosing

    Think about what they use every day — the best gifts are often the ones that fit into a routine. A shared memory can turn an ordinary idea into a personal one, so pick something that reminds you of a moment together.

    Consider things they would enjoy but would not buy for themselves, as those often make the most welcome gifts. And avoid anything they are allergic to, cannot eat, or cannot use — when in doubt, ask someone who knows them well.
