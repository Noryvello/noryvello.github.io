What is Eid al-Fitr?

Eid al-Fitr is the festival that marks the end of Ramadan, the Islamic holy month of fasting. It is a day of celebration, prayer and giving, when families come together to share food and mark the end of the fast.

How the date is set

The date of Eid al-Fitr follows the Islamic lunar calendar, so it moves earlier each year on the solar calendar. The exact day depends on the sighting of the new moon at the end of Ramadan, which is why the expected date of 9 March 2027 may move by one day.

How is the date of Eid al-Fitr decided?

Eid al-Fitr falls on the first day of Shawwal, the month after Ramadan, and the start of the month is set by the sighting of the new crescent moon. Because the sighting depends on the moon, the date is announced close to the time and can differ by a day between regions.

What happens on Eid al-Fitr?

Eid al-Fitr marks the end of Ramadan, the month of fasting. Muslims gather for a special morning prayer, share meals with family and friends, give to charity, and visit loved ones.
