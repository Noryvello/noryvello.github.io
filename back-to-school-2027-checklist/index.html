Start early, finish calmly

Most families do back-to-school shopping in August, which makes the last week before school the busiest time in the shops. Before you buy anything, look through what you already have at home: last year's bag, half-used notebooks and clothes that still fit can cover several items on the list. Spreading the shopping over a few weeks also spreads the work, so the first morning back is calm instead of rushed.

Questions people ask

What should be on a back-to-school checklist?

A back-to-school checklist should cover four areas: school supplies, clothes and shoes, daily routines, and paperwork and health forms. Start with what your child already has, then add what is missing, and check your own school's list for anything specific.

When should I start back-to-school shopping?

Most families do back-to-school shopping in August, but starting a few weeks before school begins gives you time to spread the shopping out and avoid the rush. Check what you already have at home first, so you only buy what is needed.

What school supplies do I need for 2027?

The basics are the same most years: pencils, pens, notebooks, a school bag, a pencil case and a ruler. Your school may ask for extra items, so its own list is the final word.

How can I get back into a school routine?

Move bedtime and wake-up time earlier a few days before school starts, pack the school bag the night before, and keep mornings simple with a short written routine. A regular homework time in the first week helps the routine settle.
