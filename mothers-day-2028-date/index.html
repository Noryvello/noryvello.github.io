Why the date is different in different countries

Mother's Day did not start as one worldwide holiday. Different countries built their own traditions at different times, and the dates stayed different. The most common pattern is the second Sunday of May, used by the United States, Canada, Australia, India and most other countries. Because the rule is tied to a Sunday, the date moves from year to year: in 2028 it falls on 14 May.

The United Kingdom and Ireland keep Mothering Sunday instead, which is tied to Easter rather than to May, so their date falls on 26 March in 2028. Spain and Portugal use the first Sunday of May, which gives 7 May in 2028, while France and Sweden use the last Sunday of May, which gives 28 May. Mexico and Thailand are the exceptions to the moving-date pattern: Mexico keeps the fixed date of 10 May every year, and Thailand keeps the fixed date of 12 August every year.

Questions people ask

When is Mother's Day 2028 in the United States?

Sunday 14 May 2028, the second Sunday of May. Canada, Australia, India and most other countries share the same date.

When is Mother's Day 2028 in Mexico?

Wednesday 10 May 2028. Mexico keeps the fixed date of 10 May every year, so its date never moves.

Why is Mother's Day on a different date in France?

France keeps the last Sunday of May, which falls on 28 May in 2028. Sweden follows the same rule.

Does Mother's Day fall on the same date every year?

No. Most countries tie it to a Sunday, so the date moves from year to year. Only the fixed-date countries, Mexico and Thailand, keep the same day.
