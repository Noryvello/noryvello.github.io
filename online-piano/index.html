Questions people ask

How do I play the online piano?

Click or tap any key with your mouse or finger, or use your computer keyboard: the A row plays the white keys and the W, E, T, Y, U keys play the black keys. Hold a key to hold the note, and slide across keys while holding to play a run.

Are the notes real piano notes?

Yes. Every key plays its true pitch from the standard tuning (A4 = 440 Hz, equal temperament), generated live in your browser. The piano covers two octaves, and the octave buttons shift the whole keyboard down or up.

Why is there no sound?

Browsers only allow sound after you interact with the page, so the first click or key press starts the audio. If you still hear nothing, check the device volume, the silent switch on phones, and the volume slider on the piano.

Is the online piano free?

Yes — free, with no sign-up, no download, and nothing to install. It runs in your browser on any phone or computer.
