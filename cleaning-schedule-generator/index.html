Cleaning Schedule Generator

Pick your home size, the number of people and whether you have pets, and this generator builds a weekly cleaning schedule for your home. It gives you daily tasks, weekly tasks with a room rotation for every day of the week, and monthly tasks. Print it out and stick it on the fridge.

This is a general starting plan for a typical home. Adjust it to your own home, your own rooms and your own routine, and always follow your cleaning products' own instructions.

How the schedule works

The schedule has three parts. Daily tasks are the small jobs that keep your home tidy day to day, and homes with four or more people get two extra daily jobs. Weekly tasks depend on your home size: a small home gets four, a medium home six and a large home eight. Each day of the week also has one room to focus on, so the deeper cleaning is spread out instead of piling up on one day. Monthly tasks cover the jobs that only need doing once in a while, such as wiping the inside of the fridge. If you have pets, two pet tasks are added to the weekly list.

Sticking to the plan

Do the daily tasks at the same time each day, so they become a habit rather than a chore. If a day gets busy, skip that day's room focus and do it the next day — the schedule is a guide, not a rule. Keep your cleaning things in one place so you are not hunting for them when you start.

Questions people ask

How often should I clean my house?

Most homes need a little cleaning every day and a deeper clean once a week. Daily jobs like wiping the kitchen worktops and washing the dishes keep things tidy, while weekly jobs like vacuuming and changing the bed sheets handle the deeper dirt. Monthly jobs, such as wiping the inside of the fridge, cover the things that do not need doing every week.

What should I clean every day?

The daily jobs that matter most are wiping the kitchen worktops after cooking, washing the dishes or loading the dishwasher, sweeping the kitchen floor, and taking out the rubbish when the bin is full. Making the beds each morning also makes the whole home look tidier. In a home with four or more people, one load of laundry a day helps keep it under control.

How do I make a cleaning schedule?

Start with the jobs that must happen every day, then add the weekly jobs and spread them across the week so no single day is too heavy. Give each day one room to focus on, and add a short list of monthly jobs for the things that can wait. This generator builds that schedule for you from your home size, the number of people and whether you have pets.

How do I clean with pets at home?

Pet hair and muddy paws add two jobs to the weekly list: vacuum pet hair from sofas and soft furnishings, and wash pet bedding. A weekly vacuum of the rooms your pet uses most keeps hair under control. If your pet sheds a lot, vacuum the living room twice a week instead of once.
