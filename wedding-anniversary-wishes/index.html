An anniversary marks another year of a marriage, and a few thoughtful words make the day sweeter. The wishes below are all original, written for this page, and grouped by who you are writing to.

Choose the group that fits: spouse, parents or friends. Then add a detail only you would know, such as a memory or a shared joke, and the wish becomes truly yours.

A wish lands best when it fits the couple. If you are unsure about the tone, keep it warm and simple. Avoid jokes about the marriage itself, and never mention money or gifts.

How to personalise an anniversary wish

A personal detail turns a good wish into a memorable one. Mention a memory you share, a quality you admire in the couple, or a moment from their wedding day. One specific line is worth more than a paragraph of general praise.

If you are writing to your spouse, say what the year meant to you. If you are writing to parents or friends, thank them for the example they set. Keep it honest and simple; the best wishes sound like you.

When to send your wish

Send your wish on the anniversary itself if you can. A message in the morning starts their day well, and a card posted a few days early arrives in time.

If you missed the day, send it anyway. A late wish with a short apology is far better than none, and most couples are glad to hear from you whenever it arrives.

Questions people ask

What is a good anniversary wish for a spouse?

A good wish for a spouse is personal and honest. Mention a memory, thank them for something specific, and look forward to the years ahead. A few sincere lines matter more than a long speech.

What should I write to my parents on their anniversary?

Thank them for the example they set and the home they built. A short note about what their marriage taught you is often the part they treasure most.

Is it okay to send an anniversary wish by text?

Yes. A text is fine for friends, and a card suits parents or a spouse. The words matter more than the channel, and a message sent on the day is best.

Should I mention the number of years?

Yes, if you know it. Naming the milestone, such as ten or twenty-five years, makes the wish feel personal and shows you remembered.

What should I avoid in an anniversary wish?

Avoid jokes about marriage being hard work, and do not mention gifts or money. Keep the focus on the couple and the years they have shared.
