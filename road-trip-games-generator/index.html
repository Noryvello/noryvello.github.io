How to use this generator

Choose the age of your passengers, then press the button. You will get three games that suit that age group, each with rules in three short lines. Press again for a different set.

The bank holds twenty-four games, for young children, older children, teens, adults and mixed ages. None of them need any equipment, so they work in any car with no preparation.

Keeping games friendly

Keep scores light and celebrate good guesses. If a game starts to cause arguments, switch to another one. The point of a road trip game is to pass the time happily, not to crown a winner.

Let the youngest passenger choose sometimes, and let everyone take a turn as the leader. Games feel fair when everyone gets a say.

The driver plays only with voice and ears, never with eyes off the road. Pick games everyone can join, and take a break if anyone feels travel sick.

Games for long stretches

On a long drive, rotate games every twenty minutes or so. A guessing game, then a word game, then a quiet game gives everyone a change of pace and keeps the car happy.

Save the best game for the last stretch, when patience is thinnest. A fresh game at the right moment can turn a tired car into a laughing one. A game that makes the car laugh is worth more than one that simply fills the time, so keep your best ideas in reserve for the moments that need them most.

Twenty-four original games, for young children, older children, teens, adults and mixed ages.

Can the driver join in?

Yes, for talking and guessing games. The driver should never look away from the road, so skip any game that needs reading or looking around.

What if the ages are mixed?

Pick the mixed ages option for games that work for everyone, or let the youngest passenger choose the game.

How do I keep games from turning into arguments?

Keep scores light, celebrate good guesses, and switch games when the mood dips. The point is the journey, not the win.
