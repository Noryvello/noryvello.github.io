Questions people ask

How do I measure window height for curtains?

Measure from the top of the curtain rod down to where you want your curtains to end (sill, apron, or floor), keeping header styles in mind.

What is fullness allowance in curtain making?

Fullness allowance ensures curtains look gathered and luxurious rather than stretched flat, typically multiplying the rod width by 2x to 2.5x.

How does the header style affect curtain length?

Different header styles sit differently relative to the rod. For example, grommets sit lower below the rod while tab tops hang from loops above the rod.

Should curtains pool on the floor?

Floor pooling is a stylistic choice that adds drama, but most standard installations hover about 0.5 inches above the floor for easy cleaning.
