The rules

  • United States: clocks go forward on the second Sunday in March and back on the first Sunday in November.
  • European Union: clocks go forward on the last Sunday in March and back on the last Sunday in October, under one EU-wide rule.
  • United Kingdom: British Summer Time starts and ends on the same Sundays as the EU dates.

Exceptions

Most of Arizona and all of Hawaii do not observe daylight saving time in the United States. The EU rule applies to all member states, so the whole bloc changes on the same Sundays.

Questions people ask

What is daylight saving time?

Daylight saving time (DST) is the practice of moving clocks forward by one hour during the warmer months to extend evening daylight.

Why do the US and EU have different DST dates?

The US and EU set their DST schedules based on regional legislation; the US uses the second Sunday in March and first Sunday in November, while the EU uses the last Sunday in March and last Sunday in October.

Can I rely on these dates for future years?

The dates are accurate under the current laws, but governments may change the rules, so always verify closer to the time.

How can I adjust my calendar for DST changes?

Most digital calendars update automatically, but you can manually add the start and end dates as all-day events to avoid confusion.
