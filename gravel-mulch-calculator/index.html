Questions people ask

How do I calculate gravel or mulch needed?

Multiply area by depth to find cubic volume, then convert to cubic yards or estimated weight.

Why does weight vary by material and moisture?

Wet gravel or mulch is significantly heavier than dry material due to absorbed water weight.

What is recommended depth for mulch?

A depth of 2 to 3 inches is generally recommended for garden beds to retain moisture and suppress weeds.

What is recommended depth for landscaping gravel?

Gravel pathways and beds are typically laid 2 to 3 inches deep to provide complete soil coverage.
