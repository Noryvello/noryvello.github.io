How the Lawn Seed Estimate Works

The calculator scales the seed weight on the bag to your lawn area:

  1. Find the coverage area printed on the seed packaging.
  2. Enter the seed weight associated with that coverage area.
  3. Multiply your lawn area by the package weight, then divide by the package coverage area.

Use matching area and weight units. If the label gives a range, calculate using the rate appropriate for your grass type, application method, and lawn condition.

New Lawn or Overseeding?

New lawns and overseeding often use different application rates. The calculator does not choose a rate for you; it simply scales the rate you enter from the seed label.

Questions people ask

How do I calculate how much grass seed I need?

Divide your lawn area by the coverage area listed on the seed bag, then multiply by the seed weight listed for that coverage area.

Should I use the same seed rate for overseeding and a new lawn?

No. Overseeding rates can differ from new-lawn rates. Follow the seed bag instructions for the exact use and grass mix.

Can I enter a coverage rate from a seed bag range?

Yes. Choose the specific rate on the package that matches your lawn condition and application. The calculator scales that entered rate; it does not decide which rate is appropriate.

Does this calculator account for uneven ground or seed loss?

No. It is a simple area-based estimate and does not account for uneven coverage, spreading technique, runoff, or site conditions.
