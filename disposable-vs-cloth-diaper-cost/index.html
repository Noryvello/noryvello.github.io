Disposable vs Cloth Diaper Cost

This page compares the yearly cost of disposable diapers with cloth diapers, using numbers you enter yourself. You set the changes per day, the cost of each disposable diaper, the cost of a cloth set and your laundry cost, and the calculator shows both yearly totals side by side.

How the comparison works

Disposable diapers cost a little at a time, every day, for as long as they are used. The calculator multiplies your changes per day by 365 to get the number of diapers in a year, then multiplies that by the cost of one diaper. Cloth diapers work the other way round: most of the money goes on the set at the start, and the ongoing cost is laundry. The calculator adds the one-time set cost to a full year of laundry, worked out from your loads per week and your cost per load. Both totals use only the numbers you enter, so the comparison reflects your own prices and habits rather than anyone else's. The steps under the result show every part of the working, so you can check the maths yourself.

This page compares money only. It makes no claims about health, comfort or the environment, and it cannot tell you which option suits your family. Costs depend entirely on the numbers you enter, so use your own receipts and laundry costs for a result that means something.

How to use the result

Start with the numbers you know best, then try changing one figure at a time to see what moves the result. If the two totals are close, the laundry cost is often the deciding factor: more loads a week or a higher cost per load pushes the cloth total up, while fewer loads bring it down. Remember that the cloth set cost is paid once, so from year two onwards cloth costs only the laundry, and the steps show that figure separately. If you plan to use the same cloth set for a second child, the set cost spreads even further. Use the result as a money comparison only, and weigh it against what fits your daily routine.

What this comparison does not cover

Money is only part of the choice. This page does not cover the time spent washing and drying, the space needed to store and dry cloth diapers, or the convenience of disposables when you are out. It also leaves out extras on both sides, such as wipes, creams and storage bags, because they vary too much to compare fairly. The page makes no claims about health, comfort or the environment. Those are personal matters, and this calculator only answers the money question.

Questions people ask

How many changes per day should I enter?

Enter the number that matches your baby's usual day. If you are not sure, count the changes over a few normal days and use the average.

Does the cloth total include the set cost every year?

No. The set cost is counted once, in year one. From year two onwards the cloth total is laundry only, and the steps below the result show that figure.

What should I enter for laundry cost per load?

Enter what one wash and dry costs you, including detergent if you know it. If you do not know it, check a recent bill or use your best estimate and try a higher and lower figure to see how much it matters.

Why is there no price in the calculator?

Prices vary too much by place and brand for a single figure to be useful. Entering your own prices keeps the comparison honest for your situation.

Does this page say which diaper is better?

No. It only compares yearly money cost from your own numbers. Comfort, convenience and your daily routine are personal choices this page does not judge.
