ChatGPT vs Claude vs Gemini: Which Free Plan Is Best?

Short answer: All three offer a permanent free plan, but none publishes a fixed message count. ChatGPT Free calls everyday text chats unlimited while capping uploads, images, voice and analysis separately. Claude Free gives Sonnet and Haiku on a rolling five-hour window. Gemini Free routes most chats to Flash-Lite with compute-based limits. This comparison uses only what each company publishes — checked 14 November 2026.

Honesty note: no provider on this page publishes a message count for its free plan. Articles that quote "40 messages a day" or similar are reporting community estimates, not official limits. This page reports only what OpenAI, Anthropic and Google actually publish, and marks the rest as unpublished. Last verified: 14 November 2026.

ChatGPT Free — unlimited text, capped tools

OpenAI describes ChatGPT Free as offering unlimited everyday text chats, subject to abuse guardrails. The catch is that the tools around chat are metered separately: file uploads, image generation, voice and data analysis each have their own limits, and one tool can stop while text chat keeps working.

  • Model: a Luna model — OpenAI's own pages disagree on the exact label (the pricing page lists one Luna generation, the help centre another), and both agree Free gets no Sol or Astra. The label in your account is the final check.
  • Uploads: 3 file uploads a day; 500 MB of Library storage; Projects hold up to 5 files.
  • Images: "limited" image creation with no published count; the same image model as paid plans.
  • Voice: limited time on a smaller voice model over a rolling 24 hours, with no published minute count; video and screen sharing in voice are paid-only.
  • Also included: web search, data analysis, Deep Research in limited amounts, using GPTs, and limited memory. OpenAI notes that ads may appear in the Free tier in certain countries.

Claude Free — the strongest free model, the tightest window

Anthropic's Free plan is permanent — no trial period and no card — and since June 2026 it defaults to Sonnet, the same mid-tier model Pro runs, alongside Haiku. What Free does not include: the Opus and Fable models, Claude Code, Research, scheduled tasks, or usage credits.

  • Limits: Anthropic publishes no fixed message count. Usage is a conversation budget measured in tokens, resetting on a rolling five-hour session window. How much fits depends on conversation length and complexity, attachments, features used and model. Pro is described as "at least 5x more usage per 5-hour session than Free" — the only official anchor.
  • Uploads: up to 20 files per chat.
  • Also included: web search, file creation, code execution, memory, connectors, Artifacts, and up to 5 Projects.
  • Check your allowance: Claude shows live usage under Settings, then Usage.
  • Claude does not offer AI image generation on any plan — for images, use the Gemini or ChatGPT free plans.

Gemini Free — broadest feature set, lightest model

Gemini is free with a personal Google Account, but the free plan got smaller in October 2026: since 9 October, accounts without a Google AI plan use an Auto model selector that sends most prompts to Gemini 3.5 Flash-Lite. The Flash and Pro entries left the free model picker, though Google says queries needing deeper reasoning may still route to Flash or Pro.

  • Limits: compute-based, depending on prompt complexity, models, features and chat length; they refresh every 5 hours until a weekly limit. Google publishes no prompt count and says limits may change without notice. Check your own standing under Settings, then Usage Limits.
  • Context window: 32,000 tokens on free (128K on AI Plus, 1M on AI Pro and Ultra).
  • Uploads: up to 10 files per prompt, 100 MB per file, 2 GB per video; video and audio uploads are also capped by total length.
  • Also included: image generation and editing (Nano Banana 2, 1K downloads), Gemini Live, Canvas, Gems, Deep Research, Gemini Notebook, and Google Flow with limited Nano Banana Pro access — plus 15 GB of Google Account storage.

Which free plan for what

  • Long documents and long chats: Claude Free — the largest context window and the strongest free model, if you can live with the five-hour window.
  • Most chatting without watching a meter: ChatGPT Free — everyday text chats are described as unlimited, though every tool around chat is capped.
  • Images, voice and Google apps: Gemini Free — the widest feature set, with the lightest default model.
  • Honest advice: use two of them. The free plans complement each other, and when one hits its window, the other is usually still available.

Questions people ask

Which free AI chatbot has no message limit?

None of the three publishes a fixed message count. ChatGPT Free describes everyday text chats as unlimited, subject to abuse guardrails, while uploads, image generation, voice and data analysis each have their own separate limits. Claude Free resets on a rolling five-hour window, and Gemini Free uses compute-based limits that refresh every 5 hours up to a weekly cap. Any article quoting an exact number of free messages is estimating, not quoting the provider.

Which free plan gives the strongest model?

Claude Free includes Sonnet, Anthropic's mid-tier model, alongside Haiku. ChatGPT Free runs a Luna model, the fastest and lightest of its family, with no access to Sol or Astra. Gemini Free routes most chats to Flash-Lite through an Auto selector, though deeper reasoning may still route to Flash or Pro. No free plan includes the flagship model with a manual picker.

Can I upload files on the free plans?

Yes, all three allow uploads on free, with different caps: ChatGPT Free allows 3 file uploads a day, Claude Free allows up to 20 files per chat, and Gemini Free allows up to 10 files per prompt, with a 100 MB limit per file and a 2 GB limit per video. Uploads have their own limits separate from chat on all three services.

Do the free plans include image generation?

ChatGPT and Gemini do; Claude does not offer AI image generation. ChatGPT Free includes limited image creation with no published count, and Gemini Free creates images with Nano Banana 2 under compute-based limits.
