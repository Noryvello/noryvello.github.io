Free AI Video Generators: What Is Actually Free?

Short answer: Only a few AI video tools give you free clips every day without a card. Google Flow gives 50 credits a day, Kling gives 66 credits a day, and Luma gives about 30 generations a month — all checked 14 November 2026. Runway, Hailuo and Pika offer only one-time or zero free credits, so they are trials, not free plans. Every tool below is listed with its real limit, watermark rule and commercial-use position.

Read this first: free video tiers change often — sometimes without announcement. Every figure on this page was checked against the provider's own pricing or help pages, or against dated checks of those pages, on 14 November 2026. If a number below disagrees with what the tool shows you today, the tool's own page wins. Last verified: 14 November 2026.

1. Google Flow — the strongest free allowance

Google Flow is Google's AI filmmaking studio, and it is the best free route to frontier video models. Google's Flow help page says every account receives 50 Flow credits a day, with or without a subscription. Unused credits do not roll over.

  • Veo 3.1 Lite costs 10 credits per clip (4, 6 or 8 seconds) — up to 5 clips a day.
  • Veo 3.1 Fast costs 20 credits per clip — up to 2 clips a day.
  • Veo 3.1 Quality costs 100 credits per clip, so it is out of reach on the free allowance alone.
  • Free output tops out at 720p; 1080p upscaling is for subscribers only.
  • Clips carry an invisible SynthID mark. There is no visible logo unless you are in India, South Korea or Vietnam, where Google forces a visible watermark on.
  • During peak hours, roughly 2 PM to 5 PM UTC, Google says you may not be able to generate videos without a subscription.

Note that the Gemini app itself no longer offers free video: video there now runs on Gemini Omni, which needs a Google AI plan. Use Flow, with the same Google login, for the free route.

2. Kling — daily credits, watermarked, personal use only

Kling, from Kuaishou, gives every logged-in user 66 free credits a day, which expire after 24 hours and do not accumulate. The app shows the credit cost on the Generate button before you run it.

  • At Kling 3.0's base rate of about 6 credits per second at 720p without audio, 66 credits cover roughly two 5-second test clips a day. With audio and 1080p, the rate rises to about 12 credits per second.
  • Free clips carry a watermark and, under Kling's rules, cannot be used commercially.
  • Failed generations are refunded, according to Kling's official Credits Policy.
  • The exact daily amount is listed by Kling as subject to platform announcement, so treat 66 as the current figure, not a promise.

3. Luma Dream Machine — about 30 generations a month

Luma's Dream Machine gives free accounts about 30 generations a month (checked on Luma's pricing page, November 2026). Free clips are 5 seconds at 720p, carry a watermark, and are licensed for non-commercial use only. Free users also wait in a slower queue. It is a genuine monthly allowance, but a narrow one — enough to test Luma's motion quality, not to produce a series.

4. Runway — a one-time taste, not a free plan

Runway's free plan gives 125 credits once. They never expire, but they never refresh either, and free-plan output carries a Runway watermark with access to only a limited selection of models. At Gen-4.5's rate of 12 credits per second, 125 credits cover about 10 seconds of flagship video in total across the whole life of a free account. Treat Runway Free as a trial for judging quality, not as ongoing free use.

5. Hailuo — the daily credits are gone

Hailuo (MiniMax) used to be known for free daily credits. That ended: as of November 2026, Hailuo's pricing page shows the Free plan at 0 credits a month, described as one-time free trial credits. New accounts get a single welcome pack (150 credits on the sign-up banner checked 2 November 2026) that expires three days after it is granted, one pack per user. Free downloads carry a watermark. Once the pack is spent, there is no renewing free allowance.

6. Pika — free plan, zero credits

Pika's Free plan costs $0 and, according to its own pricing page, gives 0 credits a month — packs only. You must buy a credit pack (from $25) or subscribe before you can generate anything. Pika's plan table says exports have no watermark on any plan, including Free, but the free plan includes no commercial licence, even when you buy packs. Pika is included here so you know before signing up: it is not a free video generator in any practical sense.

How to judge any "free" video tool

  1. Renews or one-time? A daily or monthly allowance (Flow, Kling, Luma) is a real free plan. A one-time credit pack (Runway, Hailuo) is a trial.
  2. Watermark? Assume free clips are watermarked unless the provider's own page says otherwise.
  3. Commercial use? Free tiers very often forbid it. If the terms do not say clearly, do not use a free clip in paid work.
  4. What does one clip cost? Always check the per-second or per-clip credit cost on the generate screen — that, not the credit total, decides how many videos you actually get.

Questions people ask

Which free AI video generator has no watermark?

Google Flow is the only tool on this page whose free downloads carry no visible watermark: with the visible-watermark toggle off, free Veo 3.1 clips download clean, except in India, South Korea and Vietnam, where Google forces a visible watermark on. Pika's plan table also says no watermark, but its free plan gives 0 credits a month, so you cannot generate anything without buying. Kling, Luma, Runway and Hailuo all watermark free output.

Can I use free AI videos commercially?

Kling and Luma both restrict free-tier output to non-commercial, personal use, and Pika's free plan includes no commercial licence even if you buy credit packs. For Google Flow, Runway and Hailuo, commercial use on the free tier is not clearly stated on their public pages, so check each service's terms before using a free clip in client work, ads or monetised content.

How many free AI videos can I make per day?

Google Flow's 50 free credits a day buy up to five Veo 3.1 Lite clips or two Fast clips. Kling's 66 daily credits cover about two short 720p clips a day. Luma's free allowance is monthly, about 30 generations a month. Runway, Hailuo and Pika have no renewing daily allowance: Runway gives 125 credits once, Hailuo gives a one-time welcome pack, and Pika gives 0 credits a month.

Is Google Flow really free?

Yes. Google's Flow help page says every account receives 50 Flow credits a day, with or without a subscription. The credits buy Veo 3.1 Lite or Fast clips at 720p, do not roll over, and may be unusable during peak hours (roughly 2 PM to 5 PM UTC). The 100-credit Quality tier costs more than a day's free allowance. Video in the Gemini app itself now needs a paid Google AI plan, so Flow is the free route.
