Women's World Cup 2027 Guide

The Women's World Cup 2027 is hosted by Brazil, from 24 June to 25 July 2027. 32 teams take part, playing in 8 host cities, and the final is at the Maracanã in Rio de Janeiro.

Host cities

The 8 host cities are:

  • Rio de Janeiro
  • São Paulo
  • Belo Horizonte
  • Fortaleza
  • Recife
  • Salvador
  • Porto Alegre
  • Brasília

Tournament dates

The tournament runs from 24 June to 25 July 2027. The final is played on 25 July 2027 at the Maracanã in Rio de Janeiro.

How the format works

32 teams take part in the tournament. It begins with a group stage, and the teams that go through then play knockout matches, ending with the final.

This guide covers only the host cities, the dates and the format. It does not list teams or match times, and details can change before the tournament — check the official announcement for the latest information.
