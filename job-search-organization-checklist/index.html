Job Search Organization Checklist

Log each application with its follow-up date, keep a steady weekly routine, and tick off your documents as you prepare them. This page keeps your whole job search organized in one place, so no opportunity slips through.

A job search falls apart when it lives only in your head. Applications blur together, follow-up dates slip past, and the same document gets rewritten from scratch. A simple log and a steady weekly routine fix most of that.

This page has three parts: an applications log with follow-up dates, a weekly routine checklist, and a documents checklist. Use them together and your search stays organized from the first application to the last interview.

This page does not list jobs and does not give career advice. It is a simple organizer for the search you are already running — always check a vacancy with the employer before you apply.

A weekly routine that works

Set aside regular days for searching, applying and following up, and keep the rest of the week for preparation and rest. A steady routine beats a frantic burst followed by a week of nothing.

Follow up on last week's applications once, politely, and then move on. If an employer does not reply, that is an answer in itself. Review your week each Friday, note what worked, and plan the next week from there.

Keep your documents ready

Keep your CV up to date and a cover letter template ready to tailor for each role. Ask your references before you name them, and keep a copy of your certificates and ID ready for the day an employer asks.

Prepare a short set of interview notes and questions in advance. Knowing what you want to ask an employer is as important as knowing what they might ask you.

Questions people ask

How often should I follow up on an application?

Once, politely, about a week after you apply. If there is no reply, move on to the next application rather than chasing the same employer again and again.

How many applications should I log?

Log every application you send, so you always know where you stand. A short note about the role and the follow-up date is enough.

What documents do I need for a job search?

A CV, a cover letter template, references who have agreed to be named, and copies of your certificates and ID. Keep them all in one folder so they are ready when asked for.

Should I tailor every application?

Yes, at least a little. Read the role description and match your cover letter to what the employer is asking for. A tailored application stands out more than a generic one.
