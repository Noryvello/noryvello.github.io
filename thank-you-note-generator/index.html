What makes a thank you note good

A good thank you note names the gift or the kindness, so the reader knows exactly what you are grateful for. One personal detail — something you noticed, enjoyed or will remember — is what turns a polite note into a warm one. Keep it short, write it in your own voice, and send it soon: a few honest sentences, sent quickly, are worth more than a long letter that never gets written.

Questions people ask

How do you write a thank you note?

Start with a warm greeting, say thank you clearly and name what you are thanking them for, add one personal detail that shows you noticed, and close with a kind line. Keep it short and sincere — a few honest sentences are better than a long formal letter.

How long should a thank you note be?

A thank you note only needs a few sentences: two or three are plenty for most occasions. If the kindness was a big one, four or five sentences give you room to say why it mattered. The best notes are short enough to read in one breath and personal enough to be remembered.

When should I send a thank you note?

Send it as soon as you can, ideally within a few days of the gift, the help or the visit. A late note is still worth sending — it is always better to thank someone late than never. For a job interview, send it the same day or the next morning.

What should I say in a thank you note after a job interview?

Thank the interviewer for their time, mention something specific from the conversation that interested you, and say you would welcome the chance to join the team. Keep it short and professional, and send it the same day or the next morning.
