The times on this page are a starting plan. Your own morning may take longer or shorter, so adjust the minutes to fit your real morning. The builder only adds up the steps you tick — it does not know your commute, your family, or your appointments.

How to build a morning routine

Start with your wake-up time, then tick the steps you actually do each morning. Change the minutes to match your real morning — the total and finish time update as you type. When the plan looks right, print it and follow it for a week before changing anything.

Keep it realistic

A routine only works if it fits the morning you actually have. Start with a few steps and add more once the habit sticks, and leave a little spare time for the mornings that do not go to plan. If the finish time is later than you need, shorten a step or drop one, rather than rushing.

Questions people ask

How do I build a morning routine?

Pick your wake-up time, then tick the steps you want to do each morning and set the minutes for each one. The builder adds up the time, shows a start time for every step, and works out when you will finish.

How long should a morning routine take?

There is no single right length — it depends on how many steps you choose and how long each one takes you. This page adds up whatever you pick, so you can see the total and adjust it until it fits the morning you actually have.

What should I include in a morning routine?

Include the steps that matter to you: getting up, washing, eating breakfast, and getting ready to leave are the usual ones. Add exercise, planning or reading if they fit, and leave out anything that does not belong in your morning.

Can I print my morning routine?

Yes. Press the Print button below your routine and the ordered list, with start times, total time and finish time, will print from your browser.
