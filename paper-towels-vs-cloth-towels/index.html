Paper Towels or Cloth: Which Creates Less Waste Each Year?

Short answer: enter your use, sheet weight, purchase costs and laundry estimates to compare yearly spending and paper waste.

What the waste number means

Paper waste is estimated from the number of tasks, sheets per task and the weight per sheet. It is a simple material-use estimate. It does not include packaging, manufacturing, transport, recycling outcomes or the water and energy used to make either product.

How to make the comparison useful

  • Use your real average number of sheets rather than the number printed on a roll.
  • Estimate the cloth towel lifespan based on how often you wash and replace it.
  • Allocate only the relevant share of laundry cost to these towels.
  • Use one currency consistently and keep your washing estimate realistic.
Important limit: Cloth towels need washing, which costs water and energy too.

Questions people ask

How do I compare paper and cloth towels fairly?

Use the same number of cleaning or drying tasks, estimate paper sheets per task, and enter the purchase and washing costs you actually face.

How is paper towel waste estimated?

The estimate multiplies sheets used per task by tasks per year and the weight per sheet you enter. It is an input-based mass estimate, not a full environmental footprint.

What costs should I include for cloth towels?

Enter the purchase cost and expected number of years of use, plus an estimated washing cost per load and the share of loads attributable to the towels.

Are cloth towels always better for the environment?

Not automatically. Reuse can reduce disposable waste, but washing uses water and energy. Material, laundry habits and towel lifespan also matter.
