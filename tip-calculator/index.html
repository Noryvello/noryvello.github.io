Tip Calculator — Bill Split and Tip Calculator

Easily figure out tips and split restaurant or service bills among any group of people. Enter your bill total, select or type a tip percentage, and set the number of diners to instantly view the tip value, total amount due, and share per person with step-by-step working.

Honest limit: This tool performs straight mathematical division for bill splitting. It does not account for individual itemized orders, differing beverage consumption, or automatic service charges that may already be included on your bill.

How the Tip Formula Works

Calculating shared service tips involves three clean arithmetic steps:

  1. Tip value: Bill Amount × (Tip Percentage ÷ 100)
  2. Total final bill: Bill Amount + Tip Value
  3. Per person share: Total Final Bill ÷ Number of People

Because the formulas are currency-agnostic, you can use the tool anywhere in the world with any numerical currency system.

3 Worked Examples

  • Example 1 — Standard Dinner Split: A bill of 85 with a 15% tip split among 4 people. Tip value is 85 × 0.15 = 12.75. Total bill is 85 + 12.75 = 97.75. Each person pays 97.75 ÷ 4 = 24.44.
  • Example 2 — Solo Coffee Shop Order: A bill of 12 with a 20% tip for 1 person. Tip value is 12 × 0.20 = 2.40. Total bill is 12 + 2.40 = 14.40.
  • Example 3 — Large Group Celebration: A bill of 300 with an 18% tip split among 6 people. Tip value is 300 × 0.18 = 54.00. Total bill is 354.00. Each person pays 354 ÷ 6 = 59.00.

Questions people ask

How is the tip amount calculated?

The tip amount is calculated by multiplying the base bill amount by the tip percentage divided by 100.

Does the calculator include taxes in the tip calculation?

This calculator operates purely on the bill amount you enter. You can choose to enter either the pre-tax subtotal or the final total bill depending on your personal tipping preference.

How is the split per person determined?

The total bill including the tip is divided evenly by the number of people sharing the payment.

Are currency symbols required?

No, this calculator is completely currency-agnostic. You can use any numerical currency unit or value without symbols.
