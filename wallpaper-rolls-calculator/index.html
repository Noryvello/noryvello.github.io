Questions people ask

How do I calculate wallpaper rolls needed?

Measure wall perimeter and height, subtract large openings like doors and windows, and divide by usable roll area including pattern match waste.

Why do patterned wallpapers need extra rolls?

Patterned wallpaper requires vertical matching between strips, meaning top or bottom trimmings are often discarded to align the pattern correctly.

Should I subtract doors and windows from wall area?

Yes, standard practice is to subtract large doors and windows, though keeping a small buffer for mistakes is recommended.

What is standard wallpaper roll size?

A standard European or US Euro-roll is typically around 21 inches wide by 33 feet long, yielding approximately 56 square feet gross.
