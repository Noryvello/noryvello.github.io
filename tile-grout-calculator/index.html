Questions people ask

How is tile grout quantity calculated?

Grout quantity depends on tile length, width, thickness, joint width, and total area covered.

Why do deep joints and uneven tiles use more grout?

Thicker tiles create deeper grout channels, and uneven or textured tiles require more material to fill gaps correctly.

What joint width should I use for wall tiles?

Wall tiles often use narrow joints like 1/16 inch or 1/8 inch, while floor tiles often use 1/8 inch to 1/4 inch.

Should I buy extra grout?

Yes, always buy 10% extra to account for mixing loss and future touch-ups.
