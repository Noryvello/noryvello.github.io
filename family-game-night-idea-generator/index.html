How to use the generator

Pick the group that matches who is playing, then pick how much time you have. The generator shows a game idea with a three-line how-to and a list of what you need. If the idea does not suit your family, press Another idea for a different one from the same group.

Tips for a good game night

Start with a short game so everyone warms up, and let a different person choose the next game each time. Keep the rules simple, and let younger players team up with an older player when a game is tricky. The best game nights end while everyone still wants one more round.

Questions people ask

What are good family game night ideas?

Good family game night ideas are simple games everyone can join in, like acting games, quizzes, treasure hunts and drawing games. The best ones need little or nothing from around the house, take as long as you have, and let every age play a part. This generator picks one for your group and your time.

What games can kids and adults play together?

Games with simple rules and no reading or counting advantage work best across ages, like hide and seek, acting games, drawing games and building challenges. Pick games where younger players can help and older players cannot win just by being older. The mixed ages group in this generator is built for exactly that.

What do I need for family game night?

Nothing more than what is already in your home. Every idea in this generator uses household items like paper, pens, socks and cushions, or nothing at all, so you can start tonight with what you already have.

How long should family game night last?

As long as your family enjoys it. Fifteen minutes is enough for a quick game before bed, thirty minutes suits a school night, and an hour gives you time for a bigger challenge or several short games. Stop while everyone is still having fun, and save the next idea for another night.
