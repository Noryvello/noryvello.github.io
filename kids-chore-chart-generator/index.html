Questions people ask

What chores can a 4 year old do?

A 4 year old can do simple tidy-ups, such as putting toys away, putting dirty clothes in the laundry basket, putting shoes by the door and helping to clear the table. Keep tasks short, show them how first, and expect a bit of mess along the way.

How many chores should a child have?

Start with two or three chores and add more only when the first ones feel easy. A short list the child can finish is better than a long list that feels impossible, and older children can usually handle a few more than younger ones.

How do I make a chore chart?

Pick your child's age band on this page, tick the chores you want, and print the chart. Stick it where your child can see it, and let them colour in a star each day they finish a chore.

What rewards work for kids' chores?

Simple rewards work best, such as an extra story at bedtime, choosing the weekend activity, a special outing or picking what is for dinner. Praise the effort as well as the finished chore, and keep the reward the same each week so your child knows what to expect.
