Why the dates differ

There is no single world rule for Father's Day. Most countries follow the third Sunday of June, but each place sets its own date: Australia and New Zealand use the first Sunday of September, Brazil the second Sunday of August, Germany Ascension Day, and Spain, Italy and Portugal a fixed date of 19 March. Questions people ask

When is Father's Day 2027 in the US?

Father's Day 2027 in the United States is on Sunday 20 June 2027, the third Sunday of June.

When is Father's Day 2027 in the UK?

Father's Day 2027 in the United Kingdom is on Sunday 20 June 2027, the same date as in the United States — the third Sunday of June.

Why is Father's Day in Australia in September?

Australia and New Zealand mark Father's Day on the first Sunday of September, which falls on 5 September 2027. The date is a local tradition, not the June date used in the United States and the United Kingdom.

Is Father's Day the same date everywhere?

No. Most countries use the third Sunday of June, but Australia and New Zealand use the first Sunday of September, Brazil the second Sunday of August, Germany Ascension Day, and Spain, Italy and Portugal a fixed date of 19 March.
