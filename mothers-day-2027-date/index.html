Mother's Day 2027 Date

Why the date is different in different countries

There is no single worldwide date for Mother's Day. Most countries follow the second Sunday of May, which in 2027 is 9 May. The United Kingdom and Ireland follow Mothering Sunday instead, which is tied to Easter rather than to May, so their date falls on 7 March in 2027. Mexico and Thailand use fixed dates — 10 May and 12 August — while France and Sweden use the last Sunday of May and Spain and Portugal use the first Sunday of May.

Questions people ask

When is Mother's Day 2027 in the US?

Sunday 9 May 2027. The United States celebrates Mother's Day on the second Sunday of May.

Why is Mother's Day in the UK on a different date?

The United Kingdom and Ireland follow Mothering Sunday, which falls three weeks before Easter Sunday, so in 2027 it is Sunday 7 March 2027. Most other countries use the second Sunday of May instead.

Is Mother's Day the same date everywhere?

No. In 2027 it is 7 March in the United Kingdom and Ireland, 2 May in Spain and Portugal, 9 May in the United States, Canada, Australia, India and most countries, 10 May in Mexico, 30 May in France and Sweden, and 12 August in Thailand.

When is Mother's Day 2027 in Australia?

Sunday 9 May 2027, the second Sunday of May, the same date as in the United States.
