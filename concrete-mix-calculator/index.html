Questions people ask

How is concrete volume calculated for a slab?

Multiply slab length, width, and depth to get total cubic volume.

Why should I add 5-10% extra for concrete?

Extra concrete accounts for sub-grade unevenness, spillages, and formwork deflection.

What is a standard concrete mix ratio?

A standard nominal mix is 1:2:3 (1 part cement, 2 parts sand, 3 parts gravel by volume).

When should I hire a professional for concrete work?

Structural work, load-bearing foundations, and large pours require professional engineering and placement.
