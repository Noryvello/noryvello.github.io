## Demand marks — method and rubric (free signals only, gathered 2026-11-13)
Marks are working judgments from free public signals (web search: current coverage freshness, published search-volume figures, "people also ask"/how-to demand patterns, seasonal timing) — **not paid keyword data**. Order in each list: HIGH → EVERGREEN → MEDIUM → UNVERIFIED.
- **HIGH** = direct evidence of strong current/peak demand (named volume figure, heavy fresh 2026 coverage, or seasonal peak within ~2 weeks).
- **EVERGREEN** = topic belongs to a class with demonstrated steady year-round demand (DIY material math, kitchen conversions/how-tos, energy-cost comparisons, name generators, device help, career how-tos, date/time lookups, CSS visual builders).
- **MEDIUM** = plausible steady demand via adjacent-class evidence.
- **UNVERIFIED** = no evidence found at topic or class level; no guessing.

## Categories deliberately avoided (and why)
1. **Prompt-pack forbidden:** crypto/speculation; medical/insurance/mental-health advice; politics; anything needing expert review; horoscopes/fortune/love calculators; personality quizzes; invented facts about real people.
2. **All ToolBari duplicates:** the held list (tip, reading-time, fuel-cost, password-strength, countdown timer, age, exam/study planners) plus every catalog slug — checked exactly and semantically.
3. **Health/diagnostic:** BMI-like tools, pregnancy, blood pressure, fasting/period trackers, nutrition-label reading — medical-adjacent.
4. **Finance advice:** investing mistakes, stock picking, tax advice — expert-review risk. (General money education and user-input comparisons were kept.)
5. **Food-safety specifics:** fridge/freezer storage times — expert-guidance risk. (USDA turkey times kept only as published guidance with the thermometer rule.)
6. **Legal documents:** NDA/terms/privacy-policy generators, loan agreements — expert-review risk.
7. **Fraud-adjacent:** Luhn checker, credit-card number generators.
8. **Backend-dependent:** polls, surveys, uptime monitors, WHOIS/SSL checkers, PDF merge/split, GIF makers — need servers or heavy libraries.
9. **Thin/low-value:** excuse generator, birthstone lookup, numerology, paper-vs-digital planner.
10. **Security-sensitive:** 2FA tools, password vaults, clipboard history.
11. **Unreliable-device:** compass, altimeter (sensor-dependent).
12. **One-per-intent pruning:** inverter-size (near battery-backup), tabata (near interval-timer), storage-needs (near data-usage), aquarium volume (near generic volume), guitar tuner (prompt-path build risk), gold/silver rate tools (need live feeds).
