Breathing Exercise Guide

This guide shows three simple breathing exercises, each with a visual pacer to follow. The circle grows as you breathe in and shrinks as you breathe out, and the words tell you each step. Sit somewhere comfortable and stop whenever you like.

1. Box breathing — 4-4-4-4

Breathe in for 4 seconds, hold for 4 seconds, breathe out for 4 seconds, then hold for 4 seconds. The four equal steps make a square, or box, shape.

Ready

Press Start when you are ready.

2. 4-7-8 breathing

Breathe in for 4 seconds, hold for 7 seconds, then breathe out for 8 seconds. The out breath is the longest step of the three.

Ready

Press Start when you are ready.

3. Slow belly breathing

Breathe in slowly for 4 seconds and let your belly rise, then breathe out slowly for 6 seconds. This is a gentle starting pace — breathe gently and stop if you feel uncomfortable.

Ready

Press Start when you are ready.

This page is general information only, not medical advice. Stop if you feel dizzy or uncomfortable. If you have a breathing condition, ask a doctor first before trying any breathing exercise.

How to use the pacers

Sit or lie somewhere comfortable and press Start on the exercise you want to try. Follow the circle: it grows while you breathe in and shrinks while you breathe out, and the words tell you when to hold. Each pacer runs on its own, so you can try one at a time. Press Stop at any point and the pacer resets.

A note on breathing exercises

The timings on this page are patterns to follow, not targets to hit. If a pace feels too fast or too slow for you, stop and rest — the pacer is only a guide. Never force a breath, and stop straight away if you feel dizzy or unwell.

Questions people ask

What is box breathing?

Box breathing is a pattern of four equal steps: breathe in for 4 seconds, hold for 4 seconds, breathe out for 4 seconds, then hold for 4 seconds. The four equal sides give it the name box breathing. Follow the pacer on this page to try the pattern.

How does the 4-7-8 breathing technique work?

The 4-7-8 pattern has three steps: breathe in for 4 seconds, hold for 7 seconds, then breathe out for 8 seconds. The numbers are the count of seconds for each step. Follow the pacer on this page to try the pattern.

What is belly breathing?

Belly breathing means breathing slowly and letting your belly rise as you breathe in, instead of taking short breaths into your chest. On this page the starting pace is 4 seconds in and 6 seconds out. Breathe gently and stop if you feel uncomfortable.

How often should I practise breathing exercises?

There is no fixed rule. A few minutes a day is a common starting point for people who practise breathing exercises. Fit it around your own day and stop whenever you like.
