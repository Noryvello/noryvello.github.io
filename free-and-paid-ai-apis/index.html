Yes, genuinely free AI APIs exist: 17 providers passed a strict test — $0, no credit card to start, a quota that renews daily or monthly, and a real API key with an HTTP endpoint. The strongest free model is a 550-billion-parameter model on OpenRouter's free roster. If you outgrow the free quotas, paid prices start from $0.075 per 1M input tokens. Every figure on this page was checked on 14 November 2026.

Free tiers change fast. Free model rosters rotate wholesale, limits get cut, and whole programs retire with little warning. Treat every free quota below as true on the check date only, and re-check the provider's own page before you build on it. Last verified: 14 November 2026.

Most daily volume: the throughput ranking

Power is not the same as volume. For the most requests per day with no card: ModelScope (about 2,000/day) leads, then Kilo (200/hour per IP, shared rotating pool), then Groq (3,000 requests/day across its 3 free models, 600K tokens/day total), then Cloudflare (roughly 80–490 replies per day depending on model size), then Gemini Flash-Lite (500/day). OpenRouter's free roster is the most powerful but the smallest at 50 requests/day. The practical setup is a fallback chain: Groq first, Cloudflare second, OpenRouter :free for frontier overflow, Gemini Flash-Lite next, and the OVH/Kilo anonymous lanes as a last resort.

Read this before you build on a free tier

  • Rosters rotate. OpenRouter's July 2026 free roster was entirely gone by October. Never hard-code one free model ID; check what is free today and keep a fallback.
  • Some free tiers ban production use. NVIDIA NIM is trial/prototyping only, Cohere trial keys are evaluation and non-commercial only, and FreeInference is framed for research and education.
  • Some free tiers use your prompts. Gemini's free tier, some OpenRouter free endpoints, Kilo, NVIDIA NIM and FreeInference may log prompts or use them for training or research. Never send private or confidential data through a free tier.
  • Identity and region gates are real. ModelScope needs ID verification, SiliconFlow needs Chinese real-name verification, SenseNova needs a +86 phone number, and Gonka needs SMS verification.
  • Relays carry trust risk. Kilo, Gonka, LLM7 and Pollinations are third-party gateways or single-operator services with no SLA. Prefer first-party providers (Groq, Cloudflare, Google, NVIDIA, Cohere, ModelScope, Zhipu) for anything user-facing.

Not actually free: what to skip

  • SambaNova Cloud — its official plans page (checked 13 November 2026) says the free plan requires adding a payment method and purchasing credits before the first requests run. It fails the no-card test.
  • GitHub Models — retired. Closed to new customers on 16 June 2026 and fully retired on 30 July 2026; the endpoint returns HTTP 410.
  • Mistral AI free tier — unresolved and conflicting as of 14 November 2026: one tracker says free-plan API access ended around 5 November 2026, while Mistral docs retrieved 4 November 2026 still describe a no-card free mode. Do not rely on it without a live console check. Mistral's paid prices are in the table below.
  • iFlytek Spark — reported free tiers rest on user reports, not official public pages, and could not be verified. Not ranked here.
  • One-time credits are not a free tier. DeepSeek, Together, Fireworks, Cerebras, Chutes, Hugging Face (free credit ended 7 October 2026), DashScope, Moonshot, MiniMax, Baidu, Tencent, ByteDance, xAI, Perplexity and Venice offer no standing renewing no-card free tier — only expiring trial credits, or none at all.

How to choose: free, paid, or both

  • Learning, prototypes and side projects: start free. Groq's free tier alone covers 1,000 requests a day per model, and a fallback chain across Groq, Cloudflare, OpenRouter :free and Gemini covers far more.
  • Real users and production apps: pay for at least one provider. Paid tiers bring higher limits, support, and terms that allow production use — and on the paid tier, providers do not use your content to improve their products.
  • Cheapest paid route: Groq's gpt-oss-20b ($0.075 input / $0.30 output per 1M) and DeepSeek's flash model off-peak ($0.15 / $0.60) are the lowest verified rates for general chat work. One million input tokens is roughly 750,000 English words — about 1,500 pages of text — so even a busy hobby app often costs cents per day.
  • Watch the dates. Google's published prices already show the next step: Gemini 4 Argon and Carbon double on 1 December 2026, and the Flash models double on 1 January 2027. Cost your product at the post-increase rate.

Questions people ask

What is the most powerful free AI API with no credit card?

OpenRouter's :free roster, led by nvidia/nemotron-3-ultra-550b-a55b:free, a 550-billion-parameter model with a 1M-token context window — but the no-card limit is only 50 requests per day, and the free roster rotates, so use it together with Groq and Cloudflare rather than alone.

Is there a genuinely free AI API with a renewing quota?

Yes. 17 providers passed a strict test on 14 November 2026: $0, no credit card to start, a quota that renews daily or monthly, and a real API key plus HTTP endpoint. Groq gives 1,000 requests per day per model, ModelScope gives 2,000 requests per day with ID verification, and Kilo and OVHcloud work with no sign-up at all.

Which paid AI API is cheapest per million tokens?

On prices verified from official pages on 14 November 2026, the cheapest paid input rate is Groq's gpt-oss-20b at $0.075 per 1M tokens, and the cheapest paid output rate is Mistral's Ministral 3 3B at $0.10 per 1M tokens. DeepSeek's flash model costs $0.15 input and $0.60 output per 1M tokens off-peak.

Why do free AI API lists go out of date so fast?

Free model rosters rotate constantly: OpenRouter's July 2026 free roster was entirely gone by October, GitHub Models retired on 30 July 2026, and Hugging Face ended its free credit on 7 October 2026. Never build on a single free model ID — use a multi-provider fallback chain and re-check the official pages before you rely on any free tier.
