Wedding Budget Planner

Short answer: type your total budget — the planner splits it across every category, tracks what you have spent in each, and shows what is left. Every percentage is editable, and the split starts from a common template you can reshape freely.

The starting split is a template, not a rule. Venue 35%, catering 25%, photography 10%, outfit and beauty 10%, decor 8%, music 5%, invitations 3%, miscellaneous 4% — a common starting shape. Change any percentage to match your own priorities; the planner only does the arithmetic.

How to use it

  1. Type your total budget.
  2. Edit any percentage — the budgeted amount for that category updates at once.
  3. As you spend, type the spent amount per category — the remaining column and the totals keep score.
  4. Add categories you need (transport, rings, cake, anything) and remove ones you do not.
  5. Save the plan on your device to come back to it later, on the same browser.

Questions people ask

How should a wedding budget be split?

There is no single right split — it depends on the venue, guest count, and what matters most to you. This planner starts from a common template (venue 35%, catering 25%, photography 10%, outfit and beauty 10%, decor 8%, music 5%, invitations 3%, miscellaneous 4%) and every percentage is editable, so the split becomes yours.

What if my percentages add up to more than 100%?

The planner warns you and shows the running total. Amounts are still worked out from the percentages you typed, so bring the total back to 100% for the plan to match your budget exactly.

Is my budget data uploaded anywhere?

No. Saving is optional and uses only your own browser's local storage on the device you are using. Nothing is sent to any server, and clearing the saved plan removes it from that device.

Is the Wedding Budget Planner free?

Yes — free, with no sign-up, and it runs in your browser on any phone or computer.
