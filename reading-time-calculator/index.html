Reading-Time Calculator — Book Finish Time & Words Per Minute

To calculate how long it takes to read any text or book, divide the total word count by your reading speed in words per minute (WPM), then divide by your daily reading minutes to find how many days you need to finish. Enter your numbers below to see exact minutes, hours, and days with step-by-step working.

Honest limit: Reading speed changes dramatically depending on text difficulty, unfamiliar vocabulary, diagrams, note-taking, and fatigue. This calculator assumes a steady constant pace and does not include extra time for re-reading complex passages or researching references.

How the Reading-Time Formula Works

Estimating completion time uses three direct arithmetic steps:

  1. Total reading minutes: Total Words ÷ Words Per Minute
  2. Total reading hours: Total Reading Minutes ÷ 60
  3. Days to finish: Total Reading Minutes ÷ Daily Reading Minutes

If your book only lists page numbers instead of words, multiply the page count by roughly 275 words per page to get a practical starting word count.

3 Worked Examples

  • Example 1 — Standard Novel: A 75,000-word novel read at 250 WPM takes 75,000 ÷ 250 = 300 minutes (5.00 hours). Reading 30 minutes per day finishes the book in 300 ÷ 30 = 10.0 days.
  • Example 2 — Long Online Guide or Essay: A 4,500-word article read at 225 WPM takes 4,500 ÷ 225 = 20 minutes (0.33 hours). At 20 minutes a day, you finish in 1.0 day.
  • Example 3 — Dense Textbook Section: A 18,000-word study module read carefully at 150 WPM takes 18,000 ÷ 150 = 120 minutes (2.00 hours). Studying 45 minutes per day finishes it in 120 ÷ 45 = 2.67 days (3 study sessions).

Questions people ask

How do I calculate reading time from word count?

Divide the total word count by your reading speed in words per minute to get total reading minutes, then divide by 60 to convert into hours.

What reading speed should I enter if I do not know mine?

Use 200 to 250 words per minute for steady leisure reading, 150 words per minute for dense textbooks or technical manuals, and 300 words per minute or more for light skimming.

How can I estimate word count if I only know the page count?

Multiply the number of pages by 250 to 300 words per page for a standard paperback novel, or by 400 to 500 words per page for dense academic or nonfiction books.

How does daily reading time affect my book finish estimate?

Dividing total reading minutes by the minutes you plan to read each day gives the exact number of days needed to finish the book.
