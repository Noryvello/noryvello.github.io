Questions people ask

Why should I inspect a used car during daylight?

Natural daylight makes it much easier to spot mismatched paint panels, subtle dents, rust spots, fluid leaks under the car, and uneven tire tread wear that artificial lighting can hide.

What should I look for on the dashboard warning lights before driving?

When you turn the ignition to the ON position before starting the engine, warning lights such as Check Engine, ABS, and Airbag should briefly illuminate to prove the bulbs work, then turn off once the engine starts.

How can I check if a car has uneven alignment during a test drive?

On a straight, flat, safe road, lightly loosen your grip on the steering wheel for a moment to see if the vehicle drifts consistently to one side or if the steering wheel sits off-center.

Does checking off this list replace a mechanic's inspection?

No. A visual walk-around and test drive help you screen out obvious problems early, while a mechanic's lift inspection checks underbody components, suspension joints, and computer fault codes.
