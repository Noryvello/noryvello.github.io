Privacy Policy

Noryvello is a free tools hub that collects no personal data from its visitors. There are no accounts, no sign-ups, no cookies set by the site itself, and no ads running on the site at the time of writing. This page explains, in plain words, what happens — and what does not happen — when you use Noryvello.

Last updated: November 2026

This policy explains what Noryvello does — and does not do — with information about the people who visit the site. The short version is simple: the site collects no personal data, sets no cookies of its own, and runs no ads. The rest of this page says the same thing in more detail, so that nothing is left unclear.

This policy describes the site as it is now. If anything changes, this policy will be updated here, on this page.

What Noryvello collects

Noryvello itself collects no personal data. There are no accounts and no sign-ups: every tool and every guide on the site is open to anyone, with nothing to create and nothing to log in to. You are never asked for your name, your address, or any other personal detail. The tools run in your browser, so what you type into a calculator or a generator stays on your own device and is never sent to Noryvello. No data is sold or shared with anyone, because there is no data to sell or share.

Cookies

Noryvello sets no cookies of its own. The site does not use cookies to track visitors, to remember visitors between visits, or to build profiles of any kind. Your browser may still hold cookies set by other websites you visit, but nothing on this site asks your browser to store anything for Noryvello. If that ever changes, this page will say so.

Analytics and advertising

No analytics is installed on this site. Noryvello does not count its visitors with a tracking service, does not record how individual visitors move through the site, and does not keep visit statistics. No ads are running on the site at the time of writing, and there is no sponsored content on any page.

Hosting and technical logs

Noryvello's pages are hosted on GitHub Pages. Like most web hosts, GitHub's servers may keep standard technical logs when a page is requested, such as the IP address of the request, the date, and the page requested. Noryvello does not see or control those logs: they belong to the hosting service, not to this site. This policy covers what Noryvello itself does, and it cannot make promises about how the host handles its own logs.

Contacting Noryvello

If you message us on Telegram, your message is used only to reply to you. It is not passed to anyone else, and Noryvello does not keep a list of the people who have written in. What you write is read only so that the problem you report, the mistake you point out, or the suggestion you make can be answered. The Contact page explains what to include in your message.

Changes to this policy

If anything on this page stops being true, the policy will be updated here, on this page, and the last-updated line at the top will change with it. The version you are reading now is always the current one. There is no separate notice: this page is the notice.

Questions people ask

No. Noryvello itself collects no personal data. There are no accounts and no sign-ups, and what you type into a tool stays in your own browser.

Do you use cookies?

No. Noryvello sets no cookies of its own and does not use cookies to track or remember visitors.

Are there ads on Noryvello?

No. No ads are running on the site at the time of writing, and there is no sponsored content on any page.

Who hosts this site?

Noryvello's pages are hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub's servers may keep standard technical logs, such as IP address, date, and page requested, which Noryvello does not see or control.
