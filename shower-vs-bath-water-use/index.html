Any calculated cost or quantity is an estimate based only on the information entered. It is not a measurement or a guarantee.

Questions people ask

How is shower water use calculated?

Multiply the shower flow rate in litres per minute by the shower duration in minutes.

How do I estimate bath water use?

Enter the approximate litres of water in the bath; the tool compares that volume with your shower estimate.

Can a long shower use more water than a bath?

Yes. Shower use rises with both flow rate and duration, so a long shower can exceed the bath volume you enter.

Does this include water used before the shower gets warm?

No. The calculation includes only the flow rate and duration you enter, not water run while waiting for temperature.
