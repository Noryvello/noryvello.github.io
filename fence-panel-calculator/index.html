Questions people ask

How do I calculate fence panels needed?

Divide total fence line length by the standard panel width (typically 8 feet).

Why do slopes require stepped panels?

Stepped panels maintain level fence lines across sloped terrain by stepping each panel down individually.

Should I check property boundaries before building a fence?

Yes, always check local zoning laws, easements, and property boundaries before installing permanent fences.

How many bags of concrete per fence post?

Standard fence posts typically require 2 to 3 bags (50 lb each) of fast-setting concrete per hole.
