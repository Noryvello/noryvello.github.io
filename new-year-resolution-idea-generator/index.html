New Year Resolution Idea Generator

Short answer: select a life area and how often you are willing to act, then press Generate for a resolution idea matched to that commitment. Small, specific resolutions survive longer than grand ones.

Honest limit: this tool provides ideas based on predefined lists; it does not guarantee personal suitability or success.

Making a resolution stick

  • Make it specific. “Walk 30 minutes every morning” beats “exercise more”.
  • Start small. A once-a-week habit you keep beats a daily habit you drop in February.
  • Track it weekly. A tick on a calendar is enough to see the streak grow.
  • Forgive slips. Missing one day is not failure; missing the next day too is how resolutions die.

The five life areas

Health and fitness, career and education, relationships, personal growth, and finance — pick the area you most want to change, then let the generator suggest a starting point.

Questions people ask

How are the resolution ideas created?

They are curated from common goal-setting categories and are meant as starting points. Edit any idea to fit your own situation.

Can I customize the suggestions?

Yes. Treat each generated idea as a draft — change the activity, the frequency or the target to match your life.

Is the commitment picker based on scientific research?

No. It is a simple frequency estimate — once a week, three times a week, or daily — to help you choose a realistic pace.

Will this replace a professional coach?

No. It is a simple idea tool and not a substitute for professional guidance on health, money or career decisions.
