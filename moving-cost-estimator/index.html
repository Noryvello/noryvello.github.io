What goes into a moving cost?

A move is made up of five parts, and this estimator lists each one separately. Boxes and packing covers the boxes, tape and wrapping you need to pack everything up. A truck or van is the vehicle that carries it all, whether you hire one or borrow one. Movers are any paid help you hire to load, drive or unload. Cleaning is the cost of leaving the old place clean, and deposits and fees are the one-off payments tied to the new place.

Questions people ask

What costs are involved in moving house?

The main costs are boxes and packing materials, a truck or van, movers if you hire help, cleaning the old place, and deposits and fees for the new place. This page lists each one so you can fill in your own figures and see the total.

Is it cheaper to move yourself or hire movers?

Doing it yourself usually costs less in money but takes more of your time and effort, while hiring movers costs more but takes the heavy work off your hands. The best way to compare is to fill in both versions on this page — once with the movers line at zero, and once with a quote from a mover.

How do I estimate moving costs?

List every cost involved — boxes and packing, a truck or van, movers, cleaning, and deposits and fees — then put your own figure next to each one and add them up. This page does the adding for you, and the example starting values can be replaced with your own figures.

What hidden costs should I budget for when moving?

Costs people often forget include cleaning the old place, deposits and fees for the new place, and extra packing materials when boxes run out. Listing each cost separately, as this page does, helps you spot the ones you might have missed.
