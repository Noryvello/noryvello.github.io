Why Black Friday falls on 24 November 2028

Black Friday is the day after Thanksgiving, so its date follows the Thanksgiving rule. Thanksgiving in the United States is always the fourth Thursday of November, and in 2028 that Thursday is 23 November. The Friday after it is 24 November, and that is Black Friday 2028. Because Thanksgiving moves a little each year, Black Friday moves with it: it always falls on the Friday after the fourth Thursday of November.

The day marks the traditional start of the holiday shopping season. Shops open early and many people take the day off, so the Friday after Thanksgiving has become one of the busiest shopping days of the year. What is Cyber Monday?

Cyber Monday is the Monday after the Thanksgiving weekend, and in 2028 it falls on 27 November. Where Black Friday began as a day for shopping in person, Cyber Monday grew up as its online twin: a day when the focus is on shopping from home. The two days sit three days apart, with the weekend between them, and together they open the run-up to the festive season.
