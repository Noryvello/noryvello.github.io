Cinema vs Streaming Cost

This page compares what a year of cinema trips costs against a year of streaming subscriptions, using your own numbers. Enter how often you go, what tickets and snacks cost, and what your subscriptions cost, and the calculator shows both yearly totals side by side.

How the comparison works

A cinema trip costs a lump sum each time you go, while streaming costs a steady amount every month whether you watch or not. The calculator works out your cinema year first: visits per month times 12 gives your visits for the year, and ticket plus snacks times the number of people gives the cost of one visit. Multiplying those gives the yearly cinema total. For streaming, the calculator multiplies your number of subscriptions by the average monthly cost and by 12 months. Both totals use only the numbers you enter, so the comparison fits your own habits and prices. The steps under the result show the working for both sides.

This page compares money only. It cannot say which way of watching is better, and it does not count travel to the cinema or the cost of the screen you watch at home. Enter only the share of each subscription that you actually pay.

How to use the result

If the two totals are close, small changes tip the balance. One extra cinema visit a month, or one more subscription, can be enough to change which side costs more, so try the numbers for a busy month and a quiet month to see the range. If you share subscriptions, remember to count only your own share, and if you sometimes skip a month, use the average over the year rather than your busiest month. The result is a money comparison only: a night out at the cinema and a quiet evening at home are different experiences, and the calculator does not try to put a value on that difference.

What the numbers do not show

Some costs sit outside both totals. Travel to the cinema, parking and the time spent getting there are not counted, and neither is the television, sound system or internet connection you use for streaming. New releases reach the cinema first, while streaming gives you a library to browse at any hour, and neither of those facts appears in a money total. Use the result to understand where your money goes, then decide for yourself what the experience is worth.

Questions people ask

Should I count snacks in the cinema cost?

Yes, if you usually buy them. Snacks are part of the real cost of a cinema trip, so the calculator includes a box for them. Enter 0 if you never buy snacks.

What if I share a streaming subscription?

Enter only the share you actually pay. If a friend pays and you pay nothing, enter 0 for that subscription or leave it out of the count.

Do cinema visits include the whole family?

Yes. Enter the number of people who usually go, and the calculator multiplies the ticket and snack cost by that number.

Why are there no prices in the calculator?

Ticket, snack and subscription prices vary too much by place for one figure to be useful. Your own numbers give a comparison that fits your situation.

Does this page say cinema is better than streaming?

No. It only compares yearly money cost. The experience of a big screen and the convenience of watching at home are personal choices.
