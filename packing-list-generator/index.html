Packing List Generator

Tell the generator your trip type, how many days you are away and what the weather will be like, and it builds a packing list with quantities for each item. Tick items off as you pack them, and print the list to check again before you leave.

How the packing list generator works

Pick your trip type, enter how many days you are away and choose the weather you expect. The generator builds a list of items with a quantity for each one, sized to the length of your trip. Tick each item off as it goes into your bag, and the counter at the bottom shows how many items are packed and how many are left.

When the list is complete, press the print button to print it out. A printed list is handy for the final check before you leave the house, and for checking that everything comes home again at the end of the trip.

Packing tips that save space

Roll soft clothes instead of folding them. Rolled clothes take up less room and come out with fewer creases. Wear your bulkiest shoes and your heaviest jacket for the journey, so they do not take up bag space. Keep travel documents, tickets and chargers together in one easy-to-reach pocket, and pack a spare bag for dirty laundry or souvenirs.

Pack the things you will need first on top, such as sleepwear for the first night. If you are travelling with others, split shared items between bags so one lost bag does not take everything with it. Finally, leave a little space. Almost every trip brings something home that was not in the bag on the way out.

How quantities are worked out

Quantities follow simple rules that you can check at a glance. Tops are counted at one per day, and socks and underwear at one per day plus one spare pair. Sleepwear is counted at one set for a short trip and an extra set for longer stays. Smart outfits for a business trip are counted at about half the number of days, plus one spare, since outfits can be worn more than once.

Weather items are added on top of the trip list. A rainy forecast adds a raincoat or umbrella and extra socks, a cool forecast adds a warm jumper, and a warm forecast adds sun protection and an extra water bottle. If your trip mixes two types, run the generator once for each type and combine the two lists.

This list covers the usual items for each trip type. It does not replace your own judgement, so check the weather forecast again before you leave, and always carry any medication or documents you personally need.

Questions people ask

How many days can the list cover?

From 1 to 30 days. Enter the number of days you will be away and the quantities adjust to match the length of your trip.

Why does the list add an extra pair of socks?

One spare pair covers a wet day, a lost sock or a longer stay. The same idea applies to underwear, which is also counted at one per day plus one spare.

Can I print the list?

Yes. After the list is built, press the print button below it. A printed list is handy for the final check before you leave.

What if my trip mixes two types?

Run the generator once for each trip type and combine the two lists. For example, a beach trip with a city stop can use both lists together.

Does the list include toiletries?

It includes a toothbrush and toothpaste and a toiletries bag. Add your own personal items, such as medication, to the list before you pack.
