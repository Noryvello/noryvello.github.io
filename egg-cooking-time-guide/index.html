Questions people ask

When should I start timing boiled eggs?

Start timing when the egg is placed into already boiling water, using the same method consistently.

Do larger eggs need more time?

Usually, larger eggs need more time than smaller eggs to reach a similar yolk texture.

Why cool eggs after boiling?

Cold or ice water helps stop carryover cooking and can make peeling easier for some eggs.

Are these times exact for every kitchen?

No. They are guide ranges; starting temperature, altitude, pan conditions, and egg size affect the result.
