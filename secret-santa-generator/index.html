Secret Santa Generator

Short answer: type one name per line, press Draw names — every person gets exactly one gift to buy, and no one ever draws themselves. Reveal pairs one at a time so the secret holds.

Keeping it secret: results are generated in your browser and never sent anywhere. Reveal one pair at a time with only that giver watching, then hide the screen before the next reveal. The "Copy results" button is for the organiser only.

How the draw stays fair

  1. Names are shuffled at random.
  2. The draw is checked: if any name matched itself, the whole shuffle is thrown away and drawn again.
  3. Every name appears exactly once as a giver and exactly once as a receiver — nobody is left out and nobody is drawn twice.
  4. With 3 or more names, a fair draw always exists. With only 2 names, secrecy is impossible, so the page asks for at least 3.

Questions people ask

Can someone draw their own name?

No. The draw is a derangement: every name is shuffled until nobody is matched with themselves, and every name is drawn exactly once. The check runs on every draw, and the draw is repeated if any name lands on itself.

How many people can join a Secret Santa draw?

At least 3. With only 2 people, each person would know exactly who drew them, so the secrecy fails. There is no upper limit on this page — paste as many names as your group has.

Are the names or results sent anywhere?

No. Names stay in your browser and are never uploaded. Reveal pairs one at a time on one shared screen, or copy the full list only if you are the organiser sharing results privately.

Is the Secret Santa Generator free?

Yes — free, with no sign-up, and it runs in your browser on any phone or computer.
