Basic safety tips

  • Tell someone your route and expected return time.
  • Check the weather forecast and trail conditions.
  • Stay on marked paths and respect signage.
  • Carry a fully charged phone and know where you have service.
  • Move at a pace comfortable for the whole group.

Questions people ask

How much water should I bring?

Bring at least 2 litres per person for a half-day hike, and more in hot conditions or for longer routes.

Do I need a map if I have a smartphone?

Yes. Bring a physical map or download offline maps — phones can lose battery or signal on the trail.

What should I do if I get lost?

Stop, stay calm, stay visible, and use a whistle to signal. Retrace your steps only if you are sure of the way; otherwise stay put and call for help.

Is a first-aid kit necessary?

Yes. Basic supplies can treat minor injuries and prevent complications. Know how to use everything in the kit before you go.
