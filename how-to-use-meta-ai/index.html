How to Use Meta AI on WhatsApp, Instagram, and Facebook

By the Noryvello team · Published 18 November 2026 · Updated 18 November 2026

Meta AI is an assistant built into WhatsApp, Instagram, and Facebook, so you can ask questions and generate images without leaving the app you already use. It works in one-to-one chats and in group chats, and it can answer questions, explain things, and create images from a description. This guide shows you where to find it in each app and how to use it well.

Meta AI is different from most chatbots because it lives inside the messaging apps you already open every day. On WhatsApp, Instagram, and Facebook, you can ask it a question, get an explanation, or generate an image without switching to a separate app. For quick questions in the middle of a conversation, that convenience is the whole point.

This guide explains where to find Meta AI in each app, how to ask good questions, how image generation works in chat, and what to keep in mind in group chats, where everyone can see what you ask. Availability varies by region, so some features may look different on your phone.

Meta AI's availability, features, and placement vary by region and app version, and they change over time. In group chats, remember that everyone in the group can see what you ask and what Meta AI answers. Never share passwords, payment details, or other sensitive information in a chat with Meta AI.

Where Meta AI appears in each app

On WhatsApp, Meta AI usually appears as a chat contact or through a search bar entry, and you can also mention it inside a group chat. On Instagram, it appears in direct messages and in search. On Facebook, it appears in Messenger and in the search bar. The exact entry point varies by app version and region.

If you cannot find it, update the app and check again, because rollouts happen gradually. You do not need to install anything extra: Meta AI is part of the app itself, and it appears once the feature reaches your account.

Asking questions in WhatsApp

Open a chat with Meta AI and ask your question in plain words, just as you would message a friend. It can explain a concept, translate a phrase, suggest ideas, or answer a factual question. Keep your questions specific: explain photosynthesis in simple words for a 12-year-old will do better than what is photosynthesis?

You can also ask follow-ups in the same chat. If the answer is too long or too technical, say so, and Meta AI will adjust. The conversation works best when you treat the first answer as a draft you can shape with a quick reply.

Using Meta AI on Instagram and Facebook

On Instagram, Meta AI in direct messages works the same way as on WhatsApp: open the chat, ask a question, and follow up. It is handy for quick questions while you are already scrolling, such as asking for caption ideas or an explanation of something you just saw.

On Facebook, Meta AI in Messenger can help with planning and ideas inside your conversations, and in search it can answer questions about topics and events. The experience is similar across the three apps, so once you are comfortable in one, the others will feel familiar.

Generating images in chat

Meta AI can generate images from a written description, right inside a chat. Describe the subject, the setting, and the style, and it will produce an image you can share with friends or use for a post. A detailed description works better than a single word: a watercolor painting of a lighthouse at sunset will do better than lighthouse.

As with any image generator, the results have limits. Text inside images often comes out garbled, faces and hands can look odd, and fine details may not match your description. Generate again with a tweaked description if the first image is not right.

Meta AI in group chats

In a group chat, you can mention Meta AI to ask a question the whole group can see, which is useful for settling a debate, planning an outing, or brainstorming together. Everyone in the group sees the question and the answer, so it becomes part of the conversation.

That visibility is also the thing to remember. Do not ask Meta AI anything private in a group chat, and do not share personal details in a question the group can read. Save personal questions for a one-to-one chat with Meta AI.

Everyday uses: planning, learning, and ideas

Meta AI is good for quick, everyday help: planning a meal from the ingredients you have, suggesting a weekend activity, explaining a news story, or helping you draft a message. Because it is inside your messaging apps, it is always at hand when a question comes up in conversation.

It is less suited to long, deep work. For a research project or a long document, a dedicated chatbot on the web will give you more room and better tools. Use Meta AI for the quick question, and switch to a bigger tool when the task grows.

Privacy and safety notes

Remember that your chats with Meta AI are stored by the service, and that anything you ask in a group chat is visible to the group. Avoid sharing personal details, and be careful with other people's information too: do not paste a friend's private message into a Meta AI chat.

If a child uses your phone, check whether they can open Meta AI and what they might ask it. As with any AI tool, the answers can be wrong, so check important facts before you act on them, and teach younger users to do the same.

Getting better answers from Meta AI

The same prompt habits that help any chatbot help Meta AI: be specific, give context, and say what format you want. Give me three easy dinner ideas using chicken and rice, each under 30 minutes will do better than what should I cook?, because it tells Meta AI your ingredients, your time limit, and how many ideas you want.

Follow up in the same chat when the first answer is close but not quite right. Ask it to make the answer shorter, simpler, or more detailed, or to try a different angle. Short follow-ups are easy to type on a phone, and they turn a generic answer into one that fits your situation.

Questions people ask

Do I need to install anything to use Meta AI?

No. Meta AI is built into WhatsApp, Instagram, and Facebook. Make sure the app is updated, and the feature will appear once it reaches your account and region.

Is Meta AI available in my country?

Availability varies by region and rolls out gradually. If you cannot find Meta AI in your apps, it may not have reached your region yet. Update the apps and check again later.

Can other people see what I ask Meta AI?

In a one-to-one chat, only you see the conversation. In a group chat, everyone in the group can see what you ask and what Meta AI answers, so keep personal questions out of group chats.

Can Meta AI see my private messages?

Meta AI only sees the messages you send to it, or the messages in a chat where it has been mentioned. It does not read your other private conversations.

How do I delete a Meta AI conversation?

Delete the chat the same way you delete any other chat in the app. Deleting removes it from your view, though the service may keep records briefly for safety reasons.
