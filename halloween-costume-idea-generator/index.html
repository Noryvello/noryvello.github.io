Halloween Costume Idea Generator

Short answer: choose a theme and how much effort you want to spend, then press the button for a random costume idea that matches. Press again for another idea.

Your costume idea will appear here.
Ideas only. The generator suggests costume concepts; it does not check availability, safety or venue rules. Check flame resistance and visibility for children's costumes, and venue rules for masks and props.

How to pick a costume

  • Comfort first. You will wear it for hours — check you can sit, walk and see clearly.
  • Mind the weather. Outdoor parties need layers; indoor parties need breathable fabric.
  • Group themes win. Matching costumes photograph better and are easier to plan together.

Last-minute ideas

Low-effort options need little more than what is already at home: a sheet ghost, a black cat with eyeliner whiskers, or a tourist with a camera and sun hat. The generator's Low filter lists them all.

Questions people ask

How does the generator work?

It picks a random costume idea that matches the theme and effort level you selected. Choose Any in either box to include every option.

Can I get a different idea?

Yes. Press the button again to draw another idea with the same filters, or change the theme or effort level for a fresh set.

Is it free to use?

Yes, the tool is completely free and runs in your browser.

Where can I share my costume?

You can post pictures on social media or community groups and tag friends. Check event and venue rules before wearing masks or props in public places.
