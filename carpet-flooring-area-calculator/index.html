Carpet and Flooring Area Calculator

Enter your room measurements and this calculator works out the floor area, the amount of carpet to buy including a waste allowance, and the number of carpet tiles if you choose tiles. It covers both broadloom carpet sold by the roll and square carpet tiles.

This calculator gives an estimate for a plain rectangular room. Rooms with bay windows, alcoves or angled walls need extra measuring, and patterned carpet may need more waste allowance than a plain one. Always check the size printed on the tile box or carpet roll before ordering.

How to use this calculator

Measure the longest length and the widest width of the room in metres, measuring wall to wall. Enter both numbers, then choose a waste allowance. Ten percent is a common starting point for a plain rectangular room, and you may want more for an awkward shape or a patterned carpet. Pick broadloom if your carpet comes off a roll, or carpet tiles if you are laying squares. For broadloom, pick the roll width your supplier sells. For tiles, pick the tile size. Press Calculate to see the area, the quantity including waste, and the tile count.

How the calculation works

The floor area is the room length multiplied by the room width. The waste allowance is then added: the area is multiplied by one plus the waste percentage divided by 100. For broadloom, the length to buy is the quantity with waste divided by the roll width. For tiles, the tile count is the quantity with waste divided by the area of one tile, rounded up to a whole tile, because part tiles cannot be bought. Every step is shown next to the result so you can check the maths.

How to use the result

Use the quantity with waste as your shopping figure, not the bare floor area. For broadloom, check the roll width with your supplier before ordering, because a wider roll can mean fewer joins across the room. For tiles, keep a few spare tiles for later repairs, since dye batches can differ. Measure each room separately and add the results together rather than guessing a total for the whole house, and measure into doorways and alcoves so no patch is missed.

Questions people ask

How do I measure a room for carpet?

Measure the longest length and the widest width, wall to wall, in metres. Measure each room separately, and measure into doorways and alcoves as well, because those areas still need covering.

How much waste allowance should I add?

Ten percent is a common starting point for a plain rectangular room. Add more for an awkward shape, a patterned carpet that needs matching, or a room with many corners.

What is the difference between broadloom and carpet tiles?

Broadloom carpet is sold off a roll in a set width and cut to length. Carpet tiles are individual squares laid one by one, which makes it easier to replace a damaged tile later.

Why is the tile count rounded up?

Tiles are sold whole, so the calculator rounds up to the next full tile. The waste allowance already covers cutting, and rounding up covers the last part tile.

Does this calculator work for other flooring?

The area and waste maths works for any flooring sold by area, such as vinyl or laminate. The tile count is for carpet tiles, so check your own tile size before using that figure for other tiles.
