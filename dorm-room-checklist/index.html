Before you buy

  • Check school rules on mini-fridges, microwaves, kettles and candles — many dorms restrict them.
  • Coordinate with roommates on shared big items such as a fridge, rug or TV so nothing is duplicated.
  • Measure first. Most dorms use twin XL mattresses — confirm the exact size in your housing guide.

Questions people ask

What size bedding do I need?

Most dorms use twin XL mattresses; check your school's housing guide for exact dimensions.

Can I bring a mini-fridge?

Many dorms allow a small fridge under 10 cubic feet, but verify the policy first.

Do I need my own cleaning supplies?

Yes, basic wipes, a small broom, and trash bags are useful for keeping the room tidy.

How many power strips can I use?

One or two strips are usually fine; avoid overloading circuits by spreading devices.
