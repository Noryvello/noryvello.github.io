What makes Eid al-Adha greetings different

Eid al-Adha arrives in the season of Hajj, the annual pilgrimage, and its mood is different from Eid al-Fitr. Where Eid al-Fitr celebrates the end of a month of fasting, Eid al-Adha turns on sacrifice and devotion — remembering the willingness of Prophet Ibrahim (AS) — and on sharing what we have with those who have less.

That is why Eid al-Adha messages often mention Qurbani, the sacrifice offered after the Eid prayer, and the sharing of meat and food with family, neighbours, and people in need. A good Eid al-Adha greeting can wish someone a joyful day, but it can also honour the deeper themes of the festival: giving, gratitude, and trust.

How to choose the right message

Start with who the message is for. Family messages can be warm and personal; messages for friends can be lighter; messages for colleagues should stay formal and respectful. A short text suits a quick message or status, while a longer message suits a card.

Then add one true detail. A name, a shared memory, or a hope for the year ahead turns a copied message into a personal one. Keep it sincere — a short, honest wish means more than a long, fancy one.

Send your greeting on the day of Eid if you can, or in the days around it. Eid al-Adha is expected on Sunday 16 May 2027, subject to moon sighting, so check your local mosque's announcement for the confirmed date.

Questions people ask

When is Eid al-Adha 2027?

Eid al-Adha is expected on Sunday 16 May 2027, subject to the sighting of the moon. The date can shift by a day, so follow your local mosque or authority's announcement for the confirmed date.

What does Eid al-Adha celebrate?

Eid al-Adha, the Festival of Sacrifice, marks the willingness of Prophet Ibrahim (AS) to sacrifice his son in obedience to Allah, and it falls during the Hajj pilgrimage season. Muslims mark it with the Eid prayer, the Qurbani sacrifice, and sharing meat and food with family, neighbours, and those in need.

What should I write in an Eid al-Adha card?

Keep it warm and simple: start with Eid Mubarak, add a wish for the person's family, and mention what the festival means — sacrifice, gratitude, or sharing. A short personal line, such as a memory or a hope for the year ahead, makes a card message feel sincere.

What is the difference between Eid al-Fitr and Eid al-Adha greetings?

Eid al-Fitr greetings celebrate the end of Ramadan and a month of fasting, while Eid al-Adha greetings fall in the Hajj season and often mention sacrifice, Qurbani, and sharing with those in need. Both begin with Eid Mubarak, but Eid al-Adha messages usually carry the themes of devotion and generosity.

How do I reply to Eid Mubarak?

A simple reply is Eid Mubarak to you too, or Taqabbal Allahu minna wa minkum, which means May Allah accept it from us and from you. Either reply works, whether the greeting came by card, message, or in person.
